### 2.5 Could the 1931 meeting cover microscope **and** frequency work?

**Short answer:** **Microscope + lab bacteriology: yes (A-tier).** **Frequency beam on pathogens in the lab, possibly shown to insiders: plausible (B/C).** **Human terminal cancer cure celebrated at that dinner: not supported by contemporaneous press and unlikely on timeline.**

| Question | Assessment | Why |
|----------|------------|-----|
| **Was frequency technology real to Rife before 1931?** | **Yes — per Rife** | His [1953 history](https://rifevideos.com/history_of_the_development_of_a_successful_treatment_for_cancer_and_other_virus_bacteria_and_fungi.html) and [rife.de PDF](https://www.rife.de/files/history_rife_cancer_treatment.pdf): initial frequency instrument **~1920**; ongoing MOR work with Universal microscope. |

| **Did contemporaneous press report frequency at the dinner?** | **No** | [LA Times 22 Nov](https://www.rifevideos.com/sciences_latest_strides_in_war_on_ills_disclosed.html), [NY Times 22 Nov](https://www.nytimes.com/1931/11/22/archives/bacilli-revealed-by-new-microscope-dr-rifes-apparatus-magnifying.html), [Dec 27 *Wonderwork*](https://www.rifevideos.com/the_wonderwork_of_1931.html) — **microscopy**, filterable typhoid, **immunization** hope; **no** MOR machine, **no** cancer remission. |
| **Did Rife publicly disavow “vibration cures” in 1931?** | **Yes — ironic** | [Popular Science, Jun 1931](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Rife) — warned against “**medical fakers**” using electrical “**vibrations**”; said his work did **not** uphold such claims. Undercuts meme that **public 1931** story was already “frequency cure.” |
| **Could doctors have seen frequency demos privately?** | **Plausible** | Closed medical dinner; press **barred** from full story; [rexresearch](https://rexresearch.com/rife/rifebeam.htm) claims Johnson ran **typhoid-in-medium** tests with the frequency device — **unsupported independently**, but **consistent** with **lab** demo, not **human cancer clinic**. |
| **Was “The End to All Diseases” the real 1931 billing?** | **Unverified in A-tier press** | Phrase appears in **Lynes / supporter** literature; **not** in indexed LA/NY Times headlines. [royal-rife.com](http://www.royal-rife.com/) attributes it to **San Diego Evening Tribune** — **TODO:** verify issue date/page. Treat as **retrospective P.R.**, not proven contemporaneous title. |
| **Human cancer cure at or right after 1931 dinner?** | **Unlikely** | Johnson to Kendall, **2 Apr 1934**: hopes by **15 Jun 1934** “**we will have human cases** to work on” ([rifevideos 1934 clinic](https://www.rifevideos.com/the_1934_cancer_and_tuberculosis_clinic.html)) — implies **terminal human cancer trial not yet running** in spring **1934**, let alone **1931**. |
| **Dec 1931 “weapon” language** | **Metaphorical in press** | *Wonderwork* article: knowledge and “**weapon**” to win “**war against disease breeding germs**” in the “**next decade**” — **visionary immunology/microscopy**, not a filed claim that **frequency cured cancer** at the banquet. |

**Synthesis for the meme:** The blackbox post is **half right in structure, wrong in dating**:

1. **1931** — credible that insiders saw **microscope + possibly lab frequency effects** on **cultures** (Rife’s later account + closed-door setting).  
2. **“Frequency cure” as headline achievement** — maps better to **1934 clinic lore** and **1953 Rife narrative**, not **A-tier 1931** press.  
3. **Redaction read that fits evidence:** **public layer** emphasized **respectable microscopy**; **frequency/cancer** may have been **discussed privately** or **shown in lab**, then **under-reported** or **later disowned** — without requiring that **LA Times articles were falsified**.

**What would falsify the dual-agenda hypothesis:** A **1931** primary (minutes, letter, news clip) describing **MOR treatment of human cancer patients** at or immediately after the Johnson dinner. **What would support it:** Dated **1931–1932** lab notes tying **frequency instrument** to **BX/cancer** observations **plus** attendee correspondence mentioning beam demos — **not yet indexed** in this file (§10 Q9).
