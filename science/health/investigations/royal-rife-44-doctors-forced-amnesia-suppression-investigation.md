## 1. Central claim

**One sentence:** Elite physicians and institutions privately documented Rife’s **microscope and frequency/clinical work** in the early 1930s while the **public record stayed on microscopy and filterable bacteria**, and that **insider window** later closed through **guild pressure, corporate litigation, and career distance** — a **clear suppressed-medicine pattern** even when **terminal cancer cure efficacy** remains **unproven** and **viral meme specifics** are **wrong**.

**What this investigation is about:** **Two-tier release** under **Flexner-era capture** — not adjudicating whether modern Rife devices work.

**What it is not:** Proof that **88% of terminal cancer patients** were cured, that **44 doctors perjured** themselves, or that a **single lab fire** erased all evidence.
