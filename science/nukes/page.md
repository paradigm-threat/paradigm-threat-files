## Author correction (2026-07-19)

### What I used to believe

I used to treat the nuclear story as a **full hoax**: no working weapons at all — Hiroshima/Nagasaki as napalm/firebomb theater, test footage as models and TNT, mushroom clouds as conventional blasts, the whole Cold War stack as money and fear alone.

That older voice still shows up in archival mirrors and captions under this folder (especially [`A.md`](/science/nukes/A.md) — Winsor lecture notes plus a [heiwaco](http://www.members.tripod.com/heiwaco/bomb.htm) total-hoax scrape). Those pages stay as **source archive**, not current thesis.

### What I believe now

**Half-hoax:**

1. **Hoax half — flash / vaporization myth.** The public picture of a **disintegrating flash for miles** — people and cities erased in a science-fiction fireball — is **not** the trustworthy core of the history. Overstated yield theater, staged or enhanced footage, and firebomb-scale destruction mislabeled as “pure fission apocalypse” belong here.
2. **Real half — depleted uranium.** **DU is very real and very cancer-causing.** Battlefield toxicity, latency, and radiological/chemical harm are not “fear propaganda.” Filing DU under science fiction while arguing about Hollywood bombs is the operational blind spot ([Installment III — DU blind spot](/governance/war/islamabad-peace-deal-the-war-nobody-cares-about-2026.md)).

Fission in reactors can be real without endorsing every kiloton claim and every flash photograph. Conflating “enriched scare story” with “DU does nothing” was the mistake.

### Why I changed my mind

The hinge was not a press release or a fatwa. I changed my mind when I saw how **NSA-aligned / antibody entities** treated the **DU proxy threat** as **more frightening than any other danger in this world** — more than the sci-fi bomb the networks sell, more than the talking points that say Iran’s enrichment is “peaceful” because religion forbids a mushroom cloud.

It is hard to imagine those actors are simply **wrong** on DU because the **press** and a **fatwa** say the Hollywood bomb is the only nuclear question that matters. If antibody factions prioritize **depleted** material and munitions latency over the flash myth, the half-hoax is the more parsimonious read: **exaggerate the spectacle; hide or underplay the weapon that actually haunts battlefields and proxies.**
