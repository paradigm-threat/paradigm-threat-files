## 1. Operational thesis — scattered gates, confiscation, heaven link

### 1.1 What the stack asserts

Ring-class **portal hardware** — **stargates** in modern SF vocabulary — was **not invented at CERN**. It was **rediscovered** on **multiple continents** during the **Age of Exploration’s late industrial phase** (roughly **1890s–1940s**): arctic surveys, colonial digs, cave clearance, jungle expeditions, and **military-adjacent** terrain mapping. Each find shares **geometry**: **thick circular ring**, **carved or machined inner rim**, **centroid aperture**, **human scale** for operator crews.

By the **1950s**, surviving specimens and **documentation** were **consolidated under state custody** — museums, war departments, polar institutes, and classified engineering bureaus — so the public cosmology could harden around **void**, **relativity-as-final**, and **Mars = dead** ([Dead Planet Protocol](/timeline/evt-mars-surface-dismantling-dead-planet-protocol); [Mariner 1962 seal](/science/cern_portal/out-of-this-door-might-come-something.md)). **CERN** (and predecessor **particle-ring** culture) is the **industrial successor**: same **harness grammar**, better **cover story**, **buried** deployment ([gateway dossier §3](/science/cern_portal/investigations/cern-mars-quantum-gateway-investigation.md)).

### 1.2 Ancient tablet grammar (author read)

Pre-redaction **cosmology** names the function without LHC vocabulary:

- **Heaven** = **Mars interior** and **paired worlds** along **discharge-scarred** routes — not metaphor-only sky ([Mars hub](/cosmos/mars/page.md); [first Jerusalem / pole stack](/history/chronology/jerusalem/troy_1st_jerusalem.md)).  
- **Earth** = **near endpoint** — **terminal** culture after **Golden Age** portal openness closed ([Stonehenge read](/history/chronology/stonehenge-quantum-portal-golden-age-and-dark-ages.md)).  
- **Gate / door / ladder** = **coupled æther distortion** — **instant** routing to **fixed nodes**, described in myth as **angels ascending**, **kings entering the underworld**, **chariots of the gods**.  
- **Faster than light** in tablets = **non-luminal** **tunnel mode** — not **EM wave** speed **c** ([Maxwell dossier §1](/science/maxwell_aether/02-did-maxwell-prove-the-aether.md); [Part A — æther ring](/science/cern_portal/out-of-this-door-might-come-something.md)).

**Tier:** **Author synthesis** across **myth + chronology** lanes — not a single **verified** cuneiform line cataloged here yet. **Open:** pull **verbatim** gate/door clauses from **Enuma Elish**, **Pyramid Texts**, **Book of Gates**, **Etana** descent, **Sumerian** **KUR** / **gate** vocabulary into §4.1 table.

### 1.3 Relationship to this image set

The **27 JPEGs** **encode the discovery myth visually** — **fedoras**, **pith helmets**, **military winter gear**, **colonial dig crews** — even when the **pixels** are **modern composites** (§3). They function as **distributed PP**: teach the eye **what a buried gate looks like** before **Geneva’s ring** enters mainstream awareness.
