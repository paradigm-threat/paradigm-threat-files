## Æther, Maxwell, and the vocabulary split

James Clerk Maxwell worked **charges and fields** in a world that assumed a **physical medium** with **permittivity and permeability**—properties of **something**, not of a literal void. Oliver Heaviside and the vector reformulation did not erase the vacuum’s **structure**; they streamlined the **equations** everyone still uses in devices. Relativity and Michelson–Morley retired the **simple** “luminiferous” story as **the whole answer**, not the **question** whether space carries **constants**, **symmetries**, and **energy** that participate in every interaction.

The repo’s canon note [LLM æther vocabulary / Maxwell debate](/docs/LLM_AETHER_VOCABULARY_MAXWELL_DEBATE.md) keeps me honest: **æther** (or **aether** in slugs) names the **substrate** voice here; **quantum / ZPE / dark sector** language is allowed as **translation**, not as a silent replacement that pretends the debate ended in 1905 with nothing left to say about **vacuum**.

The “suppression since the 1920s” story is therefore **institutional and rhetorical** as much as it is a cloak-and-dagger file. Textbooks kept the **math** and dropped the **nineteenth-century word**; funding stacks favor **gene-first** medicine; embryology rarely spells out **QED** couplings in a prenatal visit. That is **pattern**, not a claim that every physicist conspired. For the **chronology** argument that the vocabulary shift tracks **power**, not only **measurement**, see the timeline lane on [suppression of Maxwell / aether rhetoric](https://paradigmthreat.net/timeline/) (in-repo mirror under `paradigm-threat-timeline`).

## Superposition, Wi-Fi, and “planes”

Mainstream physics is clear that waves in a **linear** medium **add**: the **principle of superposition** is how interference patterns exist at all ([OpenStax — superposition and interference](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/16-10-superposition-and-interference); [Feynman Lectures, Ch. 29](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/I_29.html)). Radio and Wi-Fi are **engineering** stories on top of that math: **bands**, **power limits**, **modulation**, sometimes **orthogonal** carriers—**designed** coexistence, not a guarantee that “different frequency” means “no interaction” in every material or **nonlinear** regime (intermodulation and beats still exist where linearity fails).

I use the radio image **metaphorically**: if the æther is an **infinite vibrational plane**, then **spirits** or **conscious patterns** might occupy **bands** that **do not collapse into each other** the way two voices on the **same** narrow channel do. **Higher** harmonization could mean **awareness** of more bands at once; **lower** vibration could mean **blindness** to what sits **above**—not moral sneering, **geometry of coupling**.

That picture is **speculative**. It is also the bridge I need from **physics** to **reincarnation**.
