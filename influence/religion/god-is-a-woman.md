# God Is a Woman!

And I’m Gonna Prove It

![Golden Age remembered — Bosch garden as creation memory](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/saturn/golden_age_hieronimus_bosch.jpg)

I’m writing a book called ***The Third Story***. It tells the full story of Earth and the other planets up until the modern day. What follows is a simplified narration of that spine — the same gestation every life form on this planet still repeats — with the contexts that make the claim unavoidable: **God is a woman.**

## Warm darkness

The story of Earth starts exactly the same as the story of any life form that has ever been born here. A long-lasting period of **warm darkness**. Full sustenance. No conflict. Very little light, if any. A seed that cannot die, waiting to be born.

Every mammal still rehearses that chapter in the womb. The planet’s oldest stories rehearse it in the sky: a time before scarcity, before day and night as we know them, before the argument about who was watching. That is not metaphor for metaphor’s sake. Worlds, in the Saturnian reading kept here, lived it too — enclosure before separation, nourishment before scarcity, a cavity before a sky that could be questioned. The technical spine for field, womb, and first form sits with the [Circle of Life](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/biology/circle-of-life-and-how-it-began.md) and the [chronology of creation](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/page.md). Here the point is simpler: **before the argument about God, there was gestation.**

## The moment of light

Then came the moment of light — when the humans and animals of creation were filled with the same energy that linked the planets into the **Saturnian Golden Age**. That charge did more than warm the ground. It gave them the notion to question their existence: **why**, and **what**.

![Absu enclosure — Golden Age plasma layers](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/saturn/golden_age_absu.jpg)

For that reason, human history — the kind that stares up and names what it sees — starts at the beginning of the Golden Age. Humanity looking at the object that just created them. Very simple phonetics. Sounds like **baal**. Sounds that would later sour into **maal**. People who did not yet own thick languages still had throats and a sky. When languages were first being formed, they looked up and saw the Creator. They did not need a seminary to invent the first syllable of praise. They needed a light in the north and a mouth that could copy it.

## The Creator was female

The further you look back by analyzing Indigenous records as they were before they were redacted, the more you find the same pattern: the Creator was some kind of **female**, capable of creation, rather than the male personality most of Earth has been trained to assume in our lifetime.

That claim will get you laughed at. Good. Laughter is what a captured priesthood sounds like when the old memory returns.

Redaction is the reason the laugh works. Once the records are filtered through mission schools, museum captions, and monotheist canon, the mother looks like a “fertility goddess” — a local specialty — while the father keeps the title Creator. Read the older strata before that filter and the specialty collapses. Creation itself was her job.

Examples are not hard to find once you stop asking only European catechisms. The Kogi of Colombia remember a **Great Mother** who creates by concentrated thought. Shawnee tradition names **Our Grandmother** as supreme Creator. The *Tao Te Ching* calls being the **mother of the ten thousand things**. Cross-tradition surveys already show eastern emanation languages that keep a mother or womb grammar without needing a craftsman-king — see [creation interpretations across religions](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/investigations/creation-event-interpretations.md).

Israel’s own archaeology still argues over **Asherah** beside Yahweh. Inscriptions from Kuntillet Ajrud and Khirbet el-Qom speak of Yahweh and his asherah. Scholars fight over whether that means a goddess consort or a sacred pole. Either way, a female divine presence was loud enough that later editors had to polemicize her into lumber and “grove” translations. That is what forgetting looks like when it still leaves fingerprints.

In the Saturnian chronology, Saturn is already read as the **mother principle**, the receiving womb-body of planetary generation, while the Sun reads as the father principle of directed discharge — see again the [Circle of Life](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/biology/circle-of-life-and-how-it-began.md). Saturn also appears as Kronos devouring children. Those are not two different planets. They are two faces of the same sky memory: **nourishing enclosure** in the Golden Age, **devouring collapse** when the age ended. The mother did not leave. The story about her did.

The Creator must have been capable of creation. **Only women are.** You can’t really be a man unless you accept that. A lot of people who ran religions for the last couple of centuries were not real men, because their church did not allow them to be. That is not a joke about chromosomes. It is a judgment about who was allowed to honor the mother and who was paid to forget her.

## Baal became Maal

So what changed the gender of God?

The **Dark Age** — when Baal went and became Maal.

![Kronos devouring — Golden Age ends](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/saturn/golden_age_ends_kronos_devours.jpg)

In Saturnian cosmology and in most Bibles, the good creator god keeps changing into the bad punishing god. Baal is not a random label here. In the comparative record, Baal Hammon was equated with Roman **Saturn** in North Africa; catastrophist and Saturnian writers already track Baal among names that once pointed at the polar high god. **Mal** means bad — Latin *malus*, French *mal*, the English prefix that still marks malpractice and malfunction.

Connect those under early phonetics and you get the simplest explanation available: the balancing Creator of the Golden Age was named in the *baal* / *ball* sound-family — the same root people still use for **ball**, **balance**, and even **ballerina**, terms that look unrelated until you remember that age was about **equipoise** in the sky. When that god disappeared into catastrophe and the living generation decided it was being punished, the name soured. Something like **Maal**. Something that later languages still carry as **mal**.

Handbook etymology will tell you those English words come from different Indo-European roots and that *mal-* does not “descend from” Canaanite Baal. Fine. Handbooks also prefer not to notice that biblical writers already **poisoned** Baal’s name on purpose — swapping Baal for *bosheth*, “shame,” in personal names and prophetic memory. The habit of turning the old lord-word into a bad word is documented. Extending that habit into *mal* and the ball/balance family is my synthesis. The receipts and the falsifiers live in the [companion investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/religion/investigations/god-is-a-woman-creator-gender-redaction-investigation.md).

Suddenly those who still praised the Creator had to justify why everything was happening — why everyone was being punished. They came up with stories like: we ate the forbidden fruit. Otherwise something was disrupted on high, and it was ruined for the entire species. That is how Eden-talk functions after paradise ends. The comparative file already maps garden expulsion to the start of dark-age toil across Abrahamic witnesses.

## Love versus fear

It is actually very easy to understand that the One World Religion of the Golden Age would splinter in the Dark Age — specifically between those who still held on to the **love** of the Golden Age, and those who were falling into the **fear** of the Dark Age.

Love kept the old nourishment: enclosure, abundance, the mother who does not abandon the child even when the child cannot see her. Fear kept the new weather: famine, thunder, exile, the sense that somebody upstairs was angry and that the species had earned it. Same planet. Same ruined sky. Two religions growing out of one trauma.

As a result, it was hard for anyone, man or woman, to imagine that a punishing Creator God was still female. Compassion seemed to be gone. A mother who feeds in the dark is one memory. A sky that floods, burns, and starves is another. Fear prefers a judge. Fear prefers a king. Fear prefers a voice that sounds like the men who already held the weapons. Once the sermons needed a punisher, the mother got demoted to Mary, to Wisdom, to a pole in the temple courtyard — anything except the throne.

## Exodus and the pieces

Toward the end of the Dark Age, that one Creator God who had gone missing reappeared in another form. The Old Testament remembers the reign of a very active and influential god during the **Exodus** story — one that intervened directly in human affairs and caused world cataclysm.

![Exodus — intervening sky power](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/saturn/exodus.jpg)

In that moment, the world might have been unified in the notion that the creator of your god was always a **punishing male** character, rather than the good creator remembered in the Golden Age. Dating and identity for that intervention are argued hard elsewhere — Velikovsky’s Venus Passover, Exodus and promised-land threads, electrical catastrophe readings. The point here is the gender and mood of the god on stage: **active, male, punishing, world-breaking.**

Then later, this new form of the god seemed to fall apart into pieces — so says the Egyptian legend of Osiris cut up and scattered by Set. Afterwards there was a great confusion. Temples still named the dead god. Priests still collected the offerings. The sky no longer showed the living body those rites were built for. The main question was obvious: **why would anyone worship anything that is no longer visible?** Monotheism fell into ruin until it would reappear during the Crusades. (Different centuries pull different arrows — one Crusade layer breaks Church monopoly toward many gods; another installs austere reconquest. Both can be true on different boards. Do not mash them into one parade.)

## The sun years

During a not-too-well-recorded period of freedom, when humanity spread all over the world under the new god — the **sun** — we became largely sun-worshippers. It was again possible for everyone to remember, or to forget, or to misremember the original creator god as a woman. Instead, masculine names were given to the sun. Helios. Sol. Shamash in later filing. Christ-as-Sun in the memory lanes this chronology already keeps.

Later the monotheists caused incursions around the world, converting or killing everyone into monotheism. At that point the sun god was forgotten and became just the sun — its abundance and its revolution denied as even having been a god at all. The [chronology hub](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/page.md) already tracks solar religion after the planet-god ages, and later campaigns that turned the visible saviour-body back into “just astronomy.” The monotheists got to define God however they wanted. They decided to put all of the authority into the male members of their family, and to discourage women from having knowledge or power. Invisible God plus male clergy is a clean machine: no living sky to check the sermon against, and half the species told to stay out of the library.

## Read the Mishnah

If you don’t believe me, read the Mishnah.

Not the Talmud by accident. Not Maimonides’ code by renaming. The **Mishnah** — early rabbinic law compiled around 200 CE. In *Seder Nashim*, the Order of Women, the legal imagination already sorts marriage, divorce, vows, and suspicion around male authority. And in [Mishnah Sotah 3:4](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sotah.3.4), after Ben Azzai says a man should teach his daughter Torah, **Rabbi Eliezer** answers: anyone who teaches his daughter Torah is teaching her promiscuity (*tiflut*).

That line did not invent every patriarchy on Earth. It is a crystal. It shows the mechanism in plain court Hebrew: **knowledge for daughters framed as danger; male gatekeeping as piety.** Ben Azzai’s opposite opinion is still in the same mishnah — which proves the fight was live. Eliezer’s side is the one later piety liked to remember. Thenceforth, if the schools and pulpits that inherited that grammar got to define God, of course God forgot how to be a woman.

## Mama, be with me

![Hairy Mary — mother persistence in Christian art](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/religion/hairy_mary/hairy_mary.jpg)

For those people of any religion who still believe God has always been on their side, and who yell that name when they are in distress — they do not do so because the church told them God was a man. They do so because they remember that God was the **mother**. That is how every life form always acts toward its mother.

I got this notion from watching a *Silent Hill* movie. As gruesome as it is, the woman who gets burned says, **“Mama, be with me.”** The same film states the theology in a cleaner sentence: **“Mother is God in the eyes of a child.”**

That is the real nature of praising God. The mother you always want to be there — even at a great distance, even after death. The belief that the mother actually cannot die, and will always exist, long after we’re gone.

Warm darkness. Light. A name that balanced the age. A name that went bad. A judge who replaced a mother. Laws that told daughters not to learn. And still, in the fire, the oldest prayer left on the tongue.
