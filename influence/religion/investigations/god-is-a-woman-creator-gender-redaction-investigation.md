## 1. Author’s originating thesis

> **Author (2026-07-26 session):** Writing a book called ***The Third Story*** — full story of Earth and the other planets up to the modern day. Narrate a simplified version with contexts and citations.
>
> The story of Earth starts the same as any life form born on Earth: a long-lasting period of **warm darkness**, full sustenance, no conflict, very little light — like a seed that cannot die, waiting to be born. Then the moment of light, when humans and animals of creation were filled with the energy that linked the planets into the **Saturnian Golden Age**. That gave them the notion to question existence — why and what. Human history starts at the beginning of the Golden Age, staring up at the object that created them, using simple phonetics like **baal** and **maal**.
>
> Look further back in Indigenous records before redaction: many suggest the Creator was **female**, capable of creation, rather than the male personality of our lifetime. Saying God is female today, you’d be laughed at — but that’s the claim. The Creator must have been capable of creation, and **only women are**. You can’t really be a man unless you accept that. A lot of people who ran religions for the last couple centuries were not ‘real’ men, because their church didn’t allow them to be.
>
> What changed the gender of God? The **Dark Age**, when **Baal went and became Maal**. Those who praised the Creator had to justify why everyone was being punished — stories like forbidden fruit; something disrupted on high and ruined for the species. The One World Religion of the Golden Age splintered: those who still held the **love** of the Golden Age vs those falling into the **fear** of the Dark Age. Hard for anyone to imagine a punishing Creator as still female when compassion seemed gone.
>
> Toward the end of the Dark Age the missing Creator reappeared in another form. The Old Testament remembers a very active, intervening god in the **Exodus** story — world cataclysm. For a moment the world might unify on a punishing **male** creator rather than the good Golden Age creator. Later that form seemed to fall apart into pieces (Egyptian legend); confusion: why worship what is no longer visible? Monotheism fell into ruin until the **Crusades**. In a poorly recorded period of freedom under the **sun**, humanity spread; sun-worshippers; possible to rememorize or misremember the original creator as woman, while masculine names went to the sun. Later monotheist incursions converted or killed into monotheism; the sun god forgotten as just the sun; monotheists defined God, put authority in male family members, discouraged women from knowledge or power — **read the Mishnah**. That is why God was forgotten as a woman. But people who yell God’s name in distress do so because they remember God was the **mother** — how every life form acts toward its mother. Notion from *Silent Hill*: the burned woman says **“Mama, be with me.”** That is the real nature of praising God: the mother you want there even at a distance or after death; belief that the mother cannot die and will always exist.

**Unpack (what they mean):**

1. **Gestation → light** — Earth/life as embryogenesis before Golden Age sky-link; matches [Circle of Life](/science/biology/circle-of-life-and-how-it-began.md) mother-field / Saturn womb grammar and [chronology hub](/history/chronology/page.md) warm-dark → creation light.
2. **Feminine Creator** — creation capability as the proof of gender; philosophical/theological claim, **not** a genetics paper. Prior art exists for **female supreme creators** (Kogi, Shawnee, Taoist mother language) and for **Asherah** beside Yahweh; the **full package** (Golden Age female → Dark Age male via Baal/mal → Mishnah lock → mother-in-distress) is author synthesis (**AU-040**).
3. **Baal → Maal** — early phonetic pejoration: balancing Creator (**Baal**/ball/balance) → “bad” (**mal**/Maal) when the sky god turns punitive. Documented pieces: Baal↔Saturn syncretism; biblical Baal→*bosheth* (“shame”) dysphemism; Latin *malus* / *mal-* = bad. The **prehistoric Baal→mal sound-bridge** and the **ball/balance/ballerina** family as one Golden Age root are **author open** (§4).
4. **Religion split** — love (Golden Age memory) vs fear (punisher theology); Eden as justification narrative; Exodus as active intervening male cataclysm-god; Osiris dismemberment as “form fell into pieces”; solar interlude; Crusade-era monotheist reconsolidation (keep Crusade arrows **separate** — §11).
5. **Mishnah** — named legal corpus for male authority / women’s knowledge discouragement; cite *Sotah* 3:4 precisely; do **not** conflate Mishnah, Talmud, Mishneh Torah.
6. ***Silent Hill*** — cultural proof of mother-as-God-in-distress, not cosmology evidence.

## 4. Lexical audit — Baal → mal, ball / balance / ballerina

### 4.1 Author claim (open)

> Baal was the name of Saturn at one point in Saturnian cosmology, and **Maal means bad**. Connect those two under very early phonetics — languages first forming, people looking up at the Creator. **Ball**, **ballerina**, **balance**, and related critical terms that seem unrelated all trace back to this one Creator that **balanced** the Golden Age. In the Dark Age he disappeared and people thought he was punishing everyone, so they used a different name that later became **Maal** / **mal**.

### 4.2 Handbook contrast (required)

| Form | Mainstream etymology (Etymonline / standard refs) | Author bridge |
|------|---------------------------------------------------|---------------|
| **Baal** | NW Semitic *baʿal* “lord, owner, husband”; Canaanite storm/fertility high god | Golden Age Creator theonym; Saturnian polar lord |
| **mal- / malus** | Latin *malus* “bad”; Fr. *mal*; PIE *mel-* (3) “false, bad, wrong” | Dark Age pejorative renaming of the same sky power (**Maal**) |
| **ball** (sphere) | PIE *bhel-* “blow, swell” | Round / balanced Creator body / polar globe memory |
| **ball** (dance) / **ballerina** | Late Lat. *ballare* ← Gk. *ballizein* “dance/jump” ← PIE *gwele-* “throw” | Ritual / cosmic dance of balanced age (open rhyme) |
| **balance** | Lat. *bilanx* “two pans” (*bis* + *lanx*) | Equipoise of Golden Age configuration — **semantic** rhyme stronger than phonetic identity |

**Handbook verdict:** Standard historical linguistics **does not** derive *mal-* from *Baal*, nor unite *ball* / *balance* / *ballerina* under one Semitic theonym. Treating them as one early root is **author Lane A–style** reconstruction (pronunciation-loss / pejoration), parallel to [lexical redaction hub](/history/chronology/investigations/lexical-redaction-hub-investigation.md).

### 4.3 Strongest *documented* pejoration parallel (not mal)

Biblical writers replace theophoric **Baal** with **בֹּשֶׁת** (*bosheth*, “shame”) — Ish-bosheth, Mephibosheth, Jerubbesheth; Hosea links Baal-Peor to “shame.” Scott B. Noegel: “mnemonics of odium” — sounding Baal’s consonants pulls “shame” vocabulary ([JNSL 2015 / 2022](https://faculty.washington.edu/snoegel/PDFs/articles/noegel-baal-jnsl-41-2015.pdf)).

**Implication for AU-040:** Deliberate **name-poisoning** of Baal is **on the scholarly record**. Author extends that *habit* backward/phonetically into **mal** (“bad”) and forward into English *mal-* vocabulary. The extension is **open**, not handbook-closed.

### 4.4 Falsifiers

1. Continuous bilingual glosses showing *Baal* and *malus* as unrelated with **no** pejorative contact in the relevant window.
2. Dated early phonology proving *b-* → *m-* shift **impossible** in the claimed speech community (would constrain, not automatically kill, folk pejoration).
3. Finding that all *ball/balance* senses are fully explained by *bhel-* / *bilanx* with **zero** cultic overlap in early attestations (weakens semantic family; does not kill Baal→shame→bad *idea*).

## 10. Mishnah — women’s knowledge and male authority

**Primary:** [Mishnah Sotah 3:4](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sotah.3.4) (William Davidson / Steinsaltz English via Sefaria):

> From here Ben Azzai states: A person is obligated to teach his daughter Torah, so that if she drinks and does not die immediately, she will know that some merit she has delayed punishment for her. **Rabbi Eliezer says: Anyone who teaches his daughter Torah is teaching her promiscuity [*tiflut*].**

**Context:** Dispute inside *Seder Nashim* (Order of Women); Ben Azzai vs R. Eliezer. Later tradition (Yerushalmi hyperbole; Maimonides distinctions Written vs Oral) shows the Eliezer line was **influential**, not the only voice.

**Author weight:** Named corpus that **discourages** daughters’ Torah study and embeds women in male-contract / purity / divorce legal frames (*Ketubot*, *Gittin*, etc.). **Not** claimed: one mishnah invented monotheism, or Mishnah = Talmud = Mishneh Torah.

**Corpus hygiene:** Circle of Life cites **Sanhedrin 65b** (Talmud) for golem — different corpus; keep labels straight.

- **“Only women create”** is philosophical / theological rhetoric in the author voice — not a developmental-biology finding; do not steelman as gamete science.
- **Baal→mal** and **ball/balance/ballerina** unity are **open** against handbook etymology; biblical **Baal→bosheth** is the closest **documented** pejoration habit.
- **Osiris** and **Crusades** have **multiple** in-corpus mappings — merging them is a drafting error.
- **Mishnah ≠ Talmud ≠ Mishneh Torah.**
