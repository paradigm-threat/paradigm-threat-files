## 3. Documented — auditing / OT as “recovered history” sessions

**What the Church presents (public layer):** After **Clear**, Scientologists may purchase access to confidential **Operating Thetan (OT)** levels (OT I–VIII in standard descriptions). These are framed as **advanced spiritual training** that recovers ability and past-track / space-opera history through **auditing** — structured one-on-one sessions with an auditor and (typically) an **E-meter**, not generic “meditation” ([Wikipedia — Operating Thetan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operating_Thetan)).

**Why this matters here:** The **method claim** is that **session work recovers real history** (incident tracks, implants, galactic politics) that ordinary memory and secular schooling omit. That is the structural parallel to other **operator / recovery** stacks in this corpus — whether or not Hubbard’s cosmology is true.

| Practice | Public description | Repo parallel (thematic, not identity) |
|----------|-------------------|----------------------------------------|
| **Auditing** | Guided recall / confrontation of “engrams” and later OT incidents | Seer-stone / hat coupling; RV session worksheets |
| **OT III specifically** | Confidential materials about Xenu / Teegeeack catastrophe | “Recovered” catastrophe memory after seal / leak |
| **Secrecy gate** | Materials withheld until prior levels + payment | Inner/outer myth split; Latin / initiation gate languages |

**Not claimed:** that E-meter readings are laboratory-validated remote sensing of Mars, or that Hubbard obtained the 1984 CIA PDF.

**Documented institutional behavior:** Church entities treat upper-level materials as **trade secrets** and litigate to suppress disclosure; suppression **amplifies** distribution (Streisand / Fishman pattern). Hubbard’s parallel **science-fiction** career (*Battlefield Earth*, pulp cosmology) supplies the **hoax exit** the public uses.

## 6. Author read — volcanoes + nukes = catastrophe signal / weapon backfire

**Documented beat:** Secondary OT III summaries place victims at **volcanoes** and kill them with **hydrogen bombs**.

**Author question:** If the story were **pure pulp invention** with no catastrophe memory underneath, why **volcanoes**? A war plot can end with bombs on cities, orbital strikes, or camps. Volcano rings are a **strange specificity**.

**Author answer (AU-068):**

1. **Worldwide ecological catastrophe signal** — volcanoes encode **planet-scale atmospheric / biosphere failure**, not a localized battlefield. That matches the author’s OT III outcome: Earth **ecologically ruined**, **not** physically erased.
2. **Nuke → volcano language as weapon backfire** — read as an allusion to a **Mars-class energetic weapon** coupling into Earth’s **volcanic / energetic systems**, producing **far more damage than the operators intended** (overshoot). Parallel grammar: Ender’s **maximum strike** destroying more than the intended target ([Ender’s Game dossier](/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/card/enders-game-plot-order-mars-china-investigation.md)); MFEE as **unplanned** shock that detaches networks ([Mars dagger § MFEE detachment](/cosmos/mars/mars-dagger.md)).
3. **Not claimed:** a peer-reviewed geophysics paper proving H-bombs at volcanoes caused the MFEE; this is **myth-decode**, tiered as author thesis.
