## The simple claim

One creator was in the sky: **Saturn**. It had a sustaining face and a punishing face. Later books split those faces into **God** and **Satan**. English made the two names almost the same word. After the Sun became the obvious giver of abundance, the old creator was rewritten as the only benign God, and the cruelty was filed under Satan. People who say the names were one character get treated as blasphemers. People who believe neither name get told both were psychology for control. An honest atheist keeps the skepticism and drops the school clock: no proven Big Bang, no tree-ring count of millions of years. That path ends at Saturnian cosmology, which is omitted from school because Saturn is still the figurehead inside the religions.

## Q1: Can you prove that Satanism ever really existed?

**The argument:** Satanism is either an eternal underground church or a modern joke with no adherents. Prove which.

**Response:**

Prove the modern organizations. On **30 April 1966** (Walpurgisnacht) Anton LaVey founded the Church of Satan in San Francisco and called 1966 Year One. The church’s own account says it was the first body to take Satan as a positive symbol, and that earlier references were condemnations by other religions ([press kit](https://churchofsatan.com/online-press-kit/)). *The Satanic Bible* (1969) is a real book. In **1975** Michael Aquino left and founded the Temple of Set, which treats Egyptian Set as a real being rather than LaVey’s metaphor. The Satanic Temple (2013) is a real atheist group using the image in politics. People have called themselves Satanists, in public, for decades. That is existence.

Do not prove a continuous Church of Satan from ancient Egypt. Set is real in Egyptian religion: killer of Osiris, and in other hymns a defender of the sun-bark. Plutarch’s Greek readers call him Typhon and treat him as the disorderly principle ([*Isis and Osiris* §49](http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A2008.01.0239%3Asection%3D49)). That is not a membership roll. Medieval “devil worship” is mostly accusation.

Our position separates a third thing from both of those. Worship of the **dark face of the one creator** is what later speech calls Satanism. It did not require LaVey. It was the same Atlantean sky religion, still aimed at Saturn, after crusades had renamed the faces. LaVey’s atheist symbol is a late costume, not the origin.

## Q2: Can you demonstrate that God and Satan were the same character?

**The argument:** God and Satan are opposites. Saying they were one person is a modern blur, or blasphemy. The Renaissance only separated good and evil in fiction, not in religion.

**Response:**

Inside the Bible, one act changes subjects. [2 Samuel 24:1](https://biblehub.com/2_samuel/24-1.htm): the **LORD** incites David to take a census. [1 Chronicles 21:1](https://biblehub.com/1_chronicles/21-1.htm): **Satan** incites the same census. That is a substitutive rewrite (our **R3**), not a second event. Elsewhere the adversary is still God’s own agent: the angel of the LORD is a “satan” to Balaam ([Numbers 22:22](https://biblehub.com/numbers/22-22.htm)); in Job the accuser harms the man only with God’s leave. [Isaiah 45:7](https://biblehub.com/isaiah/45-7.htm) has the one LORD creating light and darkness, peace and evil. Later English often softens that last word to “calamity.”

So the texts already know a single divine character who owns the dark face, and a later editorial habit of giving that face another name. Christianity and Islam finish the habit: Satan and Iblis are creatures, rebels, not the creator, so the creator can stay morally pure. Cathars went the other way and called the material creator the evil god. The chronology hub already has them calling Saturn (Satan) religions materialistic. Dualism is the split completed, not the original picture.

The Renaissance did harden European literature into good persons and evil persons. It did not invent the split. Arthurian romance is already moral by Malory’s century ([Q5](#q5-did-the-king-arthur-story-split-one-sky-into-good-and-evil)). Jung’s *Answer to Job* (1952) sees the same seam and calls it psychology: Satan as God’s dark side, Christ as the repair that makes God purely good ([overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Answer_to_Job)). We keep his seam and replace his conclusion. The pre-split referent is Saturn, not an archetype.

What this does **not** demonstrate is a verse that says “YHWH is the planet Saturn.” Amos 5:26 condemns the star Kaiwan, usually identified with Saturn. It does not say Israel’s god **is** that star. The planetary identification is the Saturnian thesis of this project, read from sky memory and comparative names, with the biblical split as the editorial scar.

**Citations:** 2 Samuel 24:1; 1 Chronicles 21:1; Numbers 22:22; Isaiah 45:7; Jewish Encyclopedia, “Satan”; [dossier §5](/influence/religion/investigations/satan-saturn-same-creator-good-evil-split-investigation.md).

## Q3: Is Satan just Saturn without the R?

**The argument:** They sound the same in English, so they are the same word, or the similarity is an accident and means nothing.

**Response:**

A child hears them as one word. An average person does too. Adulthood is when we are taught to deny that hearing.

The literature we are still allowed to quote gives two roots. **Satan** is Hebrew *śāṭān*, “adversary,” via Greek and Latin ([Etymonline](https://www.etymonline.com/word/Satan)). **Saturn** is Latin *Saturnus*, an Italic god lined up with Greek Kronos, possibly Etruscan ([Etymonline](https://www.etymonline.com/word/Saturn)). There may be no way, inside that literature, to prove the connection. That is because the literature has been forbidden. A religion that still has Saturn as its figurehead will not stock the shelf with the proof of its own name.

After the churches split one creator into two characters, English left the cover in place: love God, hate Satan, and say almost the same word as the planet. The sky reconstruction and the census rewrite in Q2 are the texts that survived. They are not a license to tell the child he misheard.

**Final point.** We are never going to prove something religion forbids until we shed the yoke of that same religion.

## Q4: What does 1666 have to do with Satan as authority on earth?

**The argument:** 1666 is the Number of the Beast plus a thousand, or it is only the Great Fire, or it is when the devil visibly took the throne.

**Response:**

**Documented.** Christian writers assigned the apocalyptic year to 1666. Jews and Christians both entertained a near messianic crisis ([Jewish Encyclopedia, Shabbethai Zebi](https://www.jewishencyclopedia.com/articles/13480-shabbethai-zebi-b-mordecai)). London then suffered the Great Plague (1665) and the Great Fire (2–6 September 1666). Comets were preached as warnings. Sabbatai Zevi’s messianic movement belongs to that crest.

**In our chronology.** [1666](/history/chronology/page.md) is a climax of institutional power. People were ready to read the disasters as God or Jesus restoring order. What actually consolidated was paper sovereignty, canon law, and a money system. The hub calls that showing as **the Beast**, and it already glosses 666 as Saturn. The same hub’s heavenly Jerusalem (three walls, six gates each) is the older 6-6-6, “associated with Satan i.e. Saturn.”

**Author line.** “Satan revealed itself as the authority on earth” means that administration, not a monster in the street. The cult that had always maintained the creator did not pick a new god in 1666. It became visible as fear, debt, and sacred law, while the preached god stayed kind. LaVey’s Church in **1966** declared its own “reign of Satan.” That is a rhyme on the calendar and a modern club. It is not a document that the fire was scheduled for the Black House.

## Q5: Did the King Arthur story split one sky into good and evil?

**The argument:** Arthur, Lancelot, and Guinevere are folklore. Mapping them onto planets is arbitrary, and the good-versus-evil plot is the original plot.

Our map is fixed in the [remap registry](/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/myth-planet-remap-registry.md) as **Layer RT**, and it is already the [Round Table dossier](/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/white/white-saturnian-round-table-investigation.md):

- **Arthur** reigning is **Jupiter**’s crowned face. He was not a separate person from that planet in the sky reading.
- **Merlin** is Jupiter’s other face: the wizard, the terrible knowledge, the beard read as the coma of the Mountain of Heaven.
- **Lancelot** is **Mars**.
- **Guinevere** is **Venus** desirable. **Morgana** is Venus terrible, and she still has a healer residue, so the later “pure evil” label does not quite fit the older material.
- “Once and Future King” is a **different layer**: our Sun slowly driving the old god-kings away. It does not mean Arthur was the Sun during the Round Table.

The later story does what the author describes. Arthur is simply good. Merlin is suspect and still useful. Mordred and Morgana carry the destruction. That sorting is already medieval (Vulgate, Malory), then hardened in later European literature. It is the specimen for the religious split: one ambivalent cast becomes a moral cast. Two limits. **Mordred** and **Morgana** are not securely names that mean “death.” The folk echo of French *mort*, and the popular link from Morgan to the Morrígan, are not locked etymologies. ## Q6: Why do modern atheists say God and Satan have no physical origin?

**The argument:** Serious people now know both characters were invented, either as Jungian archetypes or as a control story. Looking for a planet is the mistake.

**Response:**

That conclusion is what you get after the split succeeds and the sky is forgotten.

Jung, in *Answer to Job*, is the sophisticated version. He treats the God of Job as morally mixed, Satan as that god’s dark side, and the purely good Christ as a later repair. He is right that the dark face was inside the god-image. He then locates the whole drama in the psyche. There is no Saturn left to point at.

Popular atheism is the blunt version. God and Satan are memes, priestcraft, or politics. They were never bodies. LaVey agrees from inside the word Satanism: his church is atheist, and Satan is a symbol of pride ([press kit](https://churchofsatan.com/online-press-kit/)). A religion named for the adversary announces that the adversary was never physical.

Our position is that control is real and incomplete as an explanation. Crusades, blasphemy rules, and the Rushdie fatwa (14 February 1989; stabbed 12 August 2022) show what happens when the composition of a scripture is examined. Rushdie’s charge was insult around Muhammad and the “satanic verses” episode, not a Saturn paper. The **class** of intimidation is the point. Velikovsky’s *Worlds in Collision* (1950) shows the quieter version: a catastrophist Saturn book forced off Macmillan by academic boycott. None of that makes the pre-split referent a mood. The referent argued here is the creator planet, then the successor planets in the Arthur map, then a Sun whose generosity was written back onto Saturn so people would keep obeying the old god as a benign father ([God Is a Woman, AU-040](/influence/religion/investigations/god-is-a-woman-creator-gender-redaction-investigation.md), sibling on gender and the solar interlude).

## Q7: Where should an honest atheist end up?

**The argument:** Atheism already finished the job. God and Satan are myths, the universe began in a Bang, and the Earth is billions of years old. Saturnian cosmology is one more religion.

**Response:**

Our position is the opposite destination. A true atheist is someone who follows patterns that add up and who uses the same skepticism on the school story that he used on the church. The Big Bang is an unobserved creation of the universe, scaled up from the life cycle of a solar system ([Cosmology Q1](/cosmos/cosmology/qa/q01-big-melt-eternal-universe-solar-cycle.md)). A billions-of-years-old Earth is a stack of inferences. Tree rings have been counted in the thousands of years. No ring sequence validates millions of years, and the isotope clocks used for the rest are calibrated to the written chronology and can jump, as the 774–775 CE carbon-14 spike shows ([Common Questions Q2](/history/common-questions.md); [radiocarbon dossier](/history/chronology/investigations/radiocarbon-dating-investigation.md)).

Once those two dogmas are refused, the account that still covers the sky, the gods, the catastrophes, and the later split of one creator into God and Satan is Saturnian cosmology. It is the atheistic explanation in the plain sense: bodies, discharges, remembered configurations, and institutions that renamed them. It leaves the stones turned over.

School does not teach it. The author reports that hearing it once made the pattern click, and that a banned idea should not be able to do that. The reason the conclusion stays banned is that Saturn is still the most important figurehead in every world religion. A percentage of those people will kill, ban, or treat you as a threat for saying so. The Rushdie fatwa and the Velikovsky boycott are the class of that enforcement ([Q6](#q6-why-do-modern-atheists-say-god-and-satan-have-no-physical-origin)). The Big Bang can be praised, because it does not name that figurehead.
