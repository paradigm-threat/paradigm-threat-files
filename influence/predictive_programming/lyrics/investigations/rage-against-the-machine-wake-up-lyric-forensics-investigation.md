## Conclusions — corrected lyrics

### A. Opening — read it (not edit)

**Posted online (wrong):**

> Come on, although ya try to discredit  
> Ya still never **edit**  
> The needle, I’ll thread it  
> Radically poetic

**Corrected (Lyric Forensics conclusion):**

> Come on, although ya try to discredit  
> You still never **read it**  
> The needle, I’ll thread it  
> Radically poetic

| Step | Finding |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Whisper **never** emits **edit** (unbiased or forced-edit prompt). |
| 2 | Unbiased vocals: *“you’re still never **ready**”* — **ready** @ **0.913**. |
| 3 | Fast delivery: **read it ≈ ready** (final /t/ swallowed); **edit** is the distant sheet word. |
| 4 | **Logic:** after “try to **discredit**,” “never **read it**” is an on-point accusation; “never **edit**” is nearly empty. |

### B. Federal men — skins off of trees (not schemes on the dream)

**Posted online (wrong):**

> Yea, the several federal men  
> Who pulled **schemes on the dream**  
> And put it to an end

**Corrected (Lyric Forensics conclusion):**

> Yea, the several federal men  
> Who **rip skins off of trees**  
> And put it to an end

| Step | Finding |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Posted **dream** forced conf **0.062**; never wins unbiased. |
| 2 | ASR ending leans **tree(s)** over dream. |
| 3 | **Logic:** trees have **skins** (bark), not schemes — “schemes on the tree” is incoherent ASR hybrid. |
| 4 | **skins ≈ schemes** mondegreen; once ending is tree(s), only **skins…trees** is a physical sentence. |
| 5 | **Meaning:** federal men **demonstrate** they can **put something to an end** by stripping living structure. |

**Hub stance:** use the two corrected couplets above; treat posted **edit** and **dream** as sanitized sheet errors.
