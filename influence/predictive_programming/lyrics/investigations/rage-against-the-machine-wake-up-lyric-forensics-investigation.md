## Conclusion — corrected couplet

**Posted online (wrong):**

> Yea, the several federal men  
> Who pulled **schemes on the dream**  
> And put it to an end

**Corrected (author hear + semantic adjudication; Lyric Forensics conclusion):**

> Yea, the several federal men  
> Who **rip skins off of trees**  
> And put it to an end

**Why this is the conclusion**

| Step | Finding |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Posted **dream** is a bad audio match (forced conf **0.062**; never wins unbiased). |
| 2 | ASR ending leans **tree(s)** over dream. |
| 3 | **Logic:** trees have **skins** (bark). Trees do **not** have schemes. “Schemes on the tree” is incoherent filler from a model stuck between sheet onset and botanical ending. |
| 4 | **skins ≈ schemes** is a classic mondegreen under shouted consonants; once the ending is tree(s), only **skins…trees** makes a grammatical, physical sentence. |
| 5 | **Meaning:** federal men demonstrate they can **put something to an end** by a vivid act of stripping — bark from trees as the image of ripping an organization or ideology to pieces (author: including weather-scale force). Soft “dream” sheet turns that into abstract hope-spoilers. |

**Corrected lyric stance for this hub:** use **rip skins off of trees**; treat **dream** as sanitized sheet error.

## Author’s originating thesis

**Verbatim capture (session 2026-10-16):**

> … the official posted lyrics say, schemes on a dream, but I hear, rip skins off of trees. Now that line, the one I hear, seems to be a lot more effective to the narration, to the message, that these people are extremely powerful. They could do things like this. They can rip an organization, an ideology, to pieces, but more in reality, they can even control the weather and send a hurricane towards you to literally rip off the skins from trees. Radiohead said something similar about the CIA. How they can blow you sky high.  
> “And where are you? / They brought in the CIA / The tanks and the whole marines. / To blow me away, to blow me sky high.” not in this song, but an example of how people talk about the CIA back then, not ruiner of dreams only - they tear their enemies to pieces, extremely vicious  
> So that's how we're. Analyzing wake up, which is a very clear. Message in general.

**What that means (unpack):** Soft sheet language (**schemes / dream**) frames federal men as abstract spoilers of hope. The heard line (**skins / trees**) frames them as agents who can **strip living structure** — ideology, organization, or literal landscape — matching a 1990s rock idiom of CIA/military as **physical annihilators** (Radiohead *The Bends*), not mere dream-wreckers. Weather / hurricane is **author extension** (L3), not a claim Zack named hurricanes on the record.
