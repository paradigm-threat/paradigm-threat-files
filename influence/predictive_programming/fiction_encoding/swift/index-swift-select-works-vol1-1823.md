## Summary — why it matters for the Swift investigation

***A Tale of a Tub* (1704)** and ***The Battle of the Books* (1704)** establish Swift’s early mode: **allegorical religious/political satire** dressed as mock-scholarship. They train readers to expect **irony and persona**, which later critics apply automatically to *Gulliver*. This volume does **not** resolve the Mars-moons question; it **contextualizes** Swift’s habit of **serious-looking false documents**—relevant to the “was it ever sold as fiction?” thread in [swift-mars-giants-publication-stance.md](swift-mars-giants-publication-stance.md).
