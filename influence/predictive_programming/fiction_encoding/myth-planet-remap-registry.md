## Layer GG — George = Mars unitary knight-king → Western split (author)

Source: [AU-033 §5.3](/history/chronology/investigations/georgia-mirror-naming-rus-horde-mars-investigation.md); [reader essay](/history/chronology/georgia-was-the-second-promised-land.md).

**Premise:** St. George sky-myth warrior slot = **Mars**; whitewash strips planet assignment; romance **slices** one knight-king into several names.

| Split residue | Aspect taken | Aligns with |
|---------------|--------------|-------------|
| **Lancelot** | Mars **good**-warrior | Layer RT Lancelot = Mars (**cite; don’t reinvent**) |
| **Arthur** | **Good** face + **Sun / God / Resurrection** language parked on the king-name | Layer OFK Sun destiny + Layer RT *good*-king affect — **not** “Arthur was never Jupiter” |
| **Merlin** | **Terrible** aspect; **Jupiter** dying god / wizard | Layer RT Merlin = Jupiter wizard; in older Merlin traditions he is **not entirely dead** but sealed under stone / in cave / in oak, still heard as a disembodied voice |

**Living entombment / resurrection grammar:** Merlin’s “resurrection” is not only a cinematic dream. Across Malory and Vulgate / Post-Vulgate Merlin traditions, he is enclosed beneath stone or in a cave / tree prison; variants preserve an unseen voice that later knights can hear but cannot free. This is effectively the same mythic grammar as death-and-return: the god is gone from the visible scene but still acts or speaks. In sky terms, **Jupiter permanently left the scene after the Dark Ages ended** — sealed away forever — while literature preserved a voice from the sealed place.

**Checked Malory anchor:** *Le Morte d’Arthur*, Book IV, ch. I says Merlin “came never out” from beneath the great stone; ch. V has Sir Bagdemagus hear him lament and speak, although the stone cannot be lifted. This directly supports **sealed-but-speaking**. Cave / tree forms belong to the wider tradition and should not be attributed to that Malory scene.

**Venus split (author refinement):** Venus is also split by Western moral literature. **Guinevere / Gwenhwyfar** (not Genevieve) is the desirable / good face; **Morgan le Fay / Morgana** is the terrible witch face, while still retaining older healing traces (Avalon, care for wounded Arthur). The split is imperfect because the older Venus was ambivalent before Western good/evil sorting.

**doNotSay:** “Round Table was wrong; Arthur is only the Sun.”  
**doSay:** “Round Table maps the Table-age sky; Georgia names how whitewash reused those romance labels when slicing George=Mars; Sun language on Arthur is Layer OFK / parked resurrection-good.”

## Method lock — comparative sky memory, not Jung

The author **does not subscribe to Carl Jung’s proposals**. This registry does not explain repeated warriors, dragons, kings, witches, or dying gods through a collective unconscious or inherited psychic archetypes. Here **archetype** means a recurring comparative **story-role** only. The project asks whether unusually specific recurrences preserve intelligent eyewitness memory of a changed sky; it does not treat ancestors as childish minds projecting inner drama onto ordinary planets.
