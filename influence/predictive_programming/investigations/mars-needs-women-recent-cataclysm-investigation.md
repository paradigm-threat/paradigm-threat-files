# Mars needs women — recent cataclysm only

> **TL;DR:** The plot under this name is narrow. A **recent** cataclysm leaves the women of Mars unable to breed, or gone, and a crew comes to Earth **now** to take women. It is not a custom of thousands of years. The one obvious picture that states that plot is ***Frankenstein Meets the Space Monster*** (22 September 1965): “recent atomic war,” “we have no women,” “Capture of the Earth women.” The famous title ***Mars Needs Women*** (1967/68) abducts Earth women for a genetic shortage of girls and does not date a cataclysm. ***The Mysterians*** (1957) wants Earth women after a nuclear war the film places thousands of years back. ***Mars Needs Moms*** (2011) takes mothers and never names a war. §3 is the known shelf, including *Mutant Swinger from Mars*, *Devil Girl from Mars*, *Queen of Outer Space*, and *Species II*. Shorter mentions already on the [Mars page](/cosmos/mars/page.md), the [predictive-programming catalog](../page.md), and the [timeline appendix](https://timeline.paradigmthreat.net/timeline/evt-predictive-programming-film-tv-games#mars-needs-women). The android in the 1965 picture is a separate find: [lifelike human robots](/events/jfk/investigations/lifelike-human-robot-deception-investigation.md), with the spoken lines in [JFK §2.3](/events/jfk/investigations/jfk-rfk-staged-assassination-hoax-investigation.md#5-frankenstein-meets-the-space-monster-1965).

## 1. The gate

A picture is in the exact column only when all four are true.

1. The home world is Mars, or the picture is sold as Mars.
2. The women there can no longer breed, or they are gone.
3. The cause is a major **recent** cataclysm. A shortage they have lived with for thousands of years does not qualify.
4. They come to Earth now to take women for breeding.

The [timeline section “Mars Needs Women”](https://timeline.paradigmthreat.net/timeline/evt-predictive-programming-film-tv-games#mars-needs-women) states the same cluster: nearly every telling points at a recent cataclysm, and *Mars Needs Moms* (2011) is the exception that explains a political order and leaves the dead surface and the underground city unexplained. *Eyes Wide Shut* stays in that section as a different encoding. This file is the list.

## 2. Exact — *Frankenstein Meets the Space Monster* (1965)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Release** | 22 September 1965, Allied Artists, double bill with *Curse of the Voodoo*. Also sold as *Mars Invades Puerto Rico*, *Mars Attacks Puerto Rico*, *Duel of the Space Monsters*, and *Operation San Juan*. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankenstein_Meets_the_Space_Monster) |
| **Mars on the label** | The astronaut is introduced for a solo trip to Mars. A reporter asks about “a trip of 49 million miles” and “when you do get to Mars.” The alien speech says “our planet.” |
| **Android, center, general** | Filed in [JFK §2.3 item 5](/events/jfk/investigations/jfk-rfk-staged-assassination-hoax-investigation.md#5-frankenstein-meets-the-space-monster-1965). Not repeated here. |

The princess, to her crew:

> Of course you all know why we left our planet home so suddenly. As a result of the recent atomic war, it was determined unfit for further habitation. At least until the radiation level decreases.

Dr. Nadir:

> The lucky ones are dead. Of the others, some will go mad. The others will slowly rot away and die in gradual agony. We have won the war. We are the sole survivors. It belongs to us. But it means that we are extinct as a race unless, of course, we can find some good breeding stock and repopulate our planet.

The princess:

> We have won the war but we have no women. We have come here to this planet for one purpose only. To acquire breeding stock to repopulate our planet.
>
> Capture of the Earth women.

“It belongs to us” comes right after “we have won the war” and “we are the sole survivors.” The next order is the capture of women. The war is recent. The radiation has not fallen yet. They have no women. The raid is the whole purpose of the landing. That is the gate.

The cheap bill and the breeding plot are how a viewer is trained to throw the picture out, and women in the audience are the ones most likely to leave a genre that takes them as breeding stock. That reading, and the general in the room, stay with the JFK catalog. This file keeps the breeding plot.

## 3. Known media

The phenomenon is the raid. A world, usually sold as Mars, can no longer breed, and someone comes to Earth for women. §1 is still the exact column. A title can sit on this shelf and fail that test. King Kong, Jabba, and a living tire belong to the separate joke that any creature wants a girl. This table is the raid.

## 4. Author’s originating thesis

**Author (2026-11-18):**

> Mars is capturing women on Earth because women on Mars can't breed anymore as a result of a major recent cataclysm. This is not something they've been doing for thousands of years. The movie is all about stealing women from Earth because the women on Mars cannot give birth anymore. All the things in the movie are supposed to be dismissed as science fiction. The hokiness, done in the Ed Wood style, lends itself to that outcome. So does the topic. Most women watching will feel alienated from a science-fiction genre that treats them like objects to be captured and bred on another planet.

**What that means here:**

The 1965 speech is the specimen because it dates the war as recent and states the raid in one breath. A genetic shortage with no date, and a nuclear war placed thousands of years ago, are the pictures people already mix into “Mars needs women.” Mixing them hides the recency. The hokey surface and the breeding plot are what get the 1965 picture dismissed, and they push women out of the genre that carries the payload. The lifelike robot in the same picture was an unexpected find. It is not the subject of this file.

## 5. Author’s open claims

1. The recent-cataclysm breeding raid is a repeated predictive-programming deposit, not one junk plot.
2. As of this pass, the only obvious theatrical film that states all four gates is *Frankenstein Meets the Space Monster*. §3 is the known shelf around it.
3. *Mars Needs Women* is the title the public remembers. Its published cause is a genetic shortage of girls, not a recent war.
4. *The Mysterians* is the thousand-year version of the same demand, and it does not belong in the exact column.
5. The cheap bill and the treatment of women as breeding stock are how the 1965 deposit is thrown out. Women are the viewers most likely to leave the genre.
