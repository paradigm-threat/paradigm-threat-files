## 3. Author’s originating thesis — Tartaria, Rus–Horde, Prester John (through FF VII)

> **Supplied stance:** The **Final Fantasy** series **up until *Final Fantasy VII*** appears **inspired by** the **Rus Horde / Tartaria** myth complex and **Prester John**–style **lost Christian East** empire imagery — not as **literal historical allegory**, but as **the same mythic furniture** the chronology lane tracks ([Prester John file](/history/chronology/investigations/prester-john-presbyter-rus-horde-nc-investigation.md)). After **VII**, interest drops (**§7**).

**Unpack:** The repo’s **NC / Tartaria** thesis treats **medieval–early modern** geography and **empire** narratives as **highly redacted**. *FF* **I–VI** repeatedly stage **lost golden ages**, **empire collapse**, **magical technology as suppressed war hardware**, and **maps that change mid-story**—useful **PP mirrors** for readers already comparing **game geography** to **Tartarian map** discourse (**compare only**; **no** claim Square Enix cited **Fomenko**).

## 4. *Final Fantasy VI* — “Catastrophe” and mud-flood grammar (hypothesis)

**Documented fiction:** Kefka disrupts the **Warring Triad** balance; the **planet’s surface** is **violently reshaped**; civilization collapses into the **World of Ruin** — ash, poison, drowned geography, **survivors scattered** ([World of Ruin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_VI#World_of_Ruin)).

**Author parallel:** Online **mud flood / MFEE** narratives describe **sudden stratigraphic burial**, **institutional amnesia**, and **scavenger** cultures atop **older layers** ([MFEE hub](/history/mudflood/investigations/mfee-investigation.md)). **World of Ruin** **rhymes** that **grammar** (broken world-surfaces, **toxic** skies, **towns isolated**, **old tech** dug out). **Not** a claim that **FFVI** documents **1776** or any **specific** calendar event — **pattern** only.

## 5. Buried airships — trope and exemplar

**Series pattern:** Multiple installments **hide** or **entomb** **airships** (and **other** vehicles) so the party **excavates** **lost mobility**—training audiences that **flight tech** returns from **tombs** rather than **open patent** economics.

**Concrete beat (*FFVI*):** The **Falcon** is **recovered** from a **tomb / memorial** context tied to **Darill / Daryl** — i.e. **air superiority literally buried** until plot **resurrection** ([Falcon — Fandom](https://finalfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Falcon)). 

**Open question:** Whether **any** designer interview ties this to **real** **archaeology** or **Tartaria** discourse (**unlikely**; treat as **receiver-side** PP reading until **primary** quote).
