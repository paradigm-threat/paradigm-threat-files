## 2. What the games say (documented story, paraphrased)

### 2.1 *Metroid Prime* (GameCube, North America 18 Nov 2002; PAL 2003)

Chozo colonists on **Tallon IV** drop high technology, cut a city from stone, water, and trees, and treat the place as a sanctuary ([Beginnings / Purification](https://www.metroidwiki.org/wiki/Chozo_Lore)). They develop **distant sight** — visions of past and future ([Harmonization](https://www.metroidwiki.org/wiki/Chozo_Lore); PAL). A meteor then hits. Impact debris, a **Great Poison** that behaves almost sentient, mutations, a worm / parasite harvest in the planetary depths, a temple (**Cradle**) over the crater, **twelve Artifacts** as a lock (**Cipher**), invaders who want the poison as fuel, and Chozo who go mad or become **The Turned**.

**NTSC-only plot that PAL later strips** ([TCRF](https://tcrf.net/Metroid_Prime/Text_Differences)):

- **Exodus:** they leave the mortal forms they no longer need; they draw aside the veils of time and space and remain watchful.  
- **Meteor Strike (USA):** they are **borne back against their will**. They had already perceived the impact from **beyond space and time**. The poison rips them from that state. They wander as **shadows of the mortal forms** they left.  
- **Contain / Cradle (USA):** they have lived on the **edges of time and space**; they cannot return to their dimension while the poison spreads; they walk as **ghosts**; the dying land pulled them from a **dreaming state**.  
- **The Turned (USA):** survivors suffer **dimensional flux**, drifting across time and space; corrupted Chozo still wear the form but hunt the Artifacts.

PAL / *Metroid Prime Trilogy* keeps the meteor, poison, temple, and twelve keys, and drops the **return-from-beyond as shadows** engine. Living Chozo watch the disaster on the ground. TCRF: the EU/JP pass **substantially altering the plotline**. **AU-067:** that is the **trim**. The public already got the disaster training in NA 2002. The durable English after 2003 no longer carries the shadow remainder that still names the Mars worksheet.

**PP wedge (this file):** bird-statues, Power Suit, and named Phazon are costume. The disaster grammar is planetary catastrophe + ghost remainder + containment lock.

### 2.2 *Metroid Prime 2: Echoes* (2004)

There is **no Chozo Artifact logbook**. The rhyme moves to the **Luminoth**: a world split into Light and Dark Aether, energy stolen, temples and keys, a dying surface, invaders who feed on the wound ([Metroid Wiki lore index](https://www.metroidwiki.org/wiki/Chozo_Lore) — Prime 2 = Luminoth Lore). Same **containment / stolen biosphere** furniture. Different species label.

### 2.3 *Metroid Prime 3: Corruption* (2007) — SkyTown Data (Chozo creators)

Elysian mechanoids record Chozo builders of a **cloud-station** on storm-world **Elysia**, satellites aimed at distant worlds, a **Chozo Searcher** who briefly sees a planet that is also **sentient / alive**, Chozo **departure**, machine **hibernation** with data fed as **dreams**, then a **Leviathan seed** and **Phazon** matching the toxin that **devoured the creators’ planet** (Tallon IV). The living world **hurls parts of itself** through a wormhole ([SkyTown Data](https://www.metroidwiki.org/wiki/SkyTown_Data) — Creators, Discovery, Alone, Slumber, Loss, Phazon, Defeat).

PAL Prime 1 already guessed the meteor might be a chunk of a **planet coated in Great Poison** that suffered a violent end ([Great Poison](https://www.metroidwiki.org/wiki/Chozo_Lore)). Prime 3 names that source **Phaaze**.

### 4.1 Pre-disclosure vs unnoticed compare (**author**, 2026-10-03)

- Far side framed as hellish / other-dimensional; story destination label is **Neptune** (Mars-as-hell analogy with a spookier place-name — [PP hub](/influence/predictive_programming/page.md); [Philadelphia §8.1](/events/philadelphia/investigations/philadelphia-experiment-portal-precursor-investigation.md)).
