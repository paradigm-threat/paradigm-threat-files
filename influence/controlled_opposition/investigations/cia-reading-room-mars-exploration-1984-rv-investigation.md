The **§5.8.1** table is therefore a log of **furniture and institutional rhymes**, **not** a claim that **Spielberg-style precogs** or **Animus genetic tourism** are **documentary** descriptions of **STARGATE** physics.

#### 5.8.1 Remote viewing, precognition, and “viewing sessions” in fiction (expanded PP cluster)

**Philip K.
