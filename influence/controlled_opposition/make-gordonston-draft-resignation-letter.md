## Letter (draft text — not sent)

**Subject:** Resignation from Gordonston volunteer participation — park access ultimatum not met

To whom it concerns — Gordonston Neighborhood Association leadership, Juliette Gordon Low Park trustees, and neighbors who rely on this corridor for children:

I am writing to **resign** from **volunteer participation** in Gordonston neighborhood programs, events, and web work I have contributed to (including **[gordonstonartfair.com](https://gordonstonartfair.com)** and related outreach), effective **[date]**.

I am putting this in writing **now** — while this file is still a **draft** until the ultimatum fails — because the **timeline is not mine**. The next **Gordonston Art Fair** is slated for **the end of this year or early next year**. **Advertisement**, **ticketing**, and **financial processing** all begin **months** before the event. People who have relied on my web work and fair-season labor deserve **warning** before that ramp starts. I will **not** help with **handoff** or **transition**. I will **not** help **again** — not with the website, not with fair operations, not with neighborhood programs — **until** things change on the ground in this neighborhood as described below. That is not a bluff; it is lead time.

This is not a dramatic exit for its own sake. It follows a **bare-minimum request** that was **not met**.

I am **not** asking for a park with no rules. I am fine with **reasonable modern systems** — **group limits** for large events, **posted hours**, permits where scale requires them, and **Mary Calder Cottage** rented out with revenue to trustees. That is normal stewardship. My lines are narrow and explicit:

- **Under ten people**, there should be **no problem** — no iron-gate theater, no treating a small picnic as a policy crisis.  
- **Gordonston-only** / **Residents Only** that excludes **Twickenham** and **Avondale** must end. If you post **local residents only**, **local** must include **Twickenham** and **Avondale**, not only Gordonston deed language.  
- **Reopen** the **pre-2011** precedent — children’s park for this **corridor**, not a walled garden.  
- Put it **in writing**, ideally alongside a **rebuild** that reads as reconciliation, not only new signs.

I asked for repair, not a lawsuit. I am **no longer willing to live on the border of an apartheid**.

It is starting to seem that those things are **not possible** in this neighborhood — not because the will and the history are absent, but because **current leadership** has let this place become **divisive**, **fractured**, **intimidating**, and **petty** — a **system of control** that is **plausibly deniable**, often **laughed off**, and yet **documented**.

### Leadership intransigence (not the bare minimum — why trust is gone)

The **ultimatum** above is only the **floor** — the **minimum** I need to stay in the room. Everything in this section is **not** a bargaining list. I am pointing at **intransigence** by **current leadership** until the community treats it as normal.

This place has become **divisive**, **fractured**, **intimidating**, and **petty**. I do not say that for drama. I say it because the same script keeps running:

- **Twickenham** — an **entire neighborhood** **defeated** by the feud and the gate, as if one long fight could erase decades of ordinary use.  
- **Dog owners** — defeated the **exact same way**: **one incident** at the iron gate becomes the story **forever**, and a **whole class of neighbors** loses the park. I do not like that geometry. **Entire neighborhoods** — and **entire user groups** — should not be written off because leadership would rather freeze a **single bad day** than publish fair rules. **Something should be done for dog owners as well** — posted access, ordinary leash-and-cleanup norms, an end to permanent exile over one gate fight. That belongs in a healthy park culture. It is **not** part of my ultimatum; it shows why I do not trust the culture that sets the rules.  
- **Deferred repair** — it is plausible **Mary Calder Cottage**, the playground, and grounds could have been **fixed long ago** if **whole neighborhoods adjacent to Gordonston** had been **partners** instead of **alienated**. When you treat Twickenham, dog walkers, and fair-season volunteers as problems, you should not wonder why the infrastructure rots and the volunteer bench empties.

I could go on all day about gossip, who gets heard at the association table, and who is told to “petition” at a gate. I am not asking you to agree with every sentence. I am saying the **ultimatum** exists because **leadership intransigence** made the **minimum** necessary.

### The last straw (Hollywood / Facebook)

The **Hollywood** incident was the **last straw** — not the first wound, and not what this letter is mainly about. I could write at length on **taxpayer-backed police**, **traveling crews**, and **industry money** that never hits a neighborhood ledger; **I will not do that here.** What matters for this resignation is what happened **after** I spoke on **Gordonston on Facebook**:

An admin (**Jack Taylor**, on the public record) **deleted** a post about **filming and public cost** before I could finish a reply, **purged** my posts, **removed** me from a group used for **cat rescue and other programs**, and **denied** any path to **appeal** the rule he alone interpreted. His public note cited **“no politics”** and **“no personal attacks”** while admitting I was **complaining about being banned** — I had **not** attacked any neighbor before or after removal. The framing was **disingenuous**. The **most intimidating** part was not one admin: **almost no one spoke in the thread when it mattered**. Private messages of support came **after** — **after the fact does not matter**. Everyone watches. I already know I am **not crazy** and **should not be treated this way** as a neighbor.

### The intimidation factor

By **intimidation** I do not mean only loud threats. I mean what happens when people **break their own rules, their own ethics, and the norms neighbors claim to believe in** — and everyone **looks the other way**.

One example: a **hashtag campaign** was developed and posted around this dispute. Someone **messaged my wife** and asked **her** to ask **me** to remove my post. In the end I agreed — but **only** because of a **deal I made with my wife**, not because the neighborhood square had answered anything on the merits. From the outside that can also be read as me **succumbing** to pressure from people who are willing to **break the rules** to get their way. I could describe what that opens at home only as the start of a **spouse war** — pressure routed through family instead of answered in public. I will not run my household or my name through that geometry again for the sake of a Facebook group.

From the **gossip** I have already heard about this neighborhood, I can understand how that pattern is **normal here**: when it happens, **nobody calls it out**. People watch. Private messages arrive later. **After the fact does not matter.**

That is the same intimidation stack as the admin delete and the purge — **plausibly deniable**, easy to **laugh off**, still **real**. I cannot **operate** in an environment where breaking norms is routine and calling it out is optional.

I will not pretend to **operate** in a neighborhood that treats **good-faith questions** as **politics**, **censorship** as **goodwill**, **family pressure** as **consensus**, and **silence** as **peace**.

**What I am stepping back from**

- Further **volunteer labor** on the **Gordonston Art Fair website**, **ticketing**, **ads**, and **payment / financial processing** tied to the fair.  
- **Transition** support — finding vendors, training a replacement, or keeping systems warm so the committee can pretend nothing changed.  
- **Showing up** for **neighborhood programs and events** in a volunteer or organizer capacity — **ever again**, unless the ultimatum is met in substance first.

**What I am not abandoning**

- My **home** on this corridor.  
- My **daughter** — you may still see us at the park **only** to bring her there when access allows; that is **parenting**, not participation in your volunteer stack.  
- My right to speak **outside** closed groups — including the public record in [Make Gordonston Great Again](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/controlled_opposition/make-gordonston-great-again.md) and linked investigations.

If leadership later meets the **ultimatum** in **substance** — **Twickenham** and **Avondale** included on equal footing, **under-ten** ordinary use without gate fights, **Gordonston-only** posture gone, written policy, optional rebuild that reads as reconciliation — I will reconsider **cooperation**. Until then, consider this **resignation** firm.

It is **nothing personal**. I mean that. But if you **disagree** with what I have said here — about the park, the ban, the silence, the intimidation — then **maybe it is**.

Respectfully,
