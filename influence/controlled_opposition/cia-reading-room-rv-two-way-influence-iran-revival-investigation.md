## 3. What the CIA Reading Room contains that a selective collection will miss

No single folder or keyword crawl equals “everything CIA posted.”

- **CREST / 25-year bulk:** CIA’s own announcement describes on the order of **12 million pages** of declassified records posted from the CREST program (January 2017 wave), i.e. a **mass** of memos, analysis, administrative ephemera, and scanned images — far beyond any one researcher’s slice. Official story hub: [CIA: “CIA Posts More Than 12 Million Pages of CREST Records Online”](https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/cia-posts-more-than-12-million-pages-of-crest-records-online/).
- **STARGATE as a labeled collection:** Third-party guides often cite **~12,000+ discrete “documents”** in the STARGATE bucket (counts vary by how “document” is defined vs. page splits). Treat published counts as **approximate** until reconciled against CIA pagination. Example secondary catalog: [The Black Vault — Stargate collection note](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/the-stargate-collection); [IRVA Stargate guide](https://www.irva.org/stargate-guide).
- **Search surfaces are wider than one collection:** A Reading Room text search for **“remote viewing”** returns on the order of **tens of thousands of hits** (the search UI reports total items; the exact number moves with indexing). That includes **non‑STARGATE** folders, **case logs**, **studies**, **congressional**, **administrative** PDFs, etc.—so “RV language” ≠ “RV program file.”

**Redaction reality**

- Many released PDFs are **image scans**; others retain **paragraph-level redactions**. Full-text search will systematically **under-count** unless machine-readable text is recovered from scans and aligned with page images.

## 5. Hypothesis map: “two-way / affect / religious overlap” without over-claiming

### 5.1 Plausible **non-mystical** overlaps (strongest, documented-class)

- **Psychological operations** and **revolutionary politics** in the same era are massively documented independent of RV. Any overlap with “visions / signs” in public religion is often better explained by **politics + charisma + violence + media** than by RV.
- **Intelligence interest in “anomalies”** sometimes reflects **foreign assessments** (“what does adversary X believe / fear?”), not endorsement.

### 5.2 Plausible **in-program language** overlaps (medium, needs close read of released text)

- RV training materials sometimes define **targets**, **sessions**, **stages**, and occasionally **beacon** concepts (official glossary items appear in Reading Room docs). That language can **sound** like spiritual epistemology when skimmed; **close reading** must separate **metaphor / protocol** from **physics claim**. On **scanned** releases, that close read may require **transcription or scan-side text extraction**—a format problem, not a metaphysical one.

### 5.3 Weak / speculative overlaps (explicitly “clue tier”)

- **Cross-correlation** between peak RV program activity (late Cold War) and **Iran revolution / hostage crisis** timing is **chronologically possible** to *ask about*; establishing **operational co-tasking** requires **document-internal co-reference** (same memo routing, same project code, dated tasking), not keyword proximity across unrelated FOIA logs.

**Separate EM / irradiation lane (documented CIA interest, different mechanism):** the **Moscow embassy microwave “signals”** episode and U.S. follow-on programs (**PANDORA**, **BIZARRE**) concern **RF exposure**, health anxiety, and **behavioral / medical** study tracks — not the **dart gun** story. **Session setup (what the file itself records — A-tier):** method described as **sealed envelope** plus **geographic coordinates**; card in envelope: **planet Mars**, time of interest **~1 million years B.C.**, coordinates **40.89° N, 9.55° W** given verbally during the interview; transcript dated **22 May 1984**, 10:09 AM block. Earlier in the same session the subject reports **pyramid / okra-colored** forms, **dust storm**, **megalithic** surfaces, etc., before the monitor drives **“when the others left”** follow-ups that yield the **boat / volcano / plants** language.

**Mainstream / skeptical reading (“what it’s supposed to be” if not taken as literal Mars history):**

- **Document type:** A **controlled remote-viewing style interview transcript** (MON prompts, SUB free-report), preserved as a **historical FOIA release**, not a peer-reviewed planetary science paper.
- **Epistemic bar:** The text proves **the program recorded these words** under the stated protocol; it does **not** establish independent corroboration that a **million-year-old Martian civilization**, **interior of a boat**, or **that** vegetation existed at those coordinates. Standard critiques: **demand bias**, **sensory metaphor**, **narrative elaboration** under repeated monitor questions, and **no falsifiable tie** to Viking / MGS / rover ground truth at 1 Ma.
- **AIR / agency posture (see §4):** Even where lab RV work was argued anomalous, **operational intelligence utility** remained contested; treat this session as **primary for RV culture and language**, not for **solar-system fact**.

**Slide vs CIA:** The **rover panorama** and **2017 copyright** are **presentation-layer** choices by whoever composed the slide; they are **not** part of the 1984 CIA PDF. For provenance of **words only**, cite **`cia.gov/readingroom`** (or checksum a mirror against the **A** PDF).

### 7.6 USAF **Forecast II** + **Super Cockpit** — institutional “two-way” *human–machine* language (analogy, not Stargate)

**Why this belongs here (explicitly weak / cross-domain):** the investigation asks about **“two-way”** or **closed-loop** influence language around RV. In **separate**, well-documented **USAF** lines, planners and cockpit researchers discussed **multimodal, bidirectional** interfaces: pilot **feeds** the aircraft (voice, gaze, later touch/haptics in various roadmaps) and the aircraft **feeds** the pilot (helmet displays, synthetic vision, audio). That is **engineering HCI**, not a declassified claim that RV was implemented as avionics — but it is the **kind of institutional vocabulary** pop narratives sometimes **fuse** with “psychic” two-way imagery. Treat as **C-tier for the specific pull-quote** until a **primary page citation** is found; **B-tier** for the programs existing at all.

**Author-supplied excerpt (unverified wording):** a typewritten block titled **“THE SUPER COCKPIT”** attributes to **Forecast II** requirements that “machines” respond to **voice** and **eye motion**, that **man and machine** “interact to share the sense of touch,” predicts **virtual cockpit** / helmet-driven outside-world views for **operational aircraft by ~2020**, and cites **Aeronautical Systems Center** (Wright-Patterson) as embodying the concept; it ends with editorial line: **Add “thinking” to that list**.
