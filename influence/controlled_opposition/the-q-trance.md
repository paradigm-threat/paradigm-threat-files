He was born Jacob Anthony Chansley. In 2005 he legally added Angeli and became Jacob Anthony Angeli Chansley; the federal Jan 6 case was filed under that name ([azcentral](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/local/arizona-breaking/2021/01/11/jake-angeli-arizona-seen-us-capitol-raid-fur-hat-horns-federal-court-hearing/6625619002/)). This page uses Chansley — the birth surname and the last name on the docket — so the court record and the lawn stay the same person. Jake Angeli is the interview handle. Q-Anon Shaman is the media brand.

What I remember from that morning is McCarthy’s face when Chansley started screaming: big grins, like the horns were amusing. He never once denounced the Q crew or the Flat Earth booth. More on that arc under [McCarthy — staging, not sloppy headliner](#mccarthy--staging-not-sloppy-headliner) below.

One dedicated broadcast camera — FOX 10 on a tripod (FOX 10 sticker on the leg) — held Chansley as the continuous subject. Two masked FOX 10 crew faced him. Other TV crews nearby ran local interviews. Handheld phones circulated on the lawn. Chansley yelled loud enough that his voice carried over those other on-camera interviews. The lens was not just collecting horns. In the same field of view sat Republicans, mask protesters, the Daniel McCarthy campaign signs and trailer, and the Flat Earth booth from [Part I](https://paradigmthreat.net/cosmos/flat_earth/the-flat-earth-trance.md).
