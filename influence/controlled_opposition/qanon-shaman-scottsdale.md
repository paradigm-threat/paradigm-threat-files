## Q clearance — what the letter actually guards

Real **[Q clearance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q_clearance)** is a U.S. Department of Energy access category for **Restricted Data** — nuclear weapons design, fissile handling, the compartmented stack. It is not a public SIGINT channel and not a follower-facing bulletin board. The letter may be alphabetic accident. The **clearance** is not.

The load-bearing secret is not “how big the Hollywood bomb is.” It is managing a **half-hoax on Power**: training the public to believe nuclear bombs are far more powerful than they are, while **denying or burying depleted uranium** as the real proxy threat, and blocking transformative understanding of what Power would change if the lie cracked. People who manage that lid hold extraordinary operational advantage. Fiction already mirrors the grammar — Bond’s **Q Branch** as secret deployable tech the public will not see for decades; Star Trek’s **Q** as omniscient red herring who bends the plot while humans argue the wrong frame. ## Scottsdale climax — Chansley, FOX 10, and the foiled association

On **Wednesday, June 24, 2020**, at **Scottsdale City Hall**, roughly two hundred people turned out for **“Unmask Us”** — an anti-mask mandate rally, **not** Stop the Steal. **Daniel McCarthy**, then a GOP Senate hopeful, spoke and framed the moment as resistance to **communist insurrection** ([azcentral](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/local/scottsdale/2020/06/24/scottsdale-councilman-leads-protest-against-mask-mandates/3245724001/)). After he finished, **Jacob Chansley** — the man later branded the **QAnon Shaman** — emerged from the side of the grounds onto an **empty grass patch** the crowd had not filled. Shirtless. Horned headdress. Face paint. A spear sign: **“Q SENT ME.”** He never joined the main crowd. He staged a second act.

**One dedicated broadcast camera — FOX 10** on a tripod (**FOX 10 sticker on the leg**) held Chansley as the continuous subject. Two masked FOX 10 crew faced him. Other TV crews nearby ran **local interviews**. Handheld phones circulated on the lawn. Chansley yelled loud enough that his voice carried over those other on-camera interviews. The lens was not just collecting horns. In the same field of view sat **Republicans**, **mask protesters**, the **Daniel McCarthy** campaign signs and trailer, and the **Flat Earth** booth from [Part I](https://paradigmthreat.net/cosmos/flat_earth/the-flat-earth-trance.md). They wanted a package that welded **QAnon** onto McCarthy’s protest. That is what I saw on the lawn, and it is what the video still shows.

I walked up — too close, in front of that FOX 10 lens — to spoil that association, not merely to interrupt a take. First I signaled to the camera that he was crazy. Then I pointed at him and yelled **“Paid! Paid! Paid! money, money, money!”** He answered my Sons of Anarchy shirt. I did not answer when he told me to get out. I walked back toward Q supporters and pointed at the Flat Earth PVC booth — **they’re paid too**. At the end of my roll, the **same FOX 10 operator turns the camera away and packs it up** while Chansley is **still ranting**. They did not wait for him to finish. Whatever they were trying to air from that staging was **foiled**. I did not know the station was FOX 10 until years later, reading a partially covered badge in frame on re-analysis. The Short is that first confrontation. Frame grabs, dialogue transcript, and claim tiers live in the [Phoenix protest timeline investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/constitution/investigations/phoenix-protest-timeline-investigation.md).

### After the Short — second exchange, normal crowd, McCarthy sign

What happened next is still mostly **memory**. Other rolls from that morning may hold it; I have not finished reviewing them. After the FOX 10 take died, Flat Earthers and Q people **surrounded Chansley**. Everyone started yelling. Other protesters yelled back. It got chaotic. A lot of the Q group looked at me and tried to pull me into argument.

I went back and engaged Chansley a **second** time. I am not sure of my exact opening line. I remember saying I wanted to hear his **Q** — and he seemed confused, as if *why bother*. He asked for something soft, a caressing kind of ask. The regular protesters took **my** statement seriously instead. They looked at him. He seemed to realize the room had shifted. Then he started ranting again. He said the **CIA** and something else had **traveled to the past** and could do something there. He named **time travel** specifically. I said I do **not** believe in time travel. He turned away, walked straight into the middle of the crowd, and the lawn erupted again.

That is when I noticed the headcount. There were **far more normal protesters** than Q or Flat Earth people — and the poison-pill crews looked like they were **dispersing quickly**. A small crowd of people **not** with Flat Earth or Q surrounded me. That is how I know the plaza was mesmerized by the adjacent theater: they asked how I knew he was wrong about **pedophiles in Congress**. I said I **absolutely agree** there are pedophiles in Congress and running the show. I do not doubt that. Their tone changed completely. They asked what **I** believed.

I was not prepared. Back then I had barely done the research it would take to say what I can say now. So I said I was totally **pagan**, and did not go deep — a little about history being a lie. People seemed interested. It sort of fizzled. Someone from the campaign handed me a **McCarthy sign** after I said I never got one. It ended in good vibes. Some of them appreciated that I had called Chansley out. It was clear he was **ruining the legitimacy** of the protest, and they were glad someone roughly their age and demographic would challenge him — and say that **some** of what he named can be true without swallowing time travel or the QAnon package. The real issue for that lawn was the **mandates**, the deep state, the attempt to control everyone. That is what I agreed with. I made clear I did not believe the time-travel story or the rest of the QAnon theater. I took the McCarthy sign, got on my bike, and went home.

McCarthy kept sharing the post-election circuit with Chansley through **November and December 2020** — Capitol, MCTEC, Executive Tower — without cleaning poison pills from rooms he headlined. That is why the stage hygiene reads as controlled opposition: not because every fraud claim was false, but because adjacent self-defeat was never expelled.
