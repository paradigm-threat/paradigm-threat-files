## problems

- **Shuman Elementary closed** — Most families commute farther; walk-to-Goebel mornings are gone.
- **Triple vacancy** — Closed school, idle church classrooms, locked Juliette Gordon Low Park.
- **2011 park feud never finished** — Residents Only lingered; no written children's access policy.
- **Twickenham children isolated** — Banished at the gate; the field and walk they knew are gone.
- **District strain on reading and writing** — SCCPSS still below state on core subjects; study hall is extra help, not a new school.

## solutions

- **Volunteer homework campus** at Christ's Community (1805 E. Gwinnett) on open weekdays — secular schoolwork only.
- **100% compatible with host worship** — No counter-proselytizing; Sunday services unchanged.
- **Free for Gordonston, Twickenham, and nearby blocks** — Pilot small: one age band, one weekday.
- **Park reconciliation** — Posted hours, head count, Twickenham and Gordonston kids together under clear rules.
- **Act as a community** — Discovery phase; tell us what you think before any formal pitch.
