# Minako Hamano — R&D1 craft, platform rupture, and credit erasure

> **TL;DR:** Minako Hamano (joined Nintendo R&D1 **1991**) is the investigative center: co-composer of *Super Metroid*, *Link's Awakening*, handheld Metroid, and later *DKC Returns* / *Tropical Freeze* / *Prime* era. Load-bearing case: **Mine Menace** (Returns, Yamamoto on stream) → four TF rocket arrangements credited **David Wise** without “arrangement” on streaming — **slippery “return of Dave Wise”** marketing on Kyoto rearrangements. **Author thesis:** Wise = British IP **stakeholder/actor**, not composer; interviews = whitewash. This dossier holds **documented facts**, **author sentiment** (consolidated below), and **falsifiers**. Surround: **1988–1996 platform rupture**, CD-i without R&D, Mario RPG theater. **Open speculation:** Mine Menace loop ↔ *Aladdin* “Friend Like Me” / Robin Williams displacement era. **Not claimed:** Hamano → *FF7*.

**Status:** Open — author sentiment consolidated (2026-09-01); evidence targets listed for follow-up.

## Author sentiment — consolidated (session)

> Everything below is **author position** unless tagged **documented** or **observed**. It governs how this dossier reads the Returns / Tropical Freeze / Mine Menace chain and the Wise stakeholder thesis.

**Epistemic stance**

- Video game music credits are **press releases**, not receipts. Streaming metadata, supervisor lines, and marketing copy are **products** of the same system under investigation — including industry histories that treat David Wise as Rare’s programmer-composer (**author rejects those interviews as whitewash**, not counter-evidence).
- [**Prisca sapientia**](/docs/PRISCA_SAPIENTIA.md): institutions and consensus histories are not default truth when credits and corporate narratives are the subject.

**Center and surround**

- **Minako Hamano** (R&D1 from **1991**) is the person; the **1988–1996 platform rupture** (Sony royalty fight, Philips pivot, SNES CD death, CD-i licenses without R&D, Mario RPG farewell theater, N64 cartridge bet) is the **crime scene** — without it, specific authorship claims have no motive structure.
- R&D1 **mixed-gender programmer-musician** craft was orphaned when the industry chose a **2D→3D hard reset** over incremental SNES + CD + coprocessor evolution (DKC already proved pre-render on stock hardware).

**David Wise — stakeholder thesis (author charge)**

- Wise is **not** a composer, programmer, or musician in the sense used for people who built the music. He is a **British IP holder / stakeholder / public actor** whose function is to **receive credit** for work built by the Kyoto pipeline (Yamamoto, Hamano, Tajima, et al.).
- Hex/assembly interviews are **cover story** — written completely enough that journalists repeat them without asking who was in the room when the cartridge shipped.
- On **Returns** he was **not on the project** (left Rare 2009). On **Tropical Freeze** he was **pulled in at the end** for the “legend returns” arc — **QA at most** (listen, comment, approve); name maximized on poster, hands minimized in production.
- SNES-era “Wise composed DKC” on paper = **same whitewash system** extended backward; not treated here as independent proof against the stakeholder read.

**Returns → Tropical Freeze — load-bearing case**

| Layer | Returns (2010) | Tropical Freeze (2014) |
|-------|----------------|------------------------|
| Hamano | **Music** team | **Music recording** (infrastructure, not authorship) |
| Yamamoto | Music **supervisor**; **Mine Menace** on streaming | Supervisor again |

| Sound | Mostly SNES rearrangements + new writing; Hamano **originals** on Club Nintendo tracks **3** & **8** | Live instruments; author hears **sloppier timing** vs programmed SNES myth — skill-set mismatch with stakeholder legend |

**Provable credit fraud (documented metadata)**

- **Mine Menace** (Returns rocket theme): streaming credits **Kenji Yamamoto**.
- **Four** TF rocket-barrel levels — **Rodent Ruckus**, **Twilight Terror**, **Reckless Ride**, **Blurry Flurry** — are **arrangements** of Mine Menace ([Mario Wiki](https://www.mariowiki.com/Mine_Menace)); Wise 2018 post admits **remixes**, plural.
- **Streaming apps credit David Wise** without “arrangement” or Returns lineage — qualification lives in **wiki footnotes / interviews** only. Literally have to dig to learn he did not compose the underlying theme.
- **Slippery by design:** surface supports *legendary composer returns*; fine print admits rearrangement of Kyoto’s prior cue. **Return of Dave Wise** sold on shallow credit.

**Forensic ear observations (author + others)**

- All four TF variations preserve Returns **style, timing, phrasing, attack** — reads as **one hand** extended the sketch, not Wise mimicking Yamamoto after a decade away. **Simple:** he was not original author or rearranger. **Complicated (whitewash):** Wise as chameleon genius — Occam favors authorship continuity.
- Toward **end of Mine Menace loop**, brass/rhythm shift resembles **“Friend Like Me”** (*Aladdin* / Genie / Robin Williams) — **atypical** for R&D1 composers; [independent listener, Patreon 2017](https://kemono.cr/patreon/user/331733/post/15833388). No official acknowledgment.

**Open speculation (gut feeling — not proven)**

- Theme with possible Genie echo → four Wise-credited sequel variations → credit hop may sit in same **cultural weather** as **Robin Williams / Genie displacement**: Disney contract fight; post-death voice restrictions; [Spotify users reporting missing Williams Genie tracks](https://community.spotify.com/t5/Content-Questions/Aladdin-Sndtrk-missing-tracks/td-p/111658); 2019 Will Smith remake dominating **search path** so younger listeners meet Genie **second**. TF shipped **Feb 2014**; Williams died **Aug 2014** — not post-death composition, but **displacement era**.
- **Mine Menace** should be examined with **Hamano in the room**; Yamamoto holds streaming tag; no primary proof she wrote it yet.
- **Hamano prejudice conjecture:** Japanese women not treated fairly by Japanese men in industry; she beat it with skill inside R&D but could not prove herself to Western press that never learned her name. **Returns safe** (understated success, supervisor names safe). **Freeze threat** — Hamano composer credit on new originals would crack Wise return + Yamamoto supervisor stories simultaneously.

- Hamano ghostwrote *Final Fantasy VII* (negative-evidence baseline only elsewhere).

## 1. Investigation thesis

**Center:** Minako Hamano represents a **Nintendo R&D1 sound culture** — small teams, programmer-musicians, mixed-gender co-authorship, techniques passed over years (Tanaka → Totaka; Yamamoto ↔ Hamano).

**Surround (required context):** From **1988**, Nintendo's CD-ROM deals threatened **software licensing monopoly** — the core of Yamauchi's business. The **1991 Sony rupture**, **1993 Philips SNES CD death**, **CD-i settlement games**, **1996 Mario RPG + FF7 platform switch**, and **N64 cartridge bet** form one continuous **control vs. craft** crisis. When corporations fight over **who taxes every game sold**, **labor attribution** becomes expendable.

**Investigative premise:** Public history (interviews, fan canon, press "returns") **over-credits safe names** and **under-credits or erases** R&D1-lineage composers — Hamano first. **David Wise** on Returns/Tropical Freeze is the load-bearing example of **stakeholder credit** (British IP face on Kyoto craft). Pattern also includes Eveline Fischer/Novakovic, credit opacity at Rare, Square/Nintendo theater.

**Author rejects as rebuttal:** Wise hex/assembly interviews and industry “programmer-musician” canon — treated as **whitewash product** within the same credit system (see § Author sentiment).

**Not claimed as proven here:** Hamano wrote uncredited music for *Final Fantasy VII*; Square reused Nintendo R&D archives for *FF8*/*FF9*; forensic proof that Wise never touched a sound driver (stakeholder thesis is **author charge**, not ROM-proven).

## 5. Precedent cases

### 5.1 Philips CD-i — IP without craft

- Contract: character licenses after SNES CD failure ([Game Informer / Radosh](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2016/12/29/meet-the-man-who-put-mario-and-zelda-on-the-philips-cd_2d00_i.aspx)).
- Developed by **Philips / US studios** (~$600k budgets); Nintendo input = **character bibles** only.
- **Result:** "Nintendo from another planet" — wrong visuals, audio, timing; infamous quality.
- **Investigative meaning:** First mass-visible proof of **Nintendo characters minus Nintendo R&D** — same class of separation author alleges for music labor.

### 5.2 Super Mario RPG — diplomatic finale

- Square-led: Shimomura (music), Fujioka (director); Nintendo: Miyamoto, Takahashi (CG), Kondo special thanks.
- **No R&D1 composers** (Hamano, Yamamoto) on credits.
- Shipped **as FF7 platform switch went public** — "one big happy family" during rupture.
- See also: [nintendo-sony-breakup.md](../nintendo-sony-breakup.md) §9.

### 5.3 SNES CD cancellations — amputated projects

- *Secret of Mana*, *Chrono Trigger*: CD scope → cartridge cuts ([Kotaku SNES-CD](https://kotaku.com/the-weird-history-of-the-super-nes-cd-rom-nintendos-mo-1828860861)).
- **Forensic target:** stranded music/assets when format died.

### 5.4 Rare / missing credits — suppression; Wise industry record

- Battletoads, most Rare NES: **no in-game credits** — anti-poaching policy ([VGMPF Rare](https://www.vgmpf.com/Wiki/index.php?title=Rare)).
- Recovered credits name **David Wise**; industry interviews describe hex/assembly workflow ([VGMO interview](https://vgmonline.net/davidwiseinterview/)).
- **Author read:** Missing credits prove **policy**. Industry Wise-as-programmer canon = **same whitewash system** under examination on Returns/Freeze — not independent disproof of stakeholder thesis.
- See: [rare-nintendo-rd.md](../rare-nintendo-rd.md), [dave-wise.md](../dave-wise.md).

### 5.5 Incremental 2D path abandoned

- **Super FX** (1993): cartridge 3D on SNES.
- **DKC** (1994): pre-rendered SGI → SNES sprites; no extra chip.
- **SNES CD** (1993 spec): SCCP + HANDS — killed when N64 chosen.
- **Author claim:** Nintendo could have evolved SNES + CD + coprocessors instead of **hard 3D reset**; home-console **2D craft lineage** treated as obsolete for platform marketing.

### 5.6 Credit erasure (general)

- Nintendo Music app: no composer credits (industry reporting).
- Grant Kirkhope: Nintendo often omits composer credit on owned music.
- Kyoto Report: recovery of omitted Nintendo personnel credits.
- See: [vgm-preservation-credits.md](../vgm-preservation-credits.md).
