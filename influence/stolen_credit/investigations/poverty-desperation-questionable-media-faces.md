## Pattern (author)

1. **Exit pressure:** factory / retail / college dropout / blocked legitimate craft path — not romantic muse poverty.
2. **Offer arrives:** job, fame noun, poster seat, “legend” marketing — often from people who already control IP or credit systems.
3. **Questionable media role:** take or keep **composition / authorship nouns** for labor built elsewhere; give whitewash interviews; sell inflated discography; narrate “clarifications” that lock safer binaries.
4. **Independent craft shelf stays thin** (optional but strong): empty non-game catalog, quality break when name alone must carry a score, reliance on teams / remasters / rearrangements.

**Swift rail (methodology parallel):** Desperation explains **what gets published under what name**, not **novel insight** (“broke → bolt of genius”). Same psychology, different century.
