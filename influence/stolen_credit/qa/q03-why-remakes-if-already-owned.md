# Q3 — Why remakes if we already paid?

**Question:** Why ship Pixel Remasters and Nintendo Switch Online if people already bought the cartridges?

## Answer

The law does not require a remake to keep a copyright alive. Silence does not put *Final Fantasy VI* in the public domain. A current store page is still useful. Fans expect a copy they can run on a machine they own now. A title that is locked and not for sale is also harder to defend in Congress and in the rule that lets libraries break a lock only when a game is no longer reasonably available.

Switch Online is a subscription. The company’s own FAQ says there are no plans to sell those classics outside the membership. Stop paying and the games go away. That is not the same bargain as a 1985 cartridge.
