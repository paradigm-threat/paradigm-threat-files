# Q10 — What is the leftover political divider?

**Question:** If this is already a political attack, what is left to argue?

## Answer

Not whether the pattern can lead to corporations controlling information and agency. That outcome is already the present. Games, movies, and the leftover public domain already run that way. Ordinary people do not compete with the official substitute. They rent it.

The leftover divider is only whether that outcome is good. One side wants more of the corporation’s vision: official remakes, official rental catalogs, official crossovers, and silence from anyone still attached to the first version. The other side is a political stand against the enclosure: after a limited time the old work should become material other people may use, including people who loved the original and hated the substitute. There is no third position that still tells the truth about the remakes.
