**Question:** Even if old classical workshops were messy, wasn’t game audio a clean technical pipeline — MIDI in, SPC/NSF out — with no real appropriation fights?

## Answer

The same incentives that produced classical-era erasure existed in studio game audio. Later high-profile fraud exposures in game music show that the “nobody with a real claim was silenced” assumption was never safe.
