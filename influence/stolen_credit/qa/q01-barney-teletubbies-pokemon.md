# Q1 — Why Barney, Teletubbies, and Pokémon?

**Question:** Why does this site talk about a Barney generation, a Teletubbies generation, and a Pokémon generation?

## Answer

Those are the first major influences for a lot of kids born in the 1990s, and for many born late in the 1980s. *Barney & Friends* reached American public television in 1992. *Teletubbies* first aired on BBC Two on 31 March 1997. *Pokémon* was a Game Boy game in Japan in 1996 and an American cartoon in 1998.

People who had already lived through the Nintendo years of the Famicom, NES, and Super NES use those names as a date, not as a joke about taste. The later kids missed the company that could still ship a finished world. They met the brand after it had already split.
