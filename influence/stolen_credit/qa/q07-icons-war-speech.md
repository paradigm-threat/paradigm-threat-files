# Q7 — Icons, war, and speech

**Question:** What did Disney’s mouse and the 2026 brick-look videos have to do with speech?

## Answer

A childhood face is a way to talk to people who already trust the face. Disney could put Donald Duck in a Nazi factory and walk a German boy through Hitler Youth schooling in 1943 because Disney held the face. In 2026, viral videos that looked like LEGO minifigs carried a war message about Iran and the United States. Those were lookalikes, not a LEGO Group film. They still worked because children know the bricks.

Santa and Lincoln are the other kind of face: anyone may use them. Mario is not. Remakes and crossovers keep the known picture inside a company even after one old drawing expires. Exclusive speech through icons is the same enclosure as sitting on a game world. Ordinary people never get the competing vision.
