# Q11 — Is the VGM MIDI hobby preservation or compliance?

**Question:** Isn’t collecting and sharing game MIDI files real preservation — a fan museum for the soundtracks we love?

## Answer

**Pro-MIDI / pro-industry position:** The finished commercial soundtrack is sacred material. Fans who archive MIDI, make covers, and treat the OST as museum stock are doing preservation. Criticism of industry credit or interchange standards is raining on a hobby that keeps the music alive.

**Counter:** That posture is the illusion the industry built. Treating the finished product as untouchable, accepting non-competitive standards the industry wants, and offering zero structural criticism of credit faces is **compliance**, not craft transfer. The next generation does not inherit competitive chip composition — it inherits permission to buy software, hardware, and training, and to stay fans of institutional nouns. Open music-editor / competitive SPC–NSF craft stays buried while MIDI-as-gospel is defended.
