# Q9 — Isn’t this just raining on a parade?

**Question:** Doesn’t this just sound like an old fan who will never be happy, spoiling launch week?

## Answer

That is what the case looks like when it is broken into pieces. One shoddy remake is a store page. One missing port is a business choice. One motion lock is a gimmick. One generation label is rudeness. Isolated, each complaint rains on someone else’s good time.

The complete case has to be made at once. The stacked pattern is a holder locking a finished world, shipping a lesser official copy, splitting the audience, and refusing every other vision that was already latent in the first version. That is not a mood. It is enclosure. Other models will defend each piece as ordinary commerce. This reading does not answer in pieces.
