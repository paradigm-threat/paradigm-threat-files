# Q13 — Didn’t composer credit always work this way?

**Question:** For centuries the named composer got the credit while assistants, copyists, and workshop hands disappeared — Beethoven, Mozart, Gershwin. Why is video game music different?

## Answer

**Pro-MIDI / pro-industry position (industry-standard credit defense):** Hierarchies of credit are normal cultural history. The person who originated the idea or held the institutional seat is the composer; subordinates finish the notes. Applying that model to game audio is continuity, not fraud.

**Counter:** The historical pattern is real — and documented on this site for classical cases. It is still **unacceptable for 8-/16-bit VGM** for two practical reasons: (1) the work fans attached to is the **chip performance**, not a theme sheet; whoever programmed the final data shaped childhood memory; (2) commercial products sold to kids and teens create a legitimate demand to know which humans actually made those sounds, not only which noun the studio parked. Preserving an older erasure convention is not a moral upgrade; it substitutes institutional marketing for mentors.
