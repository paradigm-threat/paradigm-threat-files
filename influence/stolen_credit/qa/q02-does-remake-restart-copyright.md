# Q2 — Does a remake restart the copyright?

**Question:** Did the Pixel Remaster or a Switch port restart Nintendo’s or Square’s copyright on the old game?

## Answer

No. A new edition copyrights only what is new: redrawn sprites, a new recording, new code. The 1987 *Final Fantasy* keeps 1987. The 1985 *Super Mario Bros.* keeps 1985. Under the current US corporate term those clocks still run into the 2080s. Shipping the game again on a store does not add a day to the original.

What the remake does is occupy the only legal storefront and answer the question “where do I buy this?” The dates and the 1998 term extension are in the [IP enclosure notes](/influence/stolen_credit/investigations/ip-public-domain-enclosure-nintendo-disney-investigation.md). The plain-speech version is in [Before Barney, Teletubbies, and Pokémon](/influence/stolen_credit/before-barney-and-pokemon.md).
