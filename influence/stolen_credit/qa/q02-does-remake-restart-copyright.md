# Q2 — Did Nintendo shove the clock backward like Disney?

**Question:** Did Nintendo arbitrarily push its games backward the way Disney’s mouse campaign may have moved a cliff? Does a remake restart the copyright?

## Answer

No on both, and that is not a defense of Nintendo. In 1998 Disney was staring at a 1928 mouse about to become public speech. That company led the twenty-year hike on works already made. Mario in 1985 and *Final Fantasy* in 1987 were nowhere near that cliff. Nobody had to invent a named rescue for those titles. A Pixel Remaster still does not add a day to 1987. New art and new code get a new copyright. The original keeps its year.

The ordinary model stops there. The case here is that the remakes are a different machine from the Mickey bill. They do not have to move the date. They have to exist so the vault stays decent-looking. Substandard, divisive quality is not a failure of that machine. It is the proof the companies cannot sit silent.
