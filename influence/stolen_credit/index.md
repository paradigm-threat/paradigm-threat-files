# Stolen Credit — Investigation

**Thesis:** Plagiarism and misattribution in 20th-century media function as a control mechanism. One objective: consolidate world fame into a small number of controlled personalities. Creators whose work was stolen often vanish from public view during or shortly after their fame (Beethoven, Mozart, Bruce Lee, etc.).

**Focus (this folder):** Video game musicians and composers; classical composers (Mozart, Gershwin, Beethoven); known plagiarists; potential credit theft from institutional creators; systemic patterns (British vassalship over Japan, erasure of women).

## Cross-Cutting Themes

- **British vassalship over Japan** — Post-imperial structures; credit flowing to British/Western figures
- **Erasure of women** — Minako Hamano, Eveline Fischer, Nannerl Mozart, Fanny Mendelssohn, Kay Swift
- **Institutional vs. individual credit** — Nintendo R&D, Square in-house; contractors taking public fame
- **Nintendo seal / publisher quality** — Square and Rare peak during Nintendo partnership; decline after leaving (Square→PlayStation, Rare→Microsoft)
- **Loyalty tokens** — Jeremy Soule's reported demand (gospel symphony) as gatekeeping
- **Games without credits** — VGMPF documents **335+ games** with no or incomplete credits; Rare among offenders
