# Minako Hamano — Investigation

> **TL;DR:** Minako Hamano (R&D1, from **1991**) co-authored *Super Metroid*'s most iconic tracks and anchors a wider probe into **Nintendo craft vs. platform rupture** (Sony/Philips 1988–1996, credit erasure, 2D→3D cutoff). **Load-bearing case:** [Mine Menace → Tropical Freeze credit hop](minako-hamano-and-the-whitewash.md). **Traditions + book drought + contacts:** [investigations/nintendo-rd-traditions-reverse-engineering.md](investigations/nintendo-rd-traditions-reverse-engineering.md). **IP Great Depression / scarcity:** [investigations/minako-hamano-investigation.md](investigations/minako-hamano-investigation.md) §8c. **Full dossier:** [investigations/minako-hamano-investigation.md](investigations/minako-hamano-investigation.md).

**Status:** Investigating. Hypothesis: She may be the primary creative force behind much of the music she's credited on; credit may have been stolen by collaborators or institutional bias. When she is properly involved, quality is high; when she is sidelined or credited without actually composing, the result is amateurish.

**Corpus limits (2026-09-03):** Sheff + Altice local — **Hamano absent**. No English book found on internal credit/IP disputes. **Only** public personal contact for R&D-line sound: Tanaka (`chiptanaka.official@gmail.com`). Hamano/Yamamoto/Sakamoto — no public email.

## Investigation Angles

### 1. Credit Allocation
- **Thesis:** Hamano may have been the "real soul" behind music attributed to collaborations.
- Yamamoto, Fujiwara, Tajima are male; she is female and Japanese.
- **Action:** Compare tracks by project; melodic/thematic analysis; who wrote which themes.

### 2. Female Erasure
- Few female composers in public recognition (Hamano, Eveline Fischer, etc.).
- **Pattern:** Women's contributions minimized or subsumed under male collaborators.
- **Two-tier read (author):** Japan’s sōgōshoku (career) vs ippanshoku (support) — maps to Hamano’s **music → music recording**, Wise/Yamamoto composition nouns. Super Metroid voice = early power tell.
- **Three-tier read (author):** British men (Wise) → British women (Fischer) → Asian craft (Hamano expendable). Author feels Fischer holds public DK-female credit in Hamano’s lineage — open; not same SNES cart. See dossier §5.9; [eveline-fischer.md](eveline-fischer.md).
- **Action:** Compare tracks by project; public noun matrix; Fischer vs Hamano franchise memory.

### 3. Japanese / Post-Imperial Erasure
- **Thesis:** British and post-imperial Japanese attitudes allowed credit to flow away from Japanese creators.
- Rare (British) developed Nintendo IP; Dave Wise (British) gets primary credit on DKC.
- Hamano worked on DKC — her role vs. Wise/Beanland?
- **Action:** Trace credit in Nintendo-published materials; Japanese vs. Western bylines.

### 3b. Nintendo’s own credit controversies (documented)
- **“Nintendo Sound Team”** umbrella buries individual composers in marketing ([example discussion](https://x.com/SebastianWolff/status/2088839426868924627)).
- **Nintendo Music app** shipped without individual composer credits — industry backlash.
- ***DKC Returns HD*** omitted **individual** original *Returns* staff — **including Minako Hamano** and the rest of the original **composer / development** teams — under Nintendo corporate policy; generic “original development staff” only ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/original-donkey-kong-country-returns-devs-omitted-from-hd-remaster-s-credits); [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/donkey-kong-country-returns-hd-removes-retro-studios-staff-from-credits)). **Kenji Yamamoto** still listed as **Project Manager**, not music ([Mario Wiki HD staff](https://www.mariowiki.com/List_of_Donkey_Kong_Country_Returns_HD_staff)). Nintendo “proper credit” non-answer: [Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/nintendo-responds-to-donkey-kong-credits-furore-says-it-values-contributions-that-all-staff-make). Dossier claims 31–32 / §5.6–§5.6c.
- **Not** the 2011 Toei/DBZ Kenji Yamamoto plagiarism case — different person (山本健司 ≠ Nintendo 山本健誌). See dossier §5.6–§5.6b.

### 4. Super Metroid Voice Work Incident
- Hamano was to provide voice for Samus in Game Over; Yamamoto removed it as "too sexual."
- **Interpretation:** Male colleague overrode her creative contribution; pattern of marginalization.
- 2021: Dataminers found possible original recording in ROM.

## Supporting Evidence (Deep Dive)

### Track Attribution — Hamano Composed the Iconic Themes
- **Wrecked Ship, Maridia (both themes), Kraid, Mother Brain, Ridley's battle music, Ending credits** — all attributed to Hamano.
- **Ridley's theme:** "Became a staple of both the Pirate himself and the series"; "defined both the character and the series."
- **Lower Maridia:** Harmonically complex (D♯ Minor); analyzed on Hooktheory.
- **Metroid Fusion:** She "led the composition"; fans cite it as "absolute masterpiece," "most terrifying experience."

### Community Recognition — "Overshadowed" / "Don't Get the Credit"
- **Shinesparkers 50th Birthday Tribute (2019):**
  - Darren: "I feel she has sadly been **overshadowed by other composers** who have worked alongside her."
  - RoyboyX: "you **don't get nearly the amount of credit you deserve**."
  - Renan: "Super Metroid's score is **often credited only to Kenji Yamamoto**, but it would be careless to ignore the important contributions made to it by Minako Hamano."
  - Amanda VanHiel: Her compositions "added to the intimidation factor" when "graphics were more limited."
- Fans repeatedly cite Maridia, Mother Brain, Ridley, Fusion's Sector 1/SA-X as defining work — all Hamano.

### Kenji Yamamoto — Sole-Credit Pattern / manager seat
- Super Metroid soundtrack "often credited only to Kenji Yamamoto" despite Hamano's major contributions.
- Yamamoto composed (paper): Theme of Super Metroid, Norfair; overlap with Hamano on some areas (Discogs lists both for Maridia, Kraid, Wrecked Ship — attribution varies by source).
- Hamano led Metroid Fusion; later moved to supervisor role (Samus Returns).
- **Author harden (claim 32):** Yamamoto’s durable noun is **manager / supervisor** (Prime Music credit → Returns Music Supervisor → *Returns HD* **Project Manager**). *Returns* final boss ≈ Prime boss language → invert historian read: **Hamano** wrote the fight book across platforms; he held the seat. Who learned the hardware and composed vs who supervised.

### Donkey Kong Country Returns & Tropical Freeze — The Pattern
- **DKC Returns (2010):** Hamano on **music** team; Yamamoto supervisor; Wise **not on project** (left Rare 2009). Mostly SNES rearrangements + Hamano originals on Club Nintendo album. **Mine Menace** credited **Yamamoto** on streaming — rocket-barrel theme that became the series’ signature new action cue.
- **DKC Tropical Freeze (2014):** Wise back under **Music**; press = “return of David Wise.” Hamano demoted **music → music recording**. Four rocket-barrel tracks are **arrangements of Mine Menace**; streaming credits **Wise** without “arrangement.” Author thesis: Wise = stakeholder credit front; Kyoto pipeline wrote original + variations. **Full argument:** [minako-hamano-and-the-whitewash.md](minako-hamano-and-the-whitewash.md).

### Metroid Prime 4 — "Something Is Up"
- Metroid Prime 4: Beyond — **unmitigated disaster**; everyone agrees.
- Hamano **credited this time** — but there is **no sign she actually worked on any songs**.
- The songs are **incredibly amateurish** once again.
- **Interpretation:** When Hamano is properly on a project, quality is high (Returns, Tropical Freeze). When she is credited but not composing (Prime 4?), the result collapses. The pattern suggests she is the creative force — and that her presence vs. absence in the actual composition process explains the quality divide. Something is up.

### Female Composer Underrepresentation
- GameSoundCon: ~7% of game music sector are women; 10:1 male-to-female ratio.
- Two-year pay gap: woman with 8 years experience earns same as man with 6.
