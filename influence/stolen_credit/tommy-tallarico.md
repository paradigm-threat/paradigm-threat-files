## Documented Cases

### Earthworm Jim 1 & 2 (Tony Bernetich)
- **Claim:** Tommy Tallarico took credit for composing Earthworm Jim 1 and 2.
- **Reality:** Tony Bernetich created multiple tracks for Earthworm Jim 1 and **almost every track** for Earthworm Jim 2.
- **Additional:** Tallarico reused MIDI files from the internet (e.g. Edward Grant's 1989 Moonlight Sonata transcriptions) with minor edits (speed changes).
- **Source:** Tony Bernetich, [blog post](https://tommytallarico.wordpress.com/2023/11/03/tommy-tallarico-took-credit-for-music-created-by-tony-bernetich-and-reused-midi-files-from-the-internet-for-earthworm-jim-1-and-2/)

### Video Games Live (Laura Intravia)
- **Claim:** Laura Intravia created the Zelda 25th Anniversary Medley for VGL.
- **Reality:** Her compositions were distributed to orchestras without consent; flute solo transcribed by ear without authorization.
- **Source:** Laura Intravia public statements.
