# Eveline Fischer — Investigation

**Status:** Investigating. **Industry record:** majority of SNES *DKC3* + 7 tracks on *DKC1*; under-credited vs Wise in popular memory. **Author thesis (updated):** Neither Fischer nor Wise is the true composer of *DKC3* — British names as **credit fronts**; SNES *DKC3* OST **ears like Battletoads and other “Dave Wise”–tagged Rare material** (same stakeholder-style pipeline). Fischer still sits **tier 2** above Hamano in Western DK canon ([Hamano dossier §5.9](investigations/minako-hamano-investigation.md)).

---

## Career Summary

- **British** composer (Christchurch, Dorset, b. 1969).
- Joined Rare 1993.
- Piano, organ, violin; advanced degrees in music composition.
- Also: voice acting — Joanna Dark in Perfect Dark.

## Supporting Evidence (Deep Dive)

### DKC3 SNES — published bill vs author ear

**Industry / fan record:** Fischer composed “the vast majority” of SNES *DKC3*; Wise additional. Notable billed tracks: "Rockface Rumble," "Nuts and Bolts," "Water World," "Mill Fever."

**Author ear / stakeholder read:**

- SNES *DKC3* **sounds continuous with Battletoads and other material tagged to “Dave Wise”** — not like an independent Fischer voice separate from that Rare credit machine.
- Author does **not** believe Fischer was the composer; also does **not** believe Wise was the hands-on composer (Wise had little/no composition noun on SNES *DKC3* “majority” bill, yet the sonic family matches the same whitewashed Rare pipeline).
- Same class as Returns/Freeze: **British names on Kyoto-or-hidden craft**; published split (Fischer majority / Wise additional) is the **press-release layer**, not the authorship room.

### DKC3 Reception vs. DKC1/2

- DKC3 soundtrack "received less acclaim than the first two games."
- Individual billed Fischer tracks were praised in some writeups.
- **Author:** lower profile does not prove Fischer authorship; it can mean the **front** changed while the pipeline stayed opaque.

### DKC3 GBA (2005) — Contrast under Wise’s exclusive name

- Completely different soundtrack from SNES *DKC3*; credited **exclusively to Dave Wise**.
- **Highly divisive;** many fans say it falls far below standards associated with “Wise masterpieces” like *DKC2* ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KGFIdVa_6mg); [Wired Fish Network soapbox](https://thewiredfishnetwork.com/2011/11/05/i-stand-on-my-soapbox-the-dkc3-soundtracks/)).
- **Author read:** When the Wise **name** is forced to carry a score without the usual hidden pipeline / SNES-era cover story, quality and style **break** — consistent with stakeholder thesis on [dave-wise.md](dave-wise.md). SNES *DKC3* “Fischer” bill and GBA “Wise” bill are two faces of the same credit system, not proof either wrote the beloved material.

### Copyright — Four-Way Split on Aquatic Ambience

- "Aquatic Ambience" (iconic DKC1 track) copyright lists: **Beanland, Fischer, Wise, Kaneoka**.
- Fischer appears on the most famous DKC track's legal attribution — but public discourse centers on Wise.
- Kaneoka (Nintendo) on the copyright = Japanese presence on the legal stack — supports cross-company opacity ([dave-wise.md](dave-wise.md)).

### Female Erasure / three-tier

- Industry: ~7% women in game music; 10:1 male-to-female ratio.
- Fischer under-credited vs Wise in popular memory — yet still the **nameable British woman** on DK music vs Kyoto Hamano.
- Parent: [minako-hamano-investigation.md](investigations/minako-hamano-investigation.md) §5.9 — British men → British women → Asian craft. Author feels Fischer holds public DK-female credit in Hamano’s lineage (open; not same SNES cart).
