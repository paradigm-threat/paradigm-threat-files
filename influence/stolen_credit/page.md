# Stolen Credit — Plagiarism as Control

Plagiarism and misattribution in 20th-century media function as a control mechanism. One objective: **consolidate world fame into a small number of controlled personalities**. Creators whose work was stolen often vanish from public view during or shortly after their fame (Beethoven, Mozart, Bruce Lee, etc.).
