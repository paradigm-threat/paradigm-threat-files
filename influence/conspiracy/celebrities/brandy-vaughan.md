## Thesis

Brandy Vaughan (1976–2020) was a former Merck pharmaceutical sales representative who founded Learn The Risk (2015) and the Council for Vaccine Safety. She sold Vioxx before its withdrawal for doubling stroke/heart attack risk; that experience led her to question industry practices. She died in December 2020 at 44. The coroner ruled natural causes—bilateral pulmonary thromboembolism (blood clots). Before her death, Vaughan had stated on video that she feared for her life and was not suicidal. The pattern: whistleblower or dissident dies young; official cause "natural"; community suspects foul play. Open questions remain about the source of the clots and whether her activism made her a target.

## II. Controversy

Anti-vax community members pointed to her 2019 statement as evidence of premonition or targeting. Mainstream fact-checkers (Snopes) and coroner concluded natural causes. The question: Could pulmonary thromboembolism be induced (e.g., via undisclosed substances or medical intervention)? Or is the community's suspicion confirmation bias? Both possibilities remain open.
