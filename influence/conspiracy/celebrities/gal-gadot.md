## Thesis

Gal Gadot served two years in the Israeli Defense Forces (IDF) as a combat fitness instructor and participated in the 2006 Israel-Lebanon war. She has publicly supported Israel and the IDF, making her divisive in Hollywood. Her casting as Wonder Woman—a symbol of female empowerment—alongside her IDF background, raises questions about narrative control: Is Gadot's stardom a vehicle for pro-Israeli messaging, and does her military service represent genuine patriotism or intelligence/PR coordination?
