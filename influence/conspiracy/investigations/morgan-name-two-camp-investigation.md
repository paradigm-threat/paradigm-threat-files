## Why the syllable is heavy before any of these men

**Morgan** is a Welsh given name, Old Welsh **Morcant**, then a hereditary surname. The usual gloss is *mor* “sea” plus an uncertain second element, folk-read as sea-chief or sea-born. **Glamorgan** is the country of Morgan. A medieval princely house, **Morgan ap Llywelyn**, sits on Tredegar from about 1330. Welsh tradition also uses **morgans** for water-sprites. The literary **Morgan le Fay** is a different but neighboring name (Morgen / Morgain), close enough that English speakers have mixed them for centuries ([Morgan (surname)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_(surname)); [medievalscotland.org](http://medievalscotland.org/problem/names/morgan.shtml)).

It is also **common**. One British surname index puts about **117,000** bearers in the UK, near the **41st** most frequent surname ([britishsurnames.uk](https://britishsurnames.uk/surname/morgan)). A name that common will stand on **both** sides of any long conflict by arithmetic alone. That is the non-conspiratorial floor under every row below.

## William Morgan — the exposer

**William Morgan** (born Culpeper, Virginia, 1774; disappeared from Canandaigua, New York, **11–14 September 1826**) announced *Illustrations of Masonry*, a print of the degree rituals. Local Masons had already refused him: his own membership was disputed, and a lodge that disliked his character would not have him. He was jailed on a petty debt. A publisher, **David C. Miller**, came to pay it. Morgan was taken from the jail at night and never reliably seen again. The book came out. The disappearance fed the **Anti-Masonic Party**, the first real third party in the United States ([Wikipedia — William Morgan (anti-Mason)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Morgan_(anti-Mason))).

He did not abolish the lodges. He did force the rites into daylight, and the political system had to spend a decade answering. Camp, on the author’s binary: **the people’s side of a secret society that had the sheriffs**. Entry fee: he presented himself as a Mason first. The exposer is an insider the lodge had already cast out. That is the usual shape of this camp, not a pure outsider.

He is **not** the banking family. J. P. Morgan’s line is **Miles Morgan**, who landed at Boston in **1636** from Wales with his brothers John and James ([Morgan family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_family)). William of Batavia has no published place on that tree.

## What explains the recurrence

Three explanations sit on top of each other. They do not compete.

1. **Frequency.** A top-fifty British surname will staff both the lodge and the mob that burns the lodge. Counting Morgans in power and Morgans in protest proves the name is common, not that it is a single office.
2. **Prestige.** The name was a **principality** (Glamorgan) and a **fairy** (Morgan le Fay, the water-morgans) before it was a bank. A controlling camp that wants to sound like the land will wear it. A storyteller who wants the perilous realm — Tolkien, whose profession was Welsh and whose second father was a Morgan — will wear it too. Tolkien did not take the surname. He lived inside its older meaning.
3. **The binary the author named.** Where the name is already trusted, the controlling camp **uses** it (the bank, the privateer-governor, the chromosome lab). Where a bearer breaks with the institution that credentialed him, the same syllable becomes the **exposure** (William Morgan and the rites; Francis, in the letter, as the last real world rather than as an asset of the new one). The clean American pair is the proof that this is **not** one conspiracy: the man who exposed the lodges in 1826 and the man born in 1837 who became the bank are **different families**. The syllable served both camps because both camps recruit the names people already answer to.

**Working conclusion:** Morgan does not belong to one camp. Over centuries it behaves like other high, common, princely names. The controlling camp gets the charter, the bank, and the laboratory. The other camp gets the exposer, the revolutionary, and the priest whose death felt like the real world leaving. The significance is the **split**, repeated, not a hidden Morgan directorate.
