# Investigation: Celebrity “modern slavery” — contracts, containment, NDAs, and the inability to choose

> **TL;DR:** This file investigates whether **celebrities and top artists** — actors, musicians, directors — operate under **structural constraints** that resemble **modern slavery** or **peonage**: **multi-picture / multi-album deals**, **360° label contracts**, **catalog ownership by third parties**, **NDAs**, and **reputational / financial penalties** for refusal. **Documented baseline:** Prince wrote **“slave”** on his face and called master ownership **“modern slavery”** ([BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/entertainment-arts-27081344), [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/features/prince-battle-to-control-career-artist-rights-7348551/)); California **Labor Code §2855** (the **De Havilland Law**) limits **personal services** contracts to **seven calendar years** because unlimited talent pacts were treated as **peonage** ([Hollywood Reporter — Martin Gang column](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/olivia-de-havilland-lawyer-who-helped-her-change-show-business-guest-column-1306588/)); studios **threaten breach-of-contract suits** when stars refuse assigned roles ([Observer — Ed Norton / *The Italian Job*](https://observer.com/2002/09/ed-norton-to-lansing-burn-this/)). **Author thesis (interpretive):** predictive-programming beats (e.g. Hugh Jackman’s **24-year Wolverine** arc, **Deadpool & Wolverine** meta; Morgan Freeman’s **2017 “we are at war”** Russia video) **rhyme** with a **containment layer** that keeps stars **working past intended retirement**, **unable to refuse** franchise obligations, **politically silent** unless **ordered** to speak — or **forced to deliver** establishment war rhetoric — with **one peak creative work** per franchise before **quality collapse** or **audience fatigue**. **Epistemic stance:** separate **documented contract law / artist statements** from **author pattern reads** and **second-hand anecdotes** (Michael Jackson / Sony, band-leader *Godfather* parable).

## Legal vocabulary — personal services, peonage, and “modern slavery”

### California Labor Code §2855 (De Havilland Law)

Hollywood’s **studio system** treated star contracts as **master/servant continuous employment** ([Academia — *Doing the Deal: Talent Contracts in Hollywood*](https://www.academia.edu/127897875/Doing_the_Deal_Talent_Contracts_in_Hollywood)). Studios **suspended** actors who **refused roles**, then **extended** contracts by suspension time — effective **servitude beyond seven years**.

**Olivia de Havilland v. Warner Bros. (1944):** California Court of Appeal held **§2855** means **seven calendar years**, not seven years of **actual service** — rejecting Warner’s suspension accounting ([Wikipedia — De Havilland Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Havilland_Law)). Attorney **Martin Gang** argued the statute originated to prohibit **peonage / involuntary servitude** ([Hollywood Reporter — Gang column](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/olivia-de-havilland-lawyer-who-helped-her-change-show-business-guest-column-1306588/)).

**Lesson for this investigation:** Law **recognizes** that **exclusive personal services** pacts can approach **involuntary servitude**; **modern** deals use **options**, **multi-picture clauses**, **NDAs**, and **financial ruin** instead of **suspension days** — same **family of constraint**, different **paperwork**.

### Scholarship — 360° deals and “modern slavery” label

Legal and academic literature explicitly links **360° recording deals** and **option contracts** to **Prince’s protest** and **“modern slavery”** rhetoric ([Seton Hall student scholarship — 360 deals PDF](https://scholarship.shu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=2675&context=student_scholarship); [Cardozo AELJ — unconscionability of 360 deals](https://larc.cardozo.yu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1278&context=cardozoaelj)).

**Key mechanics (L2):**

- **Only the label has options** — performer **cannot refuse** to be dropped but **cannot refuse** to continue without **severe penalties** ([Western Ontario thesis — 360 deals, cited in scholarship](https://uwo.scholaris.ca/bitstreams/23bf5145-8f07-4e03-a2d5-320a111758c2/download)).
- **Cross-collateralization** — failure in one revenue stream **recouped** from touring, merch, endorsements ([Seton Hall PDF](https://scholarship.shu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=2675&context=student_scholarship)).
- **Labels may withhold promotion** unless artist **concedes** on merch/tour splits ([Bart Day Law — 360 deals](https://bartdaylaw.com/360-deals/)).

## Hollywood — multi-picture deals and “cannot refuse” mechanics

### How contracts enforce performance (without chains)

Entertainment law: **13th Amendment** bars **specific performance** of **personal services** — you cannot **drag** an actor to set in **chains** ([Lexplug — talent agreements](https://www.lexplug.com/outlines/entertainment-law/film-television-transactional-practice/production-financing/talent-agreements)). Instead:

- **Negative injunctions** — block talent from **other work**
- **Breach damages** and **threatened litigation**
- **Multi-picture / sequel options** bundled into **breakout deal**
- **Pay-or-play** — studio pays but **owns** the **scheduling** right

## NDAs, silence, and Texas Trey’s Law

### Federal — Speak Out Act (2022)

- **Predispute** NDAs / nondisparagement **unenforceable** for **sexual assault / harassment** disputes ([GovInfo — PL 117-224](https://www.govinfo.gov/link/plaw/117/public/224?link-type=html)).
- **Not retroactive** to pre-2022 agreements in full ([Romano Law — Tiger Woods ex analysis](https://www.romanolaw.com/testing-the-breadth-of-the-speak-out-act-tiger-woods-ex-girlfriend-seeks-to-invalidate-nda/)).
- **Weinstein** used NDAs **for decades** to **silence victims** — [Enough Abuse](https://enoughabuse.org/how-do-ndas-perpetuate-child-sexual-abuse/).

### Texas — SB 835 “Trey’s Law” (effective Sept 1, 2025)

### Author thesis (L3) — “one great movie” phenomenon

**Claim:** Franchises often produce **one** **consensus masterpiece** (or **one trilogy**) where **cast + director + studio** align — ***Logan***, original ***Star Wars*** trilogy, ***The Dark Knight***, ***Mad Max: Fury Road*** — then **later entries** feel **obligatory**, **politically encoded**, or **creatively hollow**, and **even fans** admit **“something went wrong.”**

**Mechanisms (mixed tier):**

1. **Contractual obligation** — stars **return** regardless of **script** (see [Hollywood table](#hollywood--multi-picture-deals-and-cannot-refuse-mechanics)).
2. **IP mining** — studios **stretch** IP **past satiation** ([Terry PDF](https://swer.wtamu.edu/sites/default/files/Data/Terry.pdf)).
3. **Predictive programming flood** — [PP hub](/influence/predictive_programming/page.md) — **volume** of **establishment-coded** tentpoles **crowds out** **repeat** of **sincere** peak ([Trump-era Hollywood file](/influence/hollywood/trump-era-hollywood-political-media-investigation.md)).
