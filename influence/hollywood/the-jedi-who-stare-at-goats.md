# The Jedi Who Stare at Goats

Ronson’s joke title hid a real Jedi program — *Star Wars* already showed it

You have to be really bored to pick up a book or watch a movie called *Men Who Stare at Goats*. The title itself is a filter. The whole project seems designed so the majority looks away at first glance.

I only ended up watching the film because it came on automatically on Prime. Same experience most people had: weird quirky movie, interesting because Clooney is in it acting strange — and then a major revelation. There are apparently real people on this planet who think they were Jedi Knights trained by the U.S. government, especially around the time *Star Wars* came out.

That was an extremely hard disclosure to swallow. I really couldn’t believe it. I couldn’t believe someone would make a movie like that, lend it any credibility, and suggest it ties back to real testimony from U.S. soldiers and other people. I started this investigation because I wanted to see the truth of the movie and the book. What showed up pretty quickly is that both were designed to make people look away — and to mock the people who were originally part of this program.

Some of them might still be alive, living in cities somewhere. If they tried to tell anyone about the program they were in, they would look a lot like Clooney looks in the movie: absolutely crazy, without a shred of proof.

## The joke title and the real stack

Jon Ronson’s 2004 book is sold as nonfiction. The same year Channel 4 aired *Crazy Rulers of the World*, a companion documentary series — episode one shares the goats title. That is the nonfiction disclosure window: book plus television, before Hollywood finished converting the title into a Clooney farce. The 2009 film is sold as comedy. Together the book-and-film pair still do the same job: make psychic soldiers sound like a joke you stumbled into by accident.

The underlying stack is not a joke. By the early 1970s the U.S. intelligence lane already had remote-viewing work under names like SCANATE and SRI contracts. Army and DIA collection later ran under GRILL FLAME, CENTER LANE, SUN STREAK, and STAR GATE. MKULTRA sits earlier as the CIA mind-control research history everyone has heard of — drugs, hypnosis, behavioral control; the [CIA Reading Room still hosts the program paper trail](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/06760269). Jim Channon’s First Earth Battalion manual (late 1970s) pushed warrior-monk redesign language. Special Forces mind-body training in the 1980s shows up in secondary histories as Jedi Warrior / Jedi Project. Those lanes are adjacent and often conflated in pop culture. They are still real program names and real people — not Clooney improvisation.

Ronson’s own trail starts with **Uri Geller**. In October 2001 he meets Geller on a London rooftop; Geller’s long-standing claim is that he worked as a psychic for U.S. intelligence in the early 1970s. Inside the Army psychic culture Ronson documents, commanders run spoon-bending parties and metal psychokinesis demos as part of the same human-potential stack as walls and goat lore. I have no reason to deny that ability. It has been well reported for decades — even if later media spent just as much energy “debunking” it. Reporting and ridicule can both be true in public; neither erases why brass ordered officers to try.

The public wind-down story for the Fort Meade remote-viewing line lands in **1995** (AIR / STAR GATE closure narrative). A year earlier, theaters got ***Stargate* (1994)** — ancient aliens, modern authority, portal spectacle, Atlantis and Mars named so the room can laugh and walk out. Same Emmerich/Devlin shop later made *Independence Day*; camp is per film, and the 1994 title sits in the CIA/globalist misdirection lane on the [Hollywood two-camp read](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/hollywood/investigations/hollywood-two-camp-war-investigation.md). Timing matters: **containment movie in 1994, official program “closed” in 1995**, then leftover operators privatize, consult, and get mocked. The goats comedy in 2009 is a later harden of the same habit — own the weird name, move the culture on.

Ronson interviewed that world and still got critical codes wrong. Visibility is the cleanest example. The operators were not talking about vanishing from cameras. They meant not being seen: not being noticed, not being identified, walking among people without the observer treating you as present. Ronson’s own sources say that adaptation out loud. He still lets the culture hear “invisibility” as a magic trick that failed. He screws that one up even while half-agreeing with the correct reading.

Walking through walls is worse. Moving through walls was code for remote viewing — the functionality that actually featured in the declassified programs. Framing that code as a general crashing his nose into drywall and failing is an extreme violation of the duty to tell the truth from the documents and testimony. For that reason Ronson pins as a controlled opposition agent. No doubt about it. He did something screwed up: the declassified psychic material was never even the main punchline of his own book.

Apparently the book is “about” Abu Ghraib. He fails, inside his own pages, to honestly explain why Abu Ghraib would have anything to do with the Jedi program or remote viewing. The First Earth Battalion loudspeaker hobbyhorse is not the origin of sexual humiliation in war. That abuse has a long institutional history. It did not start in the 1970s New Age Army manuals.

## What the comedy subtracted

Ronson’s book does not end on goat jokes. Late chapters dig into **Frank Olson** and **MKULTRA**. Olson was a Fort Detrick scientist tied to CIA work (Artichoke / MKULTRA lane). In 1953 he went out a New York hotel window after an LSD dosing at a Deep Creek Lodge retreat — the official story sold for decades as bad-trip suicide. His son Eric spent a lifetime arguing homicide and silence: that Frank had seen terminal “expendable” experiments in Europe and become a security risk. The CIA’s own later admissions about dosing, brothels, and Gottlieb’s shop are public; Eric’s murder thesis remains contested in press, but Ronson treats the Olson house, the exhumation, and the narrow gap between poisoners and psychic administrators as load-bearing. Sidney Gottlieb — the man tied to Olson’s dosing — also shows up in the psychic-spy administration history. Light side and dark side of the same intelligence culture sit one office apart. The 2009 film largely **subtracts** this spine. You get warm war comedy instead of a citizen’s autopsy of MKULTRA. That is not a small cut. It is how the audience keeps MKULTRA as a rumor word and never has to sit with the murder question next to the Jedi ladder. Deeper program and disclosure context sits in the [RV / MKULTRA two-way investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/controlled_opposition/investigations/cia-reading-room-rv-two-way-iran-revival-investigation.md).

Ronson also follows the **privatization** of the psychic unit after the mid-1990s wind-down — retired viewers as consultants, rival schools, cut-throat civilian market. In that chapter he draws a line toward **Heaven’s Gate**: the 1997 mass suicide, the “Away Team” costumes, Hale-Bopp closure language. Critics argue over how tight that adjacency is. The book still puts it on the page: once the uniform stack dissolves, the same human-potential and exit mythology can drift into cult disaster. The comedy does not make you sit with that either. Discarded operators and cult endings are harder to laugh at than Clooney in goggles.

## Promote first, contain leftovers later

*Star Wars* came out in the same era as this stack. That film was geared toward making people believe these powers were real, even if misunderstood. If the goal in 1977 had been mockery, they would have shipped a 2009-style comedy then — not a beloved space epic with Jedi shown in a noble, artistic way.

First came mass promotion. Then came the ops window: First Earth language, Project Jedi ladders, Fort Meade remote viewing. Then incomplete disclosure: Ronson’s 2004 nonfiction, which the market already read as satire because of his literal mistakes. Then containment harden: the 2009 film, so leftover real Jedi who speak out sound like crazy Clooney.

Mockery-first would look like 2009 in 1977. What shipped instead was a civilizational hit that makes Jedihood desirable. The comedy arrives decades later — after *Stargate* (1994) already soft-contained portal/psychic-adjacent weirdness, after the 1995 STAR GATE wind-down story, after privatization and Heaven’s Gate adjacency hit the press — when the problem is leftover believers and operators, not recruitment.

Hollywood locked the goats title early. Rights were sold before Ronson finished the book. The screenwriter did not want much to do with him. The finished comedy warms the tone, renames the people, invents a buddy plot, and stamps fiction. The title card says more of this is true than you would believe — admit and inoculate in one line. Ronson later said he liked the film. That does not clear the operation. Subjects of soft versions often endorse them. Praise is not clearance.

Clooney’s Lyn Cassady is a composite psychic soldier. Jeff Bridges’s Bill Django rhymes with Channon. Kevin Spacey’s Larry Hooper is the film’s invented face for the dark twin — a name that does not appear in Ronson’s book, even though Ronson already titled a chapter THE DARK SIDE. Ewan McGregor’s reporter is the outsider tour the audience is allowed to take. Same Clooney year-cluster also gave *Burn After Reading* (2008): another comedy about intelligence decay. The laugh track is a habit.

## What Episode IV and V already showed

If the goats product teaches you to look away, *Star Wars* is the earlier disclosure. It taught a generation the skill list before the mockery hardened.

In the book, Project Jedi is told as a ladder: diet and body prep; invisibility adapted to finding a way of not being seen (“If you’re not observed, you are invisible. You only exist if someone sees you”); then kill a goat by staring — stop the heart. Stubblebine’s lane adds walls, levitation attempts, spoon-bending parties of the Geller class, cloud work, and a psychic spy unit. Map that onto the original trilogy and the rhyme is hard to miss.

Episode IV mostly gets it right. Obi-Wan’s “invisibility” works as failure of notice, not optical cloaking. He moves through Mos Eisley and the Death Star without being treated as a threat. The mind trick and the stormtrooper pass do the same job. That is exactly the adapted meaning from the real Project Jedi ladder — not being seen. Vader’s Force-choke is the lethal stare in costume. Luke destroying the Death Star without the targeting computer is intuition and Force aim under fire. Episode V adds levitation as a teaching spectacle: Yoda, the stones, the X-wing. Far-sense gets stronger too — Luke’s cave, the Luke–Leia call, Vader sensing Luke. The book’s own dark chapter covers the twin: same training stack turned toward coercion. Every serious *Star Wars* fan already felt some kind of real-life tie-in. There has to be an equivalence for what those films showed. There is.

## The real program under the joke

There was a real program to find gifted people — children, mostly — and train them as Jedi Knights in real life, then put them on missions to change the world. On those missions they thought they were heroes. Mostly they were used as spies without the political context needed to make a right choice. They lived in MKULTRA-style bubble lives: radical individual skill, thin news diet, almost no map of factions or consequences. Following orders blindly was the design.

Standard remote-viewing tradecraft already starves the viewer of meaning. You get a coordinate or a sealed envelope. You do not get the war, the coup, the hostage politics, or the legality. That can be sold as “reducing cueing.” It also means you cannot refuse on moral grounds, because you never learn what the task is for. Add false feedback, theatrical hits, and cover stories about who funds the product, and you have a sensor — not a citizen. Clooney’s Cassady is the public face of that isolation: jargon, confidence, and almost no ordinary political map.

Their actions were mostly spy actions. The dark-Jedi notion — which the goats film personifies as Hooper, and which the book’s dark half already documents — makes clear that the worst of these agents had the ability to stare at a goat or a hamster, and possibly even a person, and kill them. Of course that kind of power has limits. It had to stay in the shadows. Eventually the whole stack had to be contained so completely that nobody would even believe the powers were possible.

That is why Clooney’s face works as the vaccine. Speak the truth after 2009 and you sound like the crazy man from the comedy — not like a discarded intelligence asset asking for a hearing while still alive.

## *Star Wars* itself was contained

This page will not rebuild the entire Lucasfilm production history. The containment signs are still loud enough to name.

The notion that George Lucas alone “came up with the story” does not hold. He may have written an initial draft. Many hands touched it before final form, and it changed significantly. He then exited with little interest in the sequel, and only came back hard for the third film after the sequel’s major success. Mark Hamill himself may have been replaced after the holiday special — another contained object. Much of that special’s expansion work, including new-character energy around figures like Boba Fett, was lifted into Episode V without credit to the people who actually built those beats. Layer on layer of containment inside the project.

After the original trilogy, a long freeze kept sequels and mass cultural heat regulated mostly to models, cards, collectibles, and games — until Disney took over, which is simply more evidence of containment. Under Disney came the worst trilogy the franchise would ever see, now even discussed as something to decanonize. Spectacular evidence that a real program was being kept from daylight.

The agents trained in that program were clearly victims of massive abuse. They deserve a chance at redemption while they are still alive. The goats book and the Clooney farce were never built to give them that chance. They were built so the public would laugh first and never ask second.
