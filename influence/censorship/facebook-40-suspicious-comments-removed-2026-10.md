Mass comment deletion framed as **hacker cleanup** after forced logout and identity proof — **Undo promised, nowhere delivered**

> **TL;DR:** **9 Oct 2026** — Meta shows **Ari Asulin** a Facebook notice: *We removed 40 suspicious comments* — *These comments may have been posted by a hacker… If they are yours, you can undo it.* Same morning **08:38** Facebook Security emailed *Unlock your Facebook account* / locked for suspected unauthorized access (to ASU + paradigmthreat Gmail; **email image not stored** — date/content only). **Days before:** mass sign-outs across **Gmail, Instagram, Stripe, Picnic (tablet), phone apps** — everything except **Android Google login**. **Author read:** Meta flipped the account switch and **session-killed Meta-connected and collateral logins** before today’s unlock theater and forty-comment broom. **Undo chase failed**; Support submit **vanished** with no ticket. ## What broke (documented)

**Facebook / Meta** — after a **forced logout** and **identity / re-auth** gate, the account received a notice that Meta had already removed **40** comments as **“suspicious”** / possible **hacker** activity. Visible UI text:

- Headline: **We removed 40 suspicious comments**
- Body: *These comments may have been posted by a hacker, so Meta took action to remove them. If they are yours, you can undo it.*

**Author sequence (lived):** Meta **did not ask** before the purge. The flow was **log out → prove identity / re-auth → discover forty comments already deleted**. The “undo” offer arrives **after** the fact, not as consent.

## Undo chase — Meta offered restore, delivered nowhere (9 Oct 2026)

Author gave up after exhausting the obvious product paths the same morning. **Finding:** the notice **says** you can undo it and **does not say where**; every security / settings surface checked that day was a **dead end**. Facebook Help pages for restore/appeal have been **chronically out of date** for **at least a decade** (author lived experience).

| Surface checked | What was there | Undo / restore? |
|---|---|---|
| Original notice — scroll full comment list | Sample of removed comments; scrollbar; back / close | **No** Undo button visible |
| Accounts Center → **Password and security** | Login & recovery, Advanced Protection, Security checks | **No** comment restore |
| **Recent emails** (security, past 14 days) | Two mails at **08:38**, both *Unlock your Facebook account* (to `paradigmthreat@gmail.com` and `arasulin@asu.edu`); “OTHER EMAILS: none in past 2 days” | **No** comment-removal mail, **no** Undo link |
| **Security Checkup** | 3 recommendations: contact info, passkey, where you’re logged in; password updated Apr 22 2024 | **No** removed-comments step |
| Facebook **Notification settings** | Preference toggles (Comments, Tags, …) | **Wrong page entirely** — not Account Status / Support Inbox / removed content |
| **Support Inbox → Report details** form | Drafted full false-Undo report + screenshots; **Submit** | Modal **disappeared**; **no ticket ID / receipt** |
| **Support Inbox** after submit | Only **Reports about others** (e.g. FITZ HILTZHEIMER, Danna Lillianna, Stolen History, lukadone Braudazuth) | **No proof** the restore/false-Undo report was filed or queued |

**Author verdict:** Meta **lied**. “You can undo it” without a working control is **security theater** — delete first, promise a door that does not exist, leave Help docs that do not match the live UI. The Support path repeats the same grammar: **accept input, erase the trail, show a help façade**.

**Observed:**
1. Form **accepted** the payload (text + attached screenshot thumbnails).
2. On Submit, the modal **closed / disappeared** with **no confirmation number**, email receipt, or “we got your report” card.
3. Returning to **Support Inbox** shows only **Reports about others** — prior reports *of other people* — **not** the author’s own restore / false-Undo ticket.
