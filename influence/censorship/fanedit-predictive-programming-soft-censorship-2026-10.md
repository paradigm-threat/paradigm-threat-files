## Soft-censorship read (author)

This is not a content-policy strike for spam or piracy. It is **vocabulary policing** that collapses into a ban on **serious criticism**:

1. Hecklers / attackers treat “Predictive Programming” as proof the editor is “pushing conspiracy theories.”
2. Forum (and Reddit r/fanedits) rules forbid “political and conspiracy theories content.”
3. Mods remove the comments / posts — often without the author having opened a policy debate thread.
4. Result: people who name the pattern in film edits are **gotten rid of systematically** as soon as the **trigger words** surface — while the venue still looks like a neutral craft community.

**What the term actually implies (author):** *Predictive programming* is essentially an **implied accusation** that producers and editors wrote (or preserved) something **nefarious** — conditioning, cover, sabotage, or symbolic payload — not a neutral craft tip. The venues are not allowed to hear that Hollywood **does conspiracies**. Once “conspiracy” is the forbidden bucket, the ban **overlaps criticism of any kind** that goes past taste.

| Allowed talk (entertainment-only) | Disallowed talk (treated as “political / conspiracy”) |
|---|---|
| I don’t like this movie | This movie was **done wrong** |
| I like this plot; I’m making a change | It was **hijacked** or **sabotaged** |
| Pacing / runtime / continuity craft notes | It had **hidden meaning** |
| Preference swaps (tone, soundtrack, cuts) | **Symbolic references** that point to forbidden topics (e.g. **Saturnian cosmology**) |

**End goal (author):** keep debate inside **entertainment-only** talk — nothing “serious,” nothing “political.” That is the real enclosure. **Everything in this world is serious and political**; films encode money wars, theology, cosmology, war plans, and identity. Soft censorship pretends culture is a toy aisle so the only permitted critique is consumer preference (“I like / I don’t like / I’m remixing for fun”), never structural accusation.

**Industry function (author):** Hollywood-adjacent and fanedit gatekeeping venues do not need a memo titled “ban Ari.” They need **shared rules** that make the analytical vocabulary of predictive programming — and any adjacent claim of hijack, sabotage, or forbidden-symbol decode — **unspeakable**. Soft censorship does the rest.

**Not claimed:** that DigModiFication or FanEdit staff are paid studio agents; that every fanedit site uses identical language; that r/fanedits removed posts for this author on a specific date beyond staff’s “most likely” claim.

**Claimed:** staff **on record** tied removals to **conspiracy / political** content rules and cited **r/fanedits** parallel; author states attacks for **using the term** without having provoked forum conspiracy discussion; screenshot archived once under this path; author reads the rule as **taste-only enclosure**, not mere spam control.
