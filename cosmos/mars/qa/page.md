## Q1: What was the Secret War?

**The argument:** Soft history has novels, a Spanish–American War summer, canal “illusions,” and a dead Mars. What actually happened?

**Response (foundational — use this as the base for related questions):**

Earth and Mars were never two sealed worlds that only meet in pulp — they are one political neighborhood with a thin command channel at the top and mass publics kept dark on both ends — and in the late nineteenth century a secret war ran across that neighborhood while soft history filed it as novels, canal illusions, and a dead rock in the sky: human-stock riders in Earth-variant host suits hit Earth in cylinders, scraped scrap into tripods for a first beachhead only, built flying machines on the ground and flew them home when they could, and left unfinished kit and museum bipeds when disease and the forbidden-blood high stranded the remnant; the most successful versions of those missions left no witnesses because perfect execution exterminated them, which is why the 1896–97 mystery airships appear briefly and vanish, while Boston–New England was the massive failure that leaked — leftover machines, paper encodes, later radio lids — and London was the Empire shelf encode that Wells, who was fed rather than inventing, sealed with protest and a later Woking statue so everyone would call Boston the fake map; Edison was not a blank-page inventor but the reverse-engineering face of that inherited kit, still so new that doctors in Buffalo would not even use an X-ray on the dying McKinley; Earth’s reply, filed in Serviss the same season as the Spain cover lid, reverse-engineered lift, struck Mars under a McKinley wartime tip, butchered or watched butchered the slave class when fleets appeared, and turned the canal water off — flooding the innocent because retaliation felt like survival even when nobody was sure how to win — then McKinley was killed to cover the war, Roosevelt Progressivism opened as the next lid, and the conquering camp’s revenge on Earth took the shape of ideology, information suppression, mass influence, world wars, and imposed famines without regret, because we had just wrecked their planet’s ability to live by canal; Spain 1898 only earns the word war because that secret war ran inside it, with Church versus Constitution as the real sovereignty stakes, and soft history’s alien costume is the last mercy — strip it and the cast is humans of all shapes and sizes on both worlds, thin slaves under giant chiefs, and we blew ourselves up with no other species left to blame.
