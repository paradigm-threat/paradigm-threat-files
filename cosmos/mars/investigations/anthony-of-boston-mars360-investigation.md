4. **Theological politics** — *Ares Le Mandat* subtitles and abstracts on SSRN connect **Vatican II / Latin Mass**, **666 / Mars 360 numerology**, **Israel–Gaza**, **terrorism**, **Bitcoin**, and **IMF**-style intervention proposals — high-contention narrative layering typical of a single omnibus manuscript rather than peer-reviewed narrow claims.
