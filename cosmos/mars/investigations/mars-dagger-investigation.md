## VI. Plates, Cumorah, and the same recovery method

### 6.1 Does any source say the Mars dagger found the plates?

What sources **do** say:

| Claim | Source |

Ashurst-McGee's key passage (the closest scholarly statement to your thesis):

> If we theorize that Joseph either dreamed about the location of the plates or saw their location through the use of his seerstone and then followed the magical procedures with which he was familiar, he would have gone to the location where they were buried, **formed a magic circle**, said the requisite prayers, and only then attempted to dig them up. … There is **no historical evidence known to me** that Joseph formed a magic circle or used any magical implement other than his seerstone on this occasion, but we **do know that had he chosen to do so**, the Smith family possessed magic parchments and the **requisite magic dagger**, and Joseph was familiar with the making and operation of magic circles.

So: **same method by inference and toolkit** — not **dagger attested at Cumorah**.

### 6.2 Hill Cumorah = treasure site and plate site (same hill)

[Dan Vogel, *The Locations of Joseph Smith's Early Treasure Quests*](https://www.dialoguejournal.com/articles/the-locations-of-joseph-smiths-early-treasure-quests/) (Dialogue):

- **"Undoubtedly the most significant of Smith's treasure quests"** occurred on the hill now called **Cumorah**.
- Plate discovery (1823) and hill activity occurred **"within a treasure-seeking context."**
- **Joseph Smith Sr.** had long dug on the hill seeking buried treasure; **Joseph Jr.** accompanied him.
- Money diggers excavated on the hill **before** plates removed **22 September 1827** — witnesses describe a large hole.
- **Samuel Lawrence** (seer) had been to the hill, tried to obtain what was there, and **saw the plates in his stone** (1825); Joseph later said he had taken Lawrence to the **wrong location**.
- **Martin Harris:** Smith led treasure-digging company **until** he received the plates; **Josiah Stowell** was digging for money when Joseph took the plates.
- Vogel (citing Quinn 114–43): treasure-seeking context of plate activities is established scholarship.

**Same geography, same era, same crew** — not a separate religious geography.

### 6.3 Same instrument: seer stone in hat

Multiple hostile and friendly sources agree the **plates were located with the same seer stone** used for money digging:

- **Martin Harris (1859):** Joseph found the plates **"by looking in the stone found in the well of Mason Chase."**
- **Willard Chase affidavit (1833):** Joseph Sr. told him that **"if it had not been for that stone, he would not have obtained the book."**
- **Vogel:** Smith's use of the **same stone** and **same modus operandi** (stone in hat) for translating plates and for treasure is **"simply two sides of the same coin."** *"It is impossible to understand fully the mature Joseph Smith without coming to terms with his early role as treasure seer."*

**Hyrum Smith** borrowed Willard Chase's seer stone in **1825** for **"business of importance, which could not very well be done without the aid of the stone"** — same window as Lawrence's hill visit and pre-plate digs.

**Barrett's *Magus*** distinguishes **Intelligences** (presiding good angels of planets, e.g. **Graphiel** for Mars) from **Spirits** (subordinate daemons). The dagger carries **both** Mars seal and Intelligence sigil plus **Adonai** — a blade inscribed to command the **martial planetary stack** while standing inside a protective circle. That is exactly the posture required when gnomes or treasure spirits push back.

**Ebenezer Sibly** (cited in *Scrying for the Lord*): magicians who **left their magic circle** during a treasure operation were **"instantaneously crushed into atoms."** The circle is not decoration; it is the **only safe zone** while negotiating with guardians.

### 7.3 Guardian mechanics — why digs fail

| Mechanism | Folk / affidavit behavior | Plate episode parallel |
|-----------|---------------------------|-------------------------|
| **Treasure sinks / moves** | Chest slips deeper when spade strikes; iron chest "moved away two or three rods" (Harris on Stowell dig) | Plates disappear when Joseph looks away; "enchantment" holds them |
| **Shock / strike** | Guardian knocks diggers back | Joseph receives **"shock"** three times reaching for plates (Cowdery); knocked **"three or four rods"** (Chase) |
| **Silence rule** | One spoken word breaks the operation | Chase: failure because Joseph **"had not obeyed your orders"** |
| **Circle breach** | Guardian penetrates circle if digger mutters or lacks faith (Winchell divinity) | Theorized: no circle or wrong protocol → failed 1823 |
| **Propitiatory sacrifice** | Cock to prince of spirits (Walters) | Not attested at Cumorah; Walters parallel suggests layer existed in same culture |
| **Necromantic requirement** | Dead body / body part to satisfy guardian | Spirit demands **Alvin** present (Chase); rumors of exhuming Alvin (Sept 1824) |

**Jonathan Thompson** (1826 trial, via Stowell dig): treasure protected by a **charm**; required **faith** and **talismanic influences**; despite effort, treasure **slipped away**.

**Joseph Capron** affidavit: Smiths drew circles; treasure guarded by spirits.

**Repo read:** "Slipping treasure" and guardian repulsion are **often** failed breach survival or **wrong deposit** — but in-repo the **plate deposit** adds **embedded cataclysm memory** release. Circles and dagger may protect the **operator's psyche and body** during breach, not **authenticate against a Mars gate**.

### 7.4 Guardian typology — what appears at the stone box

European and American money-digging recognized **four major guardian classes** (Ashurst-McGee; Huggins):

| Class | Examples | Moral valence | Smith-plate echoes |
|-------|----------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Diabolical** | Devil, demons, "powers of darkness" | Evil | Joseph 1836: "powers of darkness strove hard against me" |
| **Animal / familiar** | Toad, black dog, salamander, Cerberus-without-bone | Usually evil | **"Something like a toad"** in stone box (Chase, Saunders) |
| **Human ghost** | Murdered victim buried with hoard | Often **morally neutral** victim | **Bloody clothes**; murdered guardian of deposit (Lapham 1870) |
| **Nature spirit / gnome** | Pygmies, dwarves, earth elementals | Capricious; can be friendly | Less explicit in Smith sources; grimoire background |

**The toad that becomes a man** (Willard Chase affidavit, 1833):

> Joseph looked in the box and saw **something like a toad**, which soon assumed the **appearance of a man**, and **struck him on the side of his head**. … He enquired why he could not obtain the plates; to which the spirit made reply, **because you have not obeyed your orders**.

Joseph Smith Sr. identified this figure to Chase as **"the spirit of the prophet who wrote this book"** — not the devil, but the **author-guardian** of the deposit.

**Benjamin Saunders (1884):** same toad → man sequence forbidding removal.

**Fayette Lapham (1870)** — Joseph Sr. interview, forty years on:

- Spirit in **bloody ancient clothes** appears in Joseph Jr.'s dream.
- Deposit buried **"for the benefit of the world."**
- On failed extraction: the guardian was **murdered** when the treasure was hidden, **"sworn to take charge and protect"** it until exhibition time — classic **murdered treasure-ghost** motif.

**Joseph and Hiel Lewis (Harmony, ~1879):** Joseph initially described only a **"bleeding ghost"** from a dream — **"not one word about visions of God, or of angels."** They read later angel language as **afterthought**.

### 7.5 Moroni as redacted guardian — scholarly fork

Two opposed historiographic positions (both useful for this investigation):

| Thesis | Advocates | Claim |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| **Treasure-guardian → angel** | Huggins, Quinn, Vogel, many critics | Moroni began as guardian ghost / magical figure; later "baptized" into angelic narrative |
| **Angel → treasure-guardian** | Ashurst-McGee, Morris | Hostile neighbors **defrocked** Moroni; Joseph consistently had an angel; treasure motifs are **secondary contamination** from critics |

**Huggins's strongest move:** links Moroni to the **murdered treasure-guardian ghost** — especially the subtype tied to **Captain Kidd** lore (treasure ghost guarding pirate hoards). Notes ironic **Comoros** capital **Moroni** / **Cumorah** phonetic cluster (speculative geography wordplay).

**Ashurst-McGee's strongest move:** eyewitness priority — Joseph's own earliest accounts trend **angel**; hostile affidavits **multiply inconsistent guardians** (toad, giant, dwarf, headless Spaniard, devil) proving **distortion**, not original.

**Repo synthesis (speculative):** Both camps document the **same phenomenological layer**. Whether Joseph "believed angel" or "believed guardian" first, the **sequence** matches post-MFEE **chamber breach** protocol:

```text
locate (seer stone) → approach deposit → breach / guardian phenomenon (circumstantial)
→ operator stress (orders, companions, circle, silence) →
pass/fail coupling → extract or repulsion
```

The **angel Moroni** is the **respectable face** pasted over **haunting and memory release** once the movement needs legitimacy. The **toad, bloody ghost, and shock** are **pre-redaction breach symptoms** — not proof of Martian security engineering.

Quinn (*Early Mormonism*, p. 147–48): plate-recovery elements — disappearance, shock, required companion — are **"completely consistent"** with treasure-guardian folklore. Ashurst-McGee agrees the motifs appear but argues Joseph **momentarily** misread an angel through treasure lens in 1823, then corrected.

### 7.6 Guardian + dagger + plates — unified stack (repo model, revised)

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  MFEE BURIAL CHAMBER (dead + hoard + analog medium)          │
│  └── SEAL BREACH → circumstantial guardian / memory release   │
│       └── CIRCLE + DAGGER + NAMES (operator protection)       │
│            └── SEER STONE (locate + resonance read)           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Guardian phenomenon** = **circumstantial** — spirits of the buried, embedded **cataclysm memory** in rock/jewels released on breach (§7.0). **Not** Mars access-control hardware.
- **Dagger** = draw boundary, invoke martial planetary authority (**Graphiel** / Mars intelligence) — **operator-side** ritual hardware; may stabilize operator during breach; **not** a key to a Martian lock.
- **Adonai** on the blade = divine-name in circle work (Barrett / Key of Solomon) + possible **Martian monotheist faction ID** ([Mars hub](/cosmos/mars/page.md)) — **operator protocol**, not guardian DRM.
- **Failed coin digs** = wrong deposit, partial kit, or operator **could not survive** breach phenomenology.
- **Failed plate attempt (1823)** = breach **memory/spirit** overwhelm; **disobedience to orders** and **Alvin** companion motif — operator not yet keyed for full coupling.
- **Successful plate retrieval (1827)** = operator survived breach + **resonance-coupled** to analog index ([quantum storage §2.4](/science/computing/investigations/quantum-computer-infinitely-indexed-storage-thesis-investigation.md)).

**Post-MFEE Mars role (§3.6):** Breakaway civilization may have **tutored** operators or **delivered** famine relief and promised-land narrative — but **did not** install per-chamber guardian devices. **Direct incursions** were **few**; influence ran through **humans** and **sparse handler contact**, preferring **god / ancient-alien** cover over admission of planetary destruction.

**Alvin thread (dark):** Chase says spirit required **oldest brother Alvin**; Alvin died 1823; Sept **1824** Joseph Sr. **exhumed Alvin** amid rumors the family needed body parts for necromantic guardian requirements (Brodie; Vogel). Whether literal or rumor, the **social memory** ties plate recovery to **necromantic guardian law** — not to Sunday-school angelology.

### 7.7 Scriptural retrofit — Helaman and the curse of the land

After 1830, **Helaman 13:17–18** / **Mormon 1:18** entered the canon:

> Whoso shall hide up treasures in the earth shall find them again no more, because of the **great curse of the land**, save he be a **righteous** man and shall hide it up unto the Lord.

Vogel: this **legitimized** the treasure seer who succeeds where others fail — moral filter on **technical** guardian law. Failed Palmyra digs = unrighteous or incompetent operators; Smith's plate success = righteous authorized operator.

**Repo read:** Scripture **theologizes** breach survival as **moral** selection — righteous operator vs cursed land. Under §7.0, Helaman also **suppresses** cataclysm memory while preserving **deliverance** framing ([promised-land](/history/mudflood/investigations/promised-land-investigation.md)).
