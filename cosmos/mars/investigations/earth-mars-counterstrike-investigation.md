## 1. Working thesis

### 1.1 Claim (Author — open)

1. A **Martian incursion** struck Earth in the **late 19th century** (Wells as after-action / managed disclosure; possible multi-theatre waves — London / Spain / US — see Mars hub).
2. **Critical Documented leftover:** at mission end, humans possessed a **great flying-machine** on Primrose Hill — already under atmospheric test when the invaders died — plus fighting-machines still standing for sight-seers (**§1.4**). That kit is a **very significant, very possible** bridge to Earth lift / space capability and a counterstrike. It is **less** plausible that nearly all humans would successfully **hide** such tech than that factions would **study and fly** it.
3. Sometime afterward — inside the broad band **~1865–1945**, with **literary encoding concentrated 1898–interwar** — Earth (or an Earth faction) mounted a **counterstrike against Mars**, reverse-engineering leftover Martian hardware (Serviss states this explicitly).
4. That operation used **advanced technology** (electrical / anti-grav lift, vibratory or DEW-class weapons, superior air/space craft) that later **vanished from public capability** — lost, sequestered, or mythified as pulp.
5. Fiction filed the memory as **impossible adventure** so institutions would not have to admit either the incursion or the reply.
6. **Secret war — dual motive (Author):** The same faction that can **project force** to Mars (or the far portal node) also **keeps the war secret**. Contain the outbreak **and** silence witnesses; strike the far side **and** bury the record. Not two conspiracies — **one** operation with two necessary arms (§1.7–1.8).
7. **Suppression balancing act (Author):** Use the tech for a strike **and** suppress mass knowledge. That balance **backfired**. Suppress hard enough and the adversary (or rival human faction) can erase the war until **nobody knows there was a war**. Dead Planet / “Mars is a rock” / canals-as-illusion are candidate end-states.
8. **Who ruled?** It was **not necessarily “Martians”** who tried to rule Earth. It can be **humans who could reach Mars** (lift / portal / leftover flyer lineage) and take over — then file the conflict as alien invasion pulp so the public never names the **human** side of the secret war (§1.8).
9. **MFEE agency open:** secondary discharge on Mars is easy to overlook as mere “consequence” if the engagement was **ordered from Earth** (§1.10).
10. **No isolation:** breakaway history = **~1° political** separation at all times; general publics on **both** planets kept unaware (§1.11).

### 1.2 What this is *not*

- Not a substitute for the **MFEE** (~1774 Pugachev-window DEW backfire). MFEE devastated Mars first; survivors later hit Earth; **counterstrike** is a candidate **third** beat.
- Not a claim that Serviss’s plot beats (flooding Thaumasia, Aina wedding, Edison personally) are literal telemetry.
- Not closed. Prefer **falsifiers** (§7) over confirmation bias.

### 1.4 Primrose Hill flying-machine — primary tech seed (**very significant**)

**Documented (Wells, *WotW*):**

- Artilleryman: lights over Hampstead; “something up in the air”; “I believe they've built a **flying-machine**, and are learning to fly.”
- Narrator at Primrose Hill pit: across the farther lip, “**flat and vast and strange**, lay the **great flying-machine** with which they had been experimenting upon our denser atmosphere when decay and death arrested them. **Death had come not a day too soon.**”
- Epilogue: sight-seers still gather about “**the Martian machine that stands there still**” on Primrose Hill — leftover hardware remains in the open after the war.

**Author inferences (high weight for AU-085):**

| Point | Read |
|-------|------|
| **Nearly built = transferable** | Atmosphere trials already running; structure present; not a paper sketch. Earth engineers inherit a **working-class problem**, not a blank page. |
| **Very possible reverse-engineering path** | Heat-ray, Black Smoke, handling-machines, aluminium plant, **plus** the flyer → same stack Serviss has Edison study from derelict Martian gear. |
| **Hiding from nearly all humans = less plausible** | Epilogue already shows **public** machines and museum specimens. Total concealment of a Primrose Hill flyer from “nearly all humans” fights the text and fights incentives (military, industrial, national prestige). Partial compartmentalization is plausible; **universal** silence is not the default. |
| **Counterstrike pathway** | Finish / copy the flyer → Earth-built lift tuned in **dense air / high g** → deploy to Mars (asymmetry §4) → Serviss fleet encode. |

Prior dossiers treated the flyer mainly as Martian **return vessel**. This investigation adds: once abandoned, it is also Earth’s **stolen ladder** — the most concrete Documented object that makes a real counterstrike **very possible**.

### 1.5 The balancing act that backfired (Author)

Earth’s bind after the incursion:

1. **Use the tech** — finish the flyer, field a fleet, hit Mars before they return (Serviss logic).
2. **Suppress the tech** — keep lift/DEW out of mass hands, out of rival states, out of Mars-aligned networks; file the war as fiction / bacteria miracle / “never again.”

**Author:** that dual policy is a **tricky balancing act**. Lean too far into suppression (bury the flyer, mythify the war, Dead-Planet the public sky) and you also bury the **institutional memory and industrial base** that could detect or answer the next move. Then the enemy can erase attempts entirely — wreck records, dismantle surface proof, overwrite chronology — until **nobody knows there was a war at all**. Lean too far into open use and you risk another invasion, proliferation, or capture of the kit.

**Backfire end-state candidates:** public Mars = dead rock; Serviss memory-holed; WotW “just a novel”; exotic propulsion “never existed”; only pulp and Asylum still say Earth hit Mars; **canals “never real”** (§1.6).

### 1.6 Schiaparelli’s canals — **we** turned the water off (Author)

**Documented observation history:**

| When | What |
|------|------|
| **1877+** | Schiaparelli maps *canali*; later confirmations; Lowell (1894–1908) insists on **artificial irrigation** civilization |
| **~1909–1910 onward** | Better photos / establishment push: canals “fade”; Wallace *Is Mars Habitable?* (1907) attacks Lowell |
| **1965** | Mariner 4: cratered desert, **no canals** — institutional seal |

Mainstream story: optical illusion / wishful mapping all along.

**Author join to AU-085:**

Schiaparelli and Lowell were not crazy — they were looking at a planet whose **water grid was still running** (or still scarred enough to read as linear engineering). Wells 1908 (*Things that Live on Mars*) still describes polar melt feeding a **canal civilization**. Serviss’s counterstrike climax is not random: **destroy the canal dams / waterworks → flood the inhabited basins → kill the civilization**.

So the later absence of canals is misread as “they were never there.” Better AU-085 read:

> **The Martians didn’t turn off their water. We did.**

Earth’s finishing move (or its aftermath) broke the irrigation signature that 1877–1908 telescopes were reporting. Then Dead Planet Protocol / Mariner optics finished the public story: dry rock, canals = illusion, Lowell = crank.

**Keep distinct from:**

| Layer | Water story |
|-------|-------------|
| **MFEE ~1774** | Biosphere / ocean-scale wreck — earlier |
| **Counterstrike (this §)** | Kill **remaining engineered canal grid** after late-19th-c. war — *why canals were seen, then gone* |
| **Dead Planet Protocol** | Surface dismantling / “no cities” for Mariner-era cameras |

**Falsifier:** prove canal maps have **zero** correlation with any real linear/hydrologic features and that Serviss’s flood beat has no peer encodes. Until then, treat “illusion-only” as the **suppression end-state**, not the default.

The winners on Earth need not be “aliens.” They can be **humans who could get to Mars**, break the waterworks, suppress the flyer lineage, and sell the public a Wells invasion myth so the **human** side of the ledger stays invisible.

| HECU vs Xen | Far-side projection + witness silence in one brief |

**Dual motivation (locked):**

1. **Project force** — finish the flyer / field the fleet / push armor through the gate; hit Mars / Xen / the waterworks.
2. **Keep it secret** — silence witnesses, mythify the war, Dead-Planet the sky, so the public never learns which **humans** owned the ladder.

Same actors, both motives. That is why suppression is not a separate later conspiracy — it is **half of the operation**. When suppression wins too completely, even the operators’ descendants may not know (§1.5 backfire).

### 1.9 Planet as military base + commander (Author — fiction encode)

**Observation:** Across SF, whole planets (or “the colony / the fleet / the world”) are routinely run like a **military base** with a **commander**, admiral, Overlord, Colonial Marshal, Base Commander, Fleet Admiral, Goa’uld System Lord, Combine Advisor+Overwatch, etc. — not like a civilian republic with open debate.

**AU-085 join:** If Earth (and/or Mars) was settled into a **secret-war command structure** after the flyer/fleet era — humans with far-side reach as the ruling caste — then fiction keeps teaching the **true org chart** while calling it “space opera”:

| Fiction grammar | Secret-war read |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Planet = base | Post-war Earth (or Mars remnant) still under **operational control**, not peacetime civil society |
| One commander / warlord / Overlord | Continuity of the faction that could **reach Mars** and silence witnesses |
| Civilians as personnel / colonists / cattle | Mass public outside the need-to-know; HECU-style containment culture normalized |
| “Martial law forever” settings | The war never publicly ended — only the **memory** of it did |

Examples (motif family, not a claim each title knows AU-085): *Starship Troopers* (Federation as forever war), *Ender’s Game* (International Fleet runs childhood), *Stargate* (SGC / Goa’uld planetary lords), *Aliens* (Colonial Marines / Weyland as force projection), *Half-Life 2* (Combine occupation as planetary base), Barsoom jeddaks, Serviss’s Emperor of Mars, Wells artilleryman’s “discipline” fantasy.

**Author:** the “entire planet run like a base” trope is less random pulp laziness than **leak of post–secret-war governance** — Earth still looks like a base because the people who won (or inherited) the Mars-reach war **never demobilized the command**.

### 1.10 MFEE secondary discharge — overlooked if **ordered from Earth** (Author)

**Standard AU framing (prior docs):** Martian DEW platforms fire at Earth (~1774 Pugachev window) → **secondary discharge** backfires to Mars → biosphere kill. Easy to read as Mars’s own war going wrong.

**New perspective (Author — open):** If the shot was **ordered from Earth** (Earth-side command / breakaway faction with Mars-reach already in the loop), then the “2nd discharge” on Mars is easily **misfiled as natural consequence / tragic accident / Mars attacking itself**, when it is the **predictable return path of an Earth-initiated energy war**. Analysts looking for “Mars fired first” miss the **political** trigger: Earth ordered the engagement; Mars surface paid the blowback bill.

| Misread | AU-085 preferred read |
|---------|----------------------|
| Mars attacked Earth; karma fried Mars | **Earth ordered** the DEW event; secondary discharge = consequence of that order |
| Two isolated worlds colliding | Same political graph; weapons and command already linked |
| MFEE “proves” Martian agency alone | Proves **shared infrastructure + Earth command option** |

This does **not** delete the physical secondary-discharge model in [`wotw-mfee-mars-catastrophe.md`](wotw-mfee-mars-catastrophe.md). It relocates **agency**: the overlooked question is **who gave the order**, not only what the beam did.

### 1.11 Breakaway civilizations — **never imply isolation** (Author lock)

**Reject:** “Breakaway” = lost colony, sealed planet, no contact, public on each world living in true separation.

**Lock:** At all times there was about **one degree of political separation** — a thin elite/command channel between Earth and Mars (and related nodes). Not geographic fate. Not a secret the **general public** on **either** planet was allowed to know. The separation is **political compartmentalization**, not isolation of civilizations.

| False model | AU-085 model |
|-------------|--------------|
| Two worlds, then contact | Continuous **1° political** link; mass publics kept dark on both ends |
| Breakaway = disappeared | Breakaway = **need-to-know** continuity of the Mars-reach command caste |
| Isolation explains ignorance | **Secrecy** explains ignorance; traffic and orders still moved |

Joins §1.8–1.9: humans who could reach Mars / run the planet as a base were never “cut off.” They were **one handshake** from Earth command the whole time — which is why an Earth-ordered MFEE shot (§1.10) and a later secret counterstrike are the same family of politics, not miracles of reconnection.

#### D. How Earth↔Mars amnesia works **without** mind-wipe tech (Author)

Marvel needs gadgets. A real Mars secret war only needs **attribution failure** + **thin witness set**:

| Condition | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Strike **cannot be traced back to Earth** (far-side ops, deniable propulsion, no public telemetry) | No “we did it” evidence chain for historians or newspapers |
| Almost nobody knows unless they **saw ships leave and not return** | Witnesses = dock/yard/crew families at most — not a planet-wide public |
| No returning fleet, no victory parade, no after-action in the clear | Survivors who stayed home have **absence**, not a war story |
| Cover myths (bacteria miracle, “just a novel,” canals = illusion, Dead Planet) | Fill the gap where a war narrative would be |

**Result:** Same social outcome as Fury’s wipe — operators and publics **do not carry a usable memory of the war** — produced by **secrecy + non-return**, not by psychic erasure. Anyone who only saw ships depart and vanish can be told anything (accident, deep mission, never happened). The war stays unowned.

**Join:** Dual motive (§1.8) still holds — project force **and** keep it untraceable. HECU “silence witnesses” is the aggressive edge; **ships that never come home** is the quiet edge.

| Usage | Meaning | Match to AU-085? |
|-------|---------|------------------|
| **Laos “Secret War”** (etc.) | Covert theater **inside** a known Cold War; later disclosed | Public eventually learns; same planet |
| **Austin Carson *Secret Wars* (2018)** | Covert state intervention in **ongoing** wars; often “open secrets” among powers | Escalation management — **not** planetary amnesia |
| **Covert action / proxy war** | Hide **sponsorship**, not the existence of conflict | Wrong sense of “secret” |
| **Ops declassified after wartime** | Temporary classification | User correctly rejects as weak explaination for AU-085 |

**Verdict (Author):** In ordinary history and IR, “secret war” almost never means *a war with another world that neither public remembers*. **Nothing on that shelf comes close to AU-085.** Fiction peers are thin; **Marvel 2004–05 *Secret War*** is the standout match — **mechanism**, **sin-eater pedagogy**, and **NSA-window timing** (delays through Dec 2005). **1984** and especially **2015 *Secret Wars*** own/re-own the **words** as cosmic toy events — **2015 = CIA containment** of the Fury sense. Phrase stays in the comics aisle; the real Earth↔Mars amnesia war stays unthinkable; kids who only know 2015 never meet the sin eater who was the point of 2004–05.

## 3. Primary encode — Serviss, *Edison’s Conquest of Mars* (1898)

**Documented** (public-domain text):

1. Martians devastate Earth; disease ends the first wave; Mars prepares **another**.
2. Edison fields **electrical ships** (attraction/repulsion vs gravity) and **disintegrators** (vibratory atom-unbind).
3. Global coalition funds **~100 ships / ~3,000 disintegrators** within months.
4. Fleet fights Martian airships over Mars; humans hold **altitude / propulsion advantage**.
5. Finishing move: smash canal/waterworks → **flood** inhabited basins (Aina’s counsel).
6. Martians = **tall humanoids** (~12–16 ft); Earth-line humans kept as **slaves**; tradition that they built **pyramids / Sphinx**.

**Author read for this dossier:**

Serviss was a **popular science journalist**, not a random fantasist — raises the “how much did he know?” question already flagged in [`mars-fiction-survey.md`](mars-fiction-survey.md) §X.5.

## 4. Technical asymmetry (Author — working physics read)

Hypothesis: Earth-built lift / weapons engineered against **higher g and denser air** are **overbuilt** for Mars; Martian craft tuned to **low g / thin air** underperform on Earth.

| Side | Environment design | Expected behavior |
|------|--------------------|-------------------|
| **Martian ships / walkers** | Low g, thin air | Dominant on Mars; **struggle on Earth** (Wells/Serviss: overweight, short campaign) |
| **Earth electrical / anti-grav** | High g, thick air | Harder to invent; once built, **works well on Mars** |
| **Disintegrator / DEW class** | Same family as MFEE kit | Usable both ways; Serviss aims it **at Mars** |

This asymmetry makes a successful Earth counterstrike **more plausible**, not less: the hard problem is building the kit on Earth; Mars is the easier theatre for that kit.
