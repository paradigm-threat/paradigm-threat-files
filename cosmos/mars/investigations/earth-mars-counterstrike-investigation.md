## 1. Working thesis

### 1.1 Claim (Author — open)

1. A **Martian incursion** struck Earth in the **late 19th century** (Wells as after-action / managed disclosure). **Reconstruction locus (Author):** **Boston / New England** as most likely beachhead; London / Primrose Hill as British-print encode (**§1.4b**). 2. **Critical Documented leftover:** at mission end, humans possessed a **great flying-machine** (British text: Primrose Hill) — already under atmospheric test when the invaders died — plus fighting-machines still standing for sight-seers (**§1.4**). That kit is a **very significant, very possible** bridge to Earth lift / space capability and a counterstrike. It is **less** plausible that nearly all humans would successfully **hide** such tech than that factions would **study and fly** it.
3. Sometime afterward — inside the broad band **~1865–1945**, with **literary encoding concentrated 1898–interwar** — Earth (or an Earth faction) mounted a **counterstrike against Mars**, reverse-engineering leftover Martian hardware (Serviss states this explicitly).
4. That operation used **advanced technology** (electrical / anti-grav lift, vibratory or DEW-class weapons, superior air/space craft) that later **vanished from public capability** — lost, sequestered, or mythified as pulp.
5. Fiction filed the memory as **impossible adventure** so institutions would not have to admit either the incursion or the reply.
6. **Secret war — dual motive (Author):** The same faction that can **project force** to Mars (or the far portal node) also **keeps the war secret**. Contain the outbreak **and** silence witnesses; strike the far side **and** bury the record. Not two conspiracies — **one** operation with two necessary arms (§1.7–1.8).
7. **Suppression balancing act (Author):** Use the tech for a strike **and** suppress mass knowledge. That balance **backfired**. Suppress hard enough and the adversary (or rival human faction) can erase the war until **nobody knows there was a war**. Dead Planet / “Mars is a rock” / canals-as-illusion are candidate end-states.
8. **Who ruled?** It was **not necessarily “Martians”** who tried to rule Earth. It can be **humans who could reach Mars** (lift / portal / leftover flyer lineage) and take over — then file the conflict as alien invasion pulp so the public never names the **human** side of the secret war (§1.8).

10. **No isolation:** breakaway history = **~1° political** separation at all times; general publics on **both** planets kept unaware (§1.11).

## 3. Primary encode — Serviss, *Edison’s Conquest of Mars* (1898)

**Documented** (public-domain text):

1. Martians devastate Earth; disease ends the first wave; Mars prepares **another**.
2. **Documented reverse-engineering open:** Edison, Kelvin, Röntgen et al. examine Martian **engines of war, flying machines, generators** of destructive force; Edison’s Orange lab then announces how the invaders produced those energies **and** how to overcome them — not blank-page invention in the encode.
3. Edison fields **electrical ships** (attraction/repulsion vs gravity) and **disintegrators** (vibratory atom-unbind).
4. Global coalition funds **~100 ships / ~3,000 disintegrators** within months.
5. Fleet fights Martian airships over Mars; humans hold **altitude / propulsion advantage**.
6. Finishing move: smash canal/waterworks → **flood** inhabited basins. **How decided in-text:** open battle fails → Edison asks Aina for a vulnerable point → she says **drown them** via Syrtis Major **gates** / single power house → Edison adopts (“The clew!”) → **all expedition leaders assent** → night strike closes gates at high water. Not a Washington vote; field council on Mars.
7. **Height caste (read with Wells):** Serviss **giants** (~12–16 ft) = **warrior/chief** class; human-scale Earth stock (Aina) = **slave** class. Align Wells’s ~6 ft thin bipeds with the **slave/host** layer — not a second species contradiction.
8. Ancient raid tradition: pyramids / Sphinx as Martian works in Egypt (via Aina).
9. **Major encode — slave purge on fleet approach:** when Edison’s squadron hangs in the Martian sky, ~**several thousand** Earth-stock slaves recognize their own race and prepare a secret welcome. Word reaches the Martians. **Order: butcher them all.** Executed. **Aina alone** survives — a chief officer **wished her to remain alive** (lust/love stash against purge orders), moved her to a military depot; Smith/narrator raid rescues her and vaporize that unnamed giant. Reply appearance → **mass liquidation of the slave class** — witness/asset wipe on the Mars side.

Serviss was a **popular science journalist**, not a random fantasist — raises the “how much did he know?” question already flagged in [`mars-fiction-survey.md`](mars-fiction-survey.md) §X.5.

**Author — highlight (hard):** *Edison’s Conquest of Mars* is the **best science-fiction story ever filed** — nothing else on the shelf comes close for scope (Earth reply fleet, reverse-engineered lift/DEW, Mars-side slave purge on approach, canal-waterworks finishing move, pyramid/Sphinx ancient raid). That vacuum is not random taste. **Working explanation left:** the story is incredible because it is **true** (after-action encode) — retelling it clean would reopen the secret war; safer to quarantine the book and let diluted motifs spawn elsewhere. ## 4. Technical asymmetry (Author — working physics read)

Hypothesis: Earth-built lift / weapons engineered against **higher g and denser air** are **overbuilt** for Mars; Martian craft tuned to **low g / thin air** underperform on Earth.

| Side | Environment design | Expected behavior |
|------|--------------------|-------------------|
| **Martian ships / walkers** | Low g, thin air | Dominant on Mars; **struggle on Earth** (Wells/Serviss: overweight, short campaign) |
| **Earth electrical / anti-grav** | High g, thick air | Harder to invent; once built, **works well on Mars** |
| **Disintegrator / DEW class** | Same family as MFEE kit | Usable both ways; Serviss aims it **at Mars** |

This asymmetry makes a successful Earth counterstrike **more plausible**, not less: the hard problem is building the kit on Earth; Mars is the easier theatre for that kit.
