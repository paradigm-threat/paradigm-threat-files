## History — a movement that barely existed until modern times

The Flat Earth fight online feels ancient. The historical record says otherwise.

Educated medieval authorities overwhelmingly treated Earth as spherical. The story that the Church taught a flat Earth was invented later — Washington Irving's fictional Columbus scene, nineteenth-century conflict-thesis polemics — not recovered medieval doctrine. The modern Flat Earth movement starts with Samuel Rowbotham's "Parallax" work (**Bedford Level**, 1838 onward; published **1849**) and accelerates through **internet-era revival**, not through cathedral curriculum. The [Flat Earth investigation — Part One](https://paradigmthreat.net/cosmos/flat_earth/flat-earth-investigation.md) holds the primary-history receipts; Part Two notes **refraction limits** on what ground observation alone can prove about shape — a different problem from the recruit's flat-disk landing.

Much of what Flat Earthers cite is misread older material. Ancient firmament and Absu language pointed at a former plasma sheath and sealed sky in Saturnian cosmology — a structure that used to exist and no longer does in today's open sky. Sixteenth-century Spanish maps with a southern land barrier reflected unknown geography and map fashion among elites who already knew circumnavigation, not a doctrinal flat disk. Collinear Golden Age configuration described an observer's plane with fixed up and down along a cosmic axis — a sky memory, not a claim that the planet is a pancake.
