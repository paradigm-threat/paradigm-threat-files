## My friend's debunk — and the trance it cannot break

Today's article is inspired by watching my friend fight Flerfers on social media. Under an Apollo-adjacent video, a hardcore Flat Earther insisted that gravity is fake and **density** is the real explanation: if gravity were real, a feather would slam down like a stone. My friend answered the way serious debunkers usually do. In air, drag matters — that is why the feather floats. In a vacuum, light and heavy objects fall at the same rate. **Density** describes how tightly matter is packed; it is not a force that pulls things downward. The Flat Earther came back with the usual dodge: have you done that experiment yourself, or did lying scientists tell you? My friend named the fallacy — appeal to personal incredulity dressed up as rigor.

## History — a movement that barely existed until modern times

The Flat Earth fight online feels ancient. The historical record says otherwise.

Educated medieval authorities overwhelmingly treated Earth as spherical. The story that the Church taught a flat Earth was invented later — Washington Irving's fictional Columbus scene, nineteenth-century conflict-thesis polemics — not recovered medieval doctrine. The modern Flat Earth movement starts with Samuel Rowbotham's "Parallax" work in 1849 and accelerates through internet-era revival, not through cathedral curriculum. The [Flat Earth investigation — Part One](https://paradigmthreat.net/cosmos/flat_earth/flat-earth-investigation.md) holds the primary-history receipts.

Much of what Flat Earthers cite is misread older material. Ancient firmament and Absu language pointed at a former plasma sheath and sealed sky in Saturnian cosmology — a structure that used to exist and no longer does in today's open sky. Sixteenth-century Spanish maps with a southern land barrier reflected unknown geography and map fashion among elites who already knew circumnavigation, not a doctrinal flat disk. Collinear Golden Age configuration described an observer's plane with fixed up and down along a cosmic axis — a sky memory, not a claim that the planet is a pancake.
