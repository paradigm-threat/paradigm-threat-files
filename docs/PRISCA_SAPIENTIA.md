# Prisca sapientia — epistemic foundation of Paradigm Threat

**Timeline article:** [Prisca sapientia](/timeline/evt-prisca-sapientia) (`content/00.overview/00.01.02-prisca-sapientia.md` in **paradigm-threat-timeline**).

**Prisca sapientia** (Latin: “ancient wisdom”) names the **default assumption** behind Paradigm Threat investigations and reader-facing articles: the **ancients** possessed a **vast, profound** understanding of the **universe**, **nature**, and **theology** that was **subsequently lost or degraded**. Later institutions—especially **modern consensus science**, **licensed medicine**, **chronology**, and **state-aligned history**—are read as **often wrong, incomplete, or captured**, not as automatic upgrades over what came before.

This is a **historical and philosophical** stance, not a claim that every ancient text is literally correct on every point. Investigations **still tier evidence** (primary, secondary, author thesis, speculative). **Prisca sapientia** sets **which direction the burden of proof leans**: when old and new conflict, **degradation and redaction** are **live hypotheses**, not crankery by default.

## What it implies in practice

**Recovery, not nostalgia.** The work is to **reconstruct** what was lost—pyramid function, terrain medicine, chronology, cosmology, soul science—using archaeology, witnesses, Fomenko/New Chronology mirrors, anticontagion experiment archives, and cross-linked dossiers. The goal is not to **freeze** the past but to **recover** usable truth modernity buried.

**Anti–chronological snobbery.** C. S. Lewis’s *chronological snobbery* is the error of assuming the latest fashion is truer. Paradigm Threat **inverts the default**: novelty must **earn** trust against a **prisca** baseline.

**Compatible threads in this repo.** Terrain vs germs (Béchamp, Roytas), pyramid shelter/hospital until the **11th century**, Rus–Horde chronology, ætheric disease, captured technology (well → utility, ward → metered medicine), Flexner gatekeeping, radiation cure suppression, [**magic as operator technology**](MAGIC_AS_OPERATOR_TECHNOLOGY.md) (recovered “magic” is degraded tech, not the supernatural), and **prisca sapientia** as the **umbrella** that explains **why** those lanes belong in one project.

## Explicit non-Jungian method (author lock, 2026-07-14)

The author **does not subscribe to Carl Jung’s proposals** and this project does **not** use the **collective unconscious**, inherited psychic images, or Jungian archetypal psychology to explain recurring myths. Jung lacked the planetary-catastrophe context used here and, in the author’s view, reduced precise ancestral testimony to projections of a comparatively childish mind. Paradigm Threat starts from the opposite possibility: ancestors were intelligent witnesses preserving difficult observations in durable narrative, ritual, image, and name.

When this repository uses **archetype**, it means a recurring **comparative role or pattern**—warrior, dragon, good king, dying god—not a Jungian mechanism. In the Saturnian lane, repeated and unusually specific motifs are tested first as possible descriptions of a shared, witnessed sky event. “We’re doing better” means restoring context and treating ancestors as observers, not declaring every resemblance proved; source quality and evidence tiers still apply.

See [Common Questions, Q3](/history/common-questions.md#q3-do-all-indigenous-civilizations-really-share-the-same-origin-story-or-are-the-similarities-just-universal-human-patterns) and the [myth → planet remap registry](/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/myth-planet-remap-registry.md).
