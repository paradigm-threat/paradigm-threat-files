## Strategy: `Pluralistic overlap` (trap-the-trap)

**Named strat:** **`Pluralistic overlap`** — calmly suggest there may be **legitimate overlap** with the opponent they exist to debunk; ask for **minimal on-record agreement** (one sentence, one issue). CO agents often become **extremely uncomfortable** and refuse to agree in any form — revealing **rigid promotion scope** and **anti-coalition** incentives.

### When to use

- Opponent is ** rigidly focused** on one promotion (DEW, anti-Gage, CO label) per their own words.
- They **cite** fringe-adjacent positions in writing (no-planes, COVID hoax, legal docket) but **attack** mainstream holders of the same positions (Gage).
- They demand you explain **“why”** but won’t state **why** themselves.
- You need **data for readers** on CO behavior, not conversion.

### How to run it (step-by-step)

**1. Pick 2–3 narrow overlaps only** — not “do you agree with Gage overall.” Pick **atomic** claims:

- *“Planes did not **destroy** the towers — yes or no?”* (narrow no-planes)
- *“Was COVID used as pretext to eject dissidents from truth orgs — yes or no?”* (if they run Jerm/Gage COVID threads)
- *“Was Wood’s qui tam **dismissed with prejudice** — yes or no?”* (if they cite legal proof — forces court reality vs book marketing)

**2. Ask for minimal praise, not alliance**

- *“Name **one sentence** Gage got right — one only.”*
- *“Would you put **one line** in your footer praising him on no-planes, since you share it?”*

**3. Hold the domino explicitly (disarm the trap fear)**

- *“I'm not asking you to accept CD or thermite — **only** this one narrow point.”*
- *“If you agree on no-planes only, we can still disagree on DEW — **yes or no on this slice only**.”*

When they still refuse, the **refusal** is the clip — not their concession.

**4. Mirror their written corpus**

- *“Your Substack links Camp 3 / Jerm / no-planes — Gage holds the **narrow** version. Why is he wrong on the **same** narrow point?”*

**5. Record the contradiction**

- **“Controlled demolition” slip** (~36:12) vs **no-CD** (~45 min) — same session.
- **Demands why** (~6:03) vs **“what does it matter?”** on B7 (~48:13).

**6. Do not chase infinite dominos yourself** — one or two overlap pins, then pivot to **parsimony** or **walk-off**. You indulged Gage block ~10 min; **3–4 min** is enough for CO data.

### Expected CO responses (Norman catalog)

| Response | What it means |
|----------|----------------|
| **Movement vs organization** | Taxonomy fog |
| **“You defend Gage”** | Burden inversion |
| **“Exactly” without naming Gage** | Align without credit — keeps debunk brand |
| **“Just like you”** | Mirror deflect |
| **Wrong-lane agree** (“CD”) then later **no-CD** | Contradiction bank for article |
| **Book shield** | Exit from overlap question |
| **“Only here to debunk you”** | Mission statement — not truth coalition |

### Why this works for **your** readers

- Shows CO agents **aren’t** building the coalition they pretend to want.
- Shows **fracture engine** logic: agreement is **more dangerous to them** than looking uninformed.
- Gives **actionable CX** without requiring readers to master DEW physics.
- Pairs with **`It's not manic, it's America!`** (jurisdiction) and **book shield** (homework exit).

### Discipline / failure modes (your side)

| Risk | Fix |
|------|-----|
| Sound like you **are** defending Gage overall | Frame as **“narrow slice only”**; you can still call AE911 CO |
| **10 min Gage filibuster** | Cap overlap block; move to parsimony |
| Audience thinks **you** want Norman-Gage alliance | State explicitly: *“I’m testing whether you’re honest about shared ground”* |
| They say **one** agree then you pile 10 follow-ups | They’re right to fear trap — **honor one slice** or you become the CO |
