## 2. Validation and Likelihood by Investigation

### Jonestown / CIA Latin America pattern

- **Validated:** Ryan killed at Port Kaituma; mass deaths at Jonestown same cluster — mainstream. Horman/Teruggi Chile; U.S. role in court findings — documented. Hasenfus / Iran–Contra supply chain — documented. Salvadoran churchwomen; U.S. training of Salvadoran military — documented.
- **Dead ends:** Single unified “CIA ran Jonestown” proof line — not in this file. La Penca attribution — contested multiple ways; do not cite Wikipedia alone.
- **Likelihood:** Disputed narratives around Jonestown (Holsinger, SDSU corpus) deserve archive work — **medium** as research priority. Cross-case “observation only” rhetoric vs structural U.S. responsibility — **high** as pattern where courts/commissions have spoken.

### CIA Investigation

- **Validated:** OSS→CIA continuity (1942–1947) is mainstream fact. National Security Act 1947, broad authorities, limited oversight — documented. Iran Operation Ajax (1953), Shah restoration — documented. Latin America (Contras, Noriega-era) — documented in part. WTC 7 housed a CIA facility — **validated** (NYT 4 Nov 2001, CBS).
- **Dead ends:** No smoking gun that “NSA took out the CIA” on 9/11; only structural and post-hoc interpretation. “Revelation of the method” (Iran nuke handover script) is unfalsifiable from public evidence.
- **Likelihood (objective):** OSS/CIA continuity and extra-constitutional design: **high**. CIA involvement in drug trade, regime change, and cover-up: **high** (documented in parts). WTC 7 destruction as deliberate decapitation of CIA: **possible but unproven**. Antibody (NSA vs CIA) as institutional design: **speculative**. Iran end-game script: **speculative**.

### British Divide-Conquer Israel

- **Validated:** British Mandate, Balfour, PEF Survey, partition, 19th-c. “Palestine” naming — mainstream. Belgian hardening of Tutsi/Hutu, Radcliffe partition, colonial census/identity — documented. Recurrence table (Israel, India/Pakistan, Rwanda, etc.) — factually sound.
- **Dead ends:** “Deep State ordered from remote location” is not falsifiable. Semyenko source not yet traced.
- **Likelihood:** Colonial divide-and-rule as deliberate policy: **high**. British Empire as primary instrument: **high**. Same playbook across regions: **high**. Deep State as single remote architect: **possible but not provable**; conclusion in doc (“high”) refers to pattern consistency, not to proof of a single controller.

### Soul Science Investigation

- **Validated:** First-references table (Egyptian, Homer, Thales, Plato, Aristotle, Descartes, La Mettrie) — all checkable; marked validated. Reformation as watershed for soul/mind — partially validated (conditions). Russian *dusha* and Western embarrassment — validated. Containment as deliberate strategy — **speculative** (no smoking gun).
- **Dead ends:** “When exactly soul = fantasy” requires fine-grained lexical history. Containment thesis may remain suggestive only.
- **Likelihood:** Soul→mind semantic shift and Reformation/Enlightenment role: **high**. Containment of Russian literature via soul-as-fantasy: **possible**, consistent with thesis; evidence is circumstantial.

### 9/11 Investigation (Good-Actor / WTC 7)

- **Validated:** WTC 7 housed CIA secret site — **validated** (NYT, CBS). Gulf of Tonkin second attack fabricated — **validated** (NSA declassification). NIST WTC 7 “fire-induced collapse” contested — documented (AE911Truth, etc.).
- **Dead ends:** “Good actors executed 9/11 to destroy CIA” is interpretive; no direct proof. No-planes/CGI thesis is unfalsifiable from public evidence. Controlled demolition — contested; not resolved here.
- **Likelihood:** WTC 7 as CIA facility: **certain**. Its destruction as intentional decapitation: **possible**, structurally consistent with good-actor thesis. Good-actor thesis overall: **speculative** — internally coherent but not provable.

### US Civil War

- **Validated:** British blockade runners, Confederate raiders built in UK, British financial flows — documented. USCT recruitment, demographics, use in combat — documented. Slavery as instrument (emancipation as military measure) — supported by scholarship.
- **Dead ends:** DU weapon thesis is **dead end** for conventional validation — no 1865 DU ordnance in archives; thesis depends on Verne-as-disclosure and timeline’s declassification model. Author clarification still needed.
- **Likelihood:** British involvement and slavery as instrument: **high**. Northern freedmen as cannon fodder: **high**. DU weapon ending the war: **very low** on mainstream evidence; **only plausible** if one accepts timeline’s “weapons declassified not invented” framework.
