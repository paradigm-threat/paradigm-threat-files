# Bilbo Mound and America's buried past

America's burial mounds were catastrophe graves from a recent strike—not slow ceremonial earthworks

![American burial mounds — Bilbo Mound, MFEE catastrophe graves, and the substitute narrative](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/investigations/american-burial-mounds-mfee-smithsonian-national-parks-investigation-bilbo-mound.png)

Bilbo Mound is a real hill east of Savannah, Georgia, where the Savannah River meets the sea. Archaeologists file it as site **9CH4**; you can visit the fenced rise in the swamp east of downtown. It is the starting point for an investigation into what was hidden, redacted, and whitewashed.

If you are thinking about a group trip to Bilbo specifically to investigate my claims, keep in mind that **the hill has already been picked over.** For almost a century, archaeologists, looters, and city crews have dug it, fenced it, drained it, and written the official story about what they found. You are not walking up to a sealed tomb. You are walking up to what is left after the interesting parts were removed.

You can still go—but the dirt will not settle the argument. Whatever could have settled it is **gone**: dug up, hauled away, filed in a report that already calls the mound a village dump from thousands of years ago. From here on we think: does that story still make sense when the ground cannot talk back? Bilbo is where we start, not what proves the case.

## Bilbo at the mouth of the Savannah River

Official archaeology calls it an old Indian village mound—layers of shell and pottery built up slowly over thousands of years. That reading comes from digs in the 1930s, 1950s, and 2000s, plus years of looting before the site was fenced; the [investigation file](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/investigations/american-burial-mounds-mfee-smithsonian-national-parks-investigation.md#31-site-facts-documented) has the timeline if you want names and dates. The pattern matters more than the date fight: a physical heap at a river mouth gets read as a permanent village, and institutional language turns catastrophe geology into **ceremony** and **slow accumulation**.

![Giant burial mound — earthen rise misread as ritual earthwork](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/giant_burial_mound.jpg)

The mainstream story is incremental: people dumped shell, added earth, held feasts, buried elders. That may be true for some layers. I do not believe it is the root truth for the shape of the continent. Bilbo sits beside a living city. If officials can misname one mound there, they can misname ten thousand across a hemisphere.

That is why this does not stop at Bilbo. The same heap shape repeats nationwide, and each copy gets its own ancient village or burial legend. Either tens of thousands of unrelated peoples built the same accident, or one recent catastrophe made the heaps and later institutions renamed them as slow ceremony. I read the second. You cannot test that read on one dug-over hill in Savannah—you have to name the planet-scale strike I believe formed them, then see whether the official labels still hold.

## The Mud Flood Energetic Event — what it was and why it happened

That late-eighteenth-century date is not mine alone. **[Anatoly Fomenko](https://chronologia.org/en)** and **Gleb Nosovskiy** tie **1774** to the Pugachev war, the wreckage of Rus-Horde Tartary, and maps that were redrawn within a generation. **[Nikolai Morozov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolai_Morozov)** argued that much of “ancient” history was compressed fiction. **[Heribert Illig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phantom_time_hypothesis)** attacked the AD calendar itself. Earlier critics—from **Jean Hardouin** through **Edwin Johnson**—accused church chronologists of forging a deep past on purpose. They do not agree on every detail, and mainstream historians reject most of them. What they share is simpler: **history as taught today is a whitewash.** None of them, as far as I know, assembled the full puzzle—burial mounds as catastrophe graves, off-world directed energy, Smithsonian-era cleanup—because each worked under local political constraints, language barriers, and career risk. Fomenko could not say from Moscow what we can say from Savannah. Putting the pieces together is our job, if we think we are free to say it. The [chronology hub](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology) and [MFEE investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/investigations/mfee-investigation.md) carry the longer author and map spine.

Before **1774** makes sense, you need the longer chain. During the **[Golden Age](https://paradigmthreat.net/timeline/evt-4077-bce-the-golden-age)** the inner planets rode one collinear stack—high energy, high technology, humans moving between worlds when the sky geometry aligned. **[Atlantis](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/atlantis/theory.md)** in that read was not a cartoon island in the Atlantic: the greatest habitable realm humans could reach was the **north polar region of Mars**, close to the “heaven” of the upper stack. Earth may have had a lesser copy; Mars had the real thing. When the configuration broke, Atlantis on Mars took the same **Great Deluge** blow as Earth. After that era ended, **humans on Mars became a breakaway civilization**—cut off from easy travel and trade with Earth, desperate for agrarian resources, reinventing old Atlantean survival tech while still meddling in terrestrial religion from a distance.

That is why I say **breakaway human civilization on Mars**, not little green men from another star. Earth and Mars share thousands of years of inner-solar-system history in the record—early astronomers mapping canals and life on Mars, travelogues filed as fiction, a Golden Age when advanced humans were ordinary. Martians in this read are **kin**: humans who endured colder, harsher conditions, often with different skin and stature, with an early head start in metals and weapons—but not a separate species. Until the **1773** war they used cathedrals, telepathy, and “visions” to play God on Earth and steer religious military orders. They could not absorb the **giants** or crush **Pugachev’s rebellion** without escalation.

Near the end of the eighteenth century—around **1774** in the chronology I use—that civilization turned its own directed-energy weapons on Earth. I call that planet-scale strike paired with mud burial the **Mud Flood Energetic Event**, or **MFEE**. Ground levels jumped. Cities drowned in mud to the second floor. Bones mineralized on contact. Heaps that look like “Indian burial mounds”—including Bilbo—are, on this read, **where the strike left the dead**.

No one wanted the full bill: the collateral mud, the damage to Mars’s own biosphere, the scar on Earth still raw two and a half centuries later. Perpetrators, targets, and bystanders were all trapped inside the same event. When the dust settled, survivors were sorted into **promised lands**, histories were rewritten, and languages were flattened so the catastrophe could be forgotten. The [MudFlood hub](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood) spells out the Deep State, Pugachev, and promised-land reset in more detail.

Mars still shows up in culture as a blank for robots and fantasies, not as a neighbor world of **human kin** that **fired** and then **bled**—because that story breaks too many scripts at once. Earth paid in mud, shortened lineages, and the need to pretend the eighteenth century was only waistcoats and tea. For Mars biosphere cost and displaced-soul flow toward Earth, see [death on Mars](https://paradigmthreat.net/cosmos/mars/death-on-mars-analysis.md) and the [Mars–Earth reincarnation thread](https://paradigmthreat.net/cosmos/mars/investigations/mars-earth-reincarnation-investigation.md).

## Instantaneous burial — tools, cattle, and herds together

On the MFEE read, the burial mounds were **instantaneous**: one pulse of death and burial, not platforms planned over centuries or processional astronomy unless those lines are what a mass die-off looks like after the fact. The giants and their herds huddled together—people, animals, nowhere left to run. They died in one pulse and were buried in one pulse. That is why excavators find tools still in the strata, cattle bones mixed with human remains, whole households compressed into a hillock where a later historian imagines **grave goods** and **ritual placement**.

The simpler read is mass mortality: everyone who stood together when the sky and ground turned hostile stayed together under the mud. Petrification and burial were not separate acts spaced by seasons; they were the same event from different angles—electric strike, mineralization, heap. The [mechanism file](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/investigations/american-burial-mounds-mfee-smithsonian-national-parks-investigation.md#5-mfee-mechanism--instantaneous-burial-and-fossilization) holds the technical chain; here I state the human picture. A conical hill with bones too friable to lift intact may be a mass grave that was never meant to be dug at all.

## From Wisconsin to Ohio to the continent — then the world

Spread out from Bilbo and the same mislabel repeats across the country. Wisconsin lost long animal-shaped earthworks—serpent, bird, turtle lines—bulldozed for farms and suburbs while locals kept lists of what vanished. Ohio’s Serpent Mound still stands under state protection, but the park caption treats it as ancient ceremony and never mentions electric scarring or burial in a single afternoon. Huge platform mounds recall the scale of Cahokia, the great mound city near St. Louis; ring-shaped shell heaps line the Atlantic and Gulf coasts; conical hills cover the Mississippi valley and the Southeast. Archaeology files all of that under period names like Adena, Hopewell, and Mississippian—many labels for one physical fact: **a heap where a heap should not be if only small bodies and patient seasons built the world**. The [mound types survey](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/investigations/american-burial-mounds-mfee-smithsonian-national-parks-investigation.md#4-american-mound-landscape--types-and-distribution) in the investigation breaks down the map.

## Why one fit is not enough — but the stack is

**MFEE is extremely hard to prove to a regular person** if you isolate one hill and demand a laboratory demonstration of eighteenth-century directed energy. Fair. Most people have never heard of Bilbo. Most have never read the Smithsonian’s mound reports from the 1880s, when archaeologist [Cyrus Thomas](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/investigations/american-burial-mounds-mfee-smithsonian-national-parks-investigation.md#6-smithsonian-and-bureau-of-ethnology--documented-mound-program) led the Bureau of American Ethnology’s nationwide dig program. Most meet Serpent Mound on a school field trip and go home with a coloring sheet.

What changed my mind was not one hill. It was **fits**—many independent lines that point the same direction until the ritual-burial story looks like a substitute narrative, not science.

The Smithsonian Institution and its Bureau of American Ethnology **systematically dismantled** mound fields across the eastern United States in the 1880s and 1890s—complete excavations, bones shipped or “too soft to preserve,” earthworks erased while railroads and farms had already taken their share. That is documented. Separately, nineteenth-century newspapers reported oversized skeletons in mound contexts again and again. A 2014 satire headline about the Smithsonian “admitting” giant destruction is **fake**; I name that once so nobody wastes a day on it. The satire does not erase the **real** dismantling program.

The Grand Canyon and similar terrain look less like slow water and more like a discharge scar. A vast inland empire—Tartary, Horde, call it what you will—sits **missing** from the authorized past while today’s politics often feel like a second act of the same play. None of those lines alone closes the case in a courtroom. Together they read like witnesses who never met but tell the same story: **something enormous happened recently**, and the gentle prehistory we teach children is a cover story pasted over it. That cover story is the only way the Smithsonian’s behavior looks innocent **if** the mounds were only Indian cemeteries—and I do not believe they were only that.

## The scar we pass on — and why the next generation needs better answers

We are still recovering. The scar is not only in the dirt. It is in institutions that cannot tolerate an alternate past. It is in the comfort people take from a history that is **long, mostly uneventful, and full of endless wars with no solutions**—because that story feels stable even when it is empty. Teach deep time, teach cyclical war, teach that serious people do not ask about the hill behind the supermarket, and curiosity gets treated as a threat.

I have heard the circular exits my whole life. **“I don’t know.”** **“Go read a book.”** **“You’re not qualified.”** **“Go to school.”** Those answers do not lead forward; they send you back to the same dead end. The truth comes from asking basic questions and **not stopping** until you get real answers—not until someone with a badge says the subject is closed.

The next generation will not stay in that loop. That is why I care about compiling the exact history—Bilbo, Wisconsin, the Smithsonian trail, the maps, the scar—so we do not hand our children the same non-answers our parents handed us. Stability bought by forgetting is not stability; it is silence until the next lie.

## Pro-American means respecting who was here first

To be **pro-American** is to respect the ancestry that made America possible—not the cartoon of cowboys and Indians sold to children, but the **real** populations erased when the mounds were leveled, when the bones were reclassified, when survivors were compressed into census buckets that flattered a simpler story. The virtues that found their way into our Constitution and Bill of Rights did not fall from a marketing deck. They came through lineages that remembered law, dignity, and scale—lineages the substitute narrative calls primitive so it never has to say **recent catastrophe**.

Until the destruction, annihilation, and erasure of those real natives is **named and taught**—until children learn what was buried under the golf course and the subdivision—patriotism is performance. Anyone who denies the **potential reality** of these mounds, or shows no interest in digging for America’s actual past, is in effect **anti-American**: not because they hate a flag, but because they refuse the ground the flag stands on.

## Why nothing else fits

If the heaps were only slow ceremonial earthworks, the Smithsonian’s program looks like bad archaeology but not a **memory war**. If the giants reported in the press were only hoaxes, the dismantling still needs a motive beyond science. If the maps had not changed, if the Grand Canyon had not appeared as a scar, if the empire called Tartary were not scrubbed from the classroom ([MFEE file](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/investigations/mfee-investigation.md)), you could call me curious and leave it there.

But the ritual-burial frame and the catastrophe frame **exclude each other** on cause, time, and agency. You cannot have incremental Archaic religion **and** a single eighteenth-century strike that petrified and buried everyone standing together. When institutions then **protect** the ritual label with park law, **criminalize** independent excavation, and **teach** deep prehistory to children before they meet their grandparents’ stories, the behavior matches **substitute narrative enforcement**—not neutral heritage work.

No public agency publishes “quantum mud weapon from Mars, 1774” in a press release. Classification and ridicule are how operational history stays off the lesson plan. That absence is not proof the event did not happen. It is proof the event was **too large to admit** without unwinding the present.
