# They were already in the water

Gill humans may have lived in the ocean already, and taken the cities only after they sank

The rapid growth of gills on drowned people can be entirely wrong. The conditions for that change may never have been there. Until a document turns up that says it happened, there is no reason to assume it.

People who already had gills may already have lived in the ocean. After the cities sank, those people moved in. They do not stand for the original inhabitants. The people who built the cities stayed drowned. That account stays open beside the other one.

## The mud flood

The sinking sits inside a larger wreck. Around 1774, in the years of the Pugachev war and the breakup of the Horde, the places aimed at were hit. Mars fired more than once. The weapons heated the surface. The energy built up inside the hollow Earth. It broke out at the weak places. That breakout is the secondary discharge, and it left the volcanic belt called the Ring of Fire. Mars was in opposition. The same energy went back and struck Mars. The catastrophe there was worse.

Cities went under mud. Ground floors disappeared. Some cities went under the sea. On the places most targeted, the dark lasted years. The rain that carried land debris lasted much less, and it did not have to fall the whole time. A dark sky can remain after the drops stop.

[Anatoly Fomenko and Gleb Nosovskiy](https://chronologia.org/en) tie 1774 to that war and to maps redrawn within a generation. The [mud flood page](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/page.md) carries the weapons, the hollow Earth, the secondary discharge, the return stroke, and the cities left under the Pacific. [The city in the sea](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/the-city-in-the-sea.md) is the poem, the 1965 volcano, and the long dark on those same places. [Bilbo Mound and America's buried past](https://paradigmthreat2.substack.com/p/bilbo-mound-and-americas-buried-past) is the mounds read as graves from the same strike. The maps that change after the wreck are in the [map file](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/investigations/mfee-investigation.md).

This page uses that wreck only as the date of the sinking. It does not add a new reading of Mars.

## The change that is not on paper

[The city in the sea](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/the-city-in-the-sea.md) wrote the other account in full. People in these cities drowned. The energy revived some of them. The body played back gills from a memory it already held, from a time when conditions were more ideal. The hardiest, and the ones who still held that memory, had the best chance. The ones who failed stayed drowned.

No parish book, letter, or medical note has turned up that says this. A film, a pact, a plant, and a children’s game are not that note. Until the note exists, the change does not get assumed. The conditions for it may simply not have been there.

## Already in the ocean

The other account is simpler. A people who breathed water already lived in the ocean. They were there before these cities went down. The city on land sank. Its people drowned and stayed drowned. After the streets were underwater, the gill people moved in.

They lived in the ruins. They did not build them. A later visitor who finds gill people in a drowned city is not looking at the original inhabitants. The original inhabitants are the ones who did not get back up.

This page does not say the cities were built underwater. The cities in this account stood on land. The sea took them. The gill people came after.

## What the stories do with them

The stories hand the city to the gill people, or they turn land people into gill people.

In the 1965 film [*City Under the Sea*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War-Gods_of_the_Deep), the American title [*War-Gods of the Deep*](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/moviedetails/22802), the gill-men are the builders. The Captain uses them. The plot that reached theaters gives them the city.

In [*The Shadow over Innsmouth*](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/soi.aspx) the sea people are already under Devil Reef. A land family joins them by a pact and by blood, and the change takes years. The story’s own sea people were already there. The gills on the land family are how the story lets the land family become them.

In [*Black Panther: Wakanda Forever*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Panther:_Wakanda_Forever) (2022) the film’s own cause is a vibranium plant and a flight from the Spanish. The water-breathers are changed land people.

[*E.V.O.: Search for Eden*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E.V.O.:_Search_for_Eden) (1992) lets a child buy new body parts. Crystals speed the change. That is a game.

None of those is a record of a drowned person growing gills in the flood. The film that calls the gill-men the builders is assigning them the city. The open account does not.

## What is still open

Both accounts stay open. One says the energy grew gills on some of the drowned. One says the gill people were already in the ocean and entered the city after it sank, and that they are not the original inhabitants.

The second one is the possibility this page is here to keep. The first one is not assumed. A document that names water-breathing people in the sea before the sinking would bear on the second. A document that says a drowned person grew gills would bear on the first. Neither page has turned up.

## Framing and limits

This page assumes [prisca sapientia](https://paradigmthreat.net/docs/PRISCA_SAPIENTIA.md): later institutions lost or captured an older understanding of the world, and modern consensus is not the default. The mud flood, as the condition of the sinking, is the account already written on the mud flood page. The poem texts and the film plots are on the record in the city essay. The claim that drowned people grew gills is not assumed here. The claim that gill humans already lived in the ocean and entered the cities after the sinking is open. Neither is a parish record. The longer split sits in the [investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/mudflood/investigations/gill-people-already-in-the-ocean-investigation.md).
