### 3.1 DNA lab report — Helix P-234 (archived)

**Primary file (Spurr-hosted):** [mfutools.com — `Modern_Human_size_and_Giants_found_DNA_LAB_REPORT.docx`](https://mfutools.com/Modern_Human_size_and_Giants_found_DNA_LAB_REPORT.docx) (same title family as the [Academia upload](https://www.academia.edu/16914891/Modern_Human_size_and_Giants_found_toghether_DNA_Lab_report)). Local extract: investigator archive `wget/mudflood/mudfossil-university/helix/helix-biolab-report-P-234-2015.txt`.

| Field | From report text |
|-------|------------------|
| **Lab** | **Helix Biological Laboratory**, 28044 James Dr, Warren, MI 48092; `info@helixbiolab.com`; phone **586-806-4243** — matches live [helixbiolab.com](https://helixbiolab.com/) / [ancient DNA services](https://helixbiolab.com/ancient-dna-analysis/). |
| **Director** | Results “verified by **Tom Prychitko**, Laboratory Director.” Sole-proprietor bio on Helix site mirrors. |
| **Customer / project** | **Roger Spurr**; Project **P-234** |
| **Dates** | Received **2015-06-20**; testing/analysis **2015-07-01 through 2015-09-17** |
| **Samples** | **36 inch tip**; **Lung**; **Mud Tip** |
| **Methods** | Gene Clean Ancient DNA Extract Kit (MP Bio); proteinase K 56°C 24h; nanodrop; PCR with **Alonso et al. 2003** mtDNA primer sets (~**100 bp** amplicons); agarose gel; amplicons sent to **Eton Biosciences Inc. (Union, New Jersey)** for Sanger sequencing; NCBI **BLAST** |
| **PCR summary** | A1/A1R: all three samples **positive**, negative control **negative**. 2F/2R and 4F/4R: tip + lung **positive**, mud tip **negative**. |
| **BLAST (tip + lung)** | Primer A1 → *Homo sapiens* mitochondrial **Cytochrome b**; primer 4F → *Homo sapiens* mitochondrial **D-loop**. Report states ~**80–100 bp** of usable sequence. |
| **Yale** | **Not mentioned** in this document. |
| **DOCX media (archived)** | Helix logo; agarose gel (matches lane narrative); **four chromatogram PNGs**; one WMF (unconverted this pass). Path: local `wget/.../helix/docx-media/`. |
| **Independent BLAST recheck** | Sequence taken from chromatogram `image3.png` (~80 bp clean region) submitted to NCBI BLASTN/nt (**RID `5HJPCABD014`**, 2026-07-16): top hits *H. sapiens* **D-loop** / **CYTB** at **96–98%** identity. **Confirms** Helix’s human-mtDNA BLAST class for that trace; **does not** establish antiquity or rule out contamination. |

**Lab is real; interpretation is contested.** Helix markets paleo/aDNA work to the public. A paid PCR+BLAST report that returns human mtDNA on short amplicons is **not** the same as authenticated ancient human DNA from a giant soft-tissue fossil.

**Validation / contamination checklist (against P-234 as written):**

| Issue | Why it matters |
|-------|----------------|
| **Very high DNA yields** | Nanodrop lists **~92–300 ng/µL** from “mud fossil” extracts — typical of **modern** DNA-rich samples, not classic ultra-degraded aDNA. |
| **Human-targeted short primers** | Alonso-style ~100 bp mtDNA primers will amplify **handler / lab / soil human** contamination easily; BLAST-to-human then **follows**. |
| **Odd extraction wording** | Report says “**Bacterial DNA** was extracted” while targeting **human mtDNA** markers — sloppy language or kit default; either way, weakens confidence in protocol narrative. |
| **No aDNA authentication suite** | No reported damage patterns, uracil treatment, independent clean-room replication, nuclear markers, or quantification of endogenous vs exogenous. |
| **Mud Tip weaker** | Not BLAST-matched in the sequenced set the way tip/lung are; still counted in Spurr’s “3 tests” marketing. |
| **Eton ≠ Yale** | Sequencing vendor is **Eton Biosciences** (commercial; [etonbio.com](https://www.etonbio.com/)). Conference abstract variants saying “Eaton Biosciences” / “Helix Paleo Lab” are **name drift**, not Ivy League co-authorship. |

**Conference abstract (secondary):** Longdom proceedings listing — Adrian **Husbands** & Roger **Spurr**, *Could geological features be mineralized human remains? What DNA testing reveals* — ties samples to a **2012 Middletown, CT** shallow dig; lung ≈ modern adult size; fingertips scaled to ~**50 ft** and ~**200 ft** beings; mentions Helix + “Eaton” + CAT + **coroner**. Page was flaky (522); treat as **conference-series abstract**, not peer-reviewed geology. Aligns with Spurr’s Connecticut residence and 2012 origin story. **Adrian Husbands** could not be firmly linked to mudfossil work this pass (a Dundee medical-education Academia profile exists — may be a different person or listing noise).

**Provenance still thin:** Discourse (Jan 2025) “**4 ft wide** left hand” / “36 inch tip”; Academia/OSOM “**10 × 12 hole**”; Longdom “shallow construction dig” in **Middletown, CT** (city only — no GPS). Continuous custody photos **not** in hand.

## 4. Yale, Harvard, and academia outreach

Two **distinct** Spurr narratives appear in public writing. They are **not automatically compatible**; both need documentary chase.

### 4.1 Narrative A — Dismissal without examination (~LinkedIn era)

From Spurr’s LinkedIn article *[Academia's Dirty Little Illegal secret](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/academias-dirty-little-illegal-secret-roger-spurr)* (written when he said he had been “deeply involved… about **3 years**” — consistent with **~2015** if discovery is **2012**):

- Presented evidence (videos/pictures) to **Yale**, **Harvard**, and “dozens more.”
- **Yale** “declared them simple rocks not fossils at all, **without examination**.”
- **Harvard** “polite but dismissive… refused to look into this.”
- After Yale’s rock call, others followed; institutions then “gone **dark**.”
- Claimed **scores** of contact attempts with **zero** responses (email/phone); hung up on by name.
- Frames educators as having **fiduciary** duties to students and hints at legal remedy.

**Documented so far:** Spurr’s own LinkedIn text. **Not documented:** named Yale/Harvard contacts, dated correspondence, departmental stamps, or any Yale publication citing Spurr specimens.

### 4.2 Narrative B — “Yale took credit” (Discourse, Jan 2025)

On the MFU Discourse intro thread ([Hello Friends… Roger Spurr](https://mudfossil.discourse.group/t/hello-friends-this-is-roger-spurr-from-mudfossil-university/63)), Spurr posts (image caption references DNA tests):

> “These are the DNA tests on 3 Mudfossils (2 are Giants) from **10yrs ago** and **Yale took credit for my work**.”

Timing (~10 years before Jan 2025) matches the **2015 Helix P-234** window — but **P-234 never mentions Yale**. Sequencing credit in the report goes to **Eton Biosciences Inc.** (Union, NJ).

**Open conflict with Narrative A:** Dismissal-without-looking ≠ taking credit. Working hypotheses (unproven):

1. Spurr conflates **outreach rejection** with later **soft-tissue / aDNA headlines** (often **Schweitzer / NCSU** dinosaur soft tissue — *not* Yale).
2. A **specific Yale contact** (Peabody / med / imaging) is alleged but never named publicly.
3. Rhetorical escalation; no paper/PI exists.

**This pass still finds no** Yale paper, press release, or PI citing Spurr mudfossils or Helix P-234.
