## 1. Author’s originating thesis

The author, 23 October 2026, after the gill-mutation account had already been written into AU-079 and into the reader essay:

> The rapid mutation of gills could be entirely wrong. It is possible that there simply were not the conditions to allow this to happen. Until some kind of real documentation turns up, there is no reason to assume that it happens. There is another explanation. The people who had gills and lived in the ocean were already there. They only inhabited the underwater sunken cities after the cities were sunk. These gill humans do not represent the original inhabitants. That is still an open possibility.

What that means: the drowned-and-revived account is not the default. A sea people who already breathed water can be the later occupants of a land city that sank. The builders stay the people who drowned and did not get back up.

## 3. Author open claims (AU-080)

Prior art is partial. Innsmouth, the 1965 film, and Talokan each already put a water-breathing people in or under the sea. None of them is a document of gills grown on a drowned person during the flood. The fork below is the author’s.

1. The rapid mutation of gills on the drowned can be entirely wrong. The conditions for that change may not have existed.
2. Until a real document says a drowned person grew gills, that change is not assumed.
3. People who already had gills already lived in the ocean.
4. They inhabited the sunken cities only after the cities sank.
5. Those gill humans are not the original inhabitants. The original inhabitants are the people who stayed drowned.
6. Stories that call the gill people the builders, or that turn land people into them by a pact, a plant, or a game, are later tellings. They are not the missing document.

## 4. What the stories assign

**Documented as fiction, not as a flood record.**

- [*The Shadow over Innsmouth*](https://www.hplovecraft.com/writings/texts/fiction/soi.aspx), written November–December 1931. The change grows over years from a pact and from blood. The people go to Y’ha-nthlei under Devil Reef. The novella’s sea people are already in the water before the narrator changes. The change is how a land family joins them. That is closer to a prior sea people than to a citizen growing gills on the night the city sank. It is still a story. Publication dates and the 30 November 1936 letter stay in AU-079.
- [*City Under the Sea*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War-Gods_of_the_Deep) / [*War-Gods of the Deep*](https://catalog.afi.com/Catalog/moviedetails/22802), 1965. The shipped plot calls the gill-men the builders of the drowned city. The Captain uses them. That plot assigns the city to them. AU-080 does not.
- [*Black Panther: Wakanda Forever*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_Panther:_Wakanda_Forever) (2022). The film’s own cause is a vibranium plant and a flight from the Spanish. The water-breathers on screen are changed land people.
- [*E.V.O.: Search for Eden*](/influence/predictive_programming/investigations/evo-search-for-eden-deep-time-saturnian-cosmology-predictive-programming-investigation.md) (1992). A child buys body parts. Crystals speed the change. That is play. It is not a record of the flood.

**Author:** these four hand the gills to a transformation, or they hand the drowned city to the gill people as its builders. The open account does neither. The gill people were already in the ocean. The city was on land. They moved in after it sank.

## 5. The mud flood as the condition

**Documented as the project’s existing account, not re-argued here.** Around 1774, in the years of the Pugachev war and the breakup of the Horde, the places aimed at were wrecked. Mars fired more than once. The energy built up inside the hollow Earth and broke out at the weak places. That breakout is the secondary discharge and the Ring of Fire. Mars was in opposition, and the same energy returned and struck Mars. Cities went under mud. Some went under the sea. On the targeted places the dark lasted years. The rain that carried land debris lasted less. The reader pages for that sequence are named in [§2](#2-scope).

AU-080 uses that sequence only to date the sinking. It does not add a new observation of Mars, a new map, or a new poem.

## 6. Questions to clarify, verify, or debunk

| # | Question | What would settle it |
|---|----------|----------------------|
| 1 | Did a drowned person grow gills in the flood? | A parish book, letter, medical note, or chronicle that says so. Until that page exists, the mutation account is not assumed |
| 2 | Were gill humans already living in the ocean before these cities sank? | A record that names water-breathing people in the sea before the sinking, separate from the city’s own dead |
| 3 | Did they enter the city after it was underwater? | A record of occupation after the sinking, with the original people still drowned |
| 4 | Which stories show arrival after the sinking, rather than builders who changed? | A title, a year, and the scene. The 1965 film, Innsmouth, Talokan, and *E.V.O.* are already logged, and none of them is that scene |
| 5 | Is any “sunken city” in this file a city that was built underwater? | Out of scope. A find of that kind belongs in another file |
