# Investigation: American Burial Mounds — MFEE Fossilization, Smithsonian Erasure, and National-Park Gatekeeping

> **TL;DR:** North America's earthen mounds — from the Bilbo shell mound at Savannah to Wisconsin effigy complexes and Ohio's Serpent Mound — are framed here not as planned ritual burial architecture but as **instantaneous burial and fossilization sites** from the [MudFlood Energetic Event](/timeline/evt-the-mudflood-world-cataclysm) (MFEE, ~1774). The [Smithsonian Institution](https://www.si.edu/) and its Bureau of American Ethnology **did** conduct systematic mound excavations (1881–1890, Cyrus Thomas); mainstream debunks of **giant-skeleton hoaxes** are real but often **overreach** into dismissing mound destruction, newspaper reports, and the **memory-war** function of federal parks. This dossier cross-reads repo giant/mudflood material, documents general claims and debunks, and tests the author thesis that mound leveling was **evidence erasure** (not cheap housing), that **"building on Indian burial grounds"** was manufactured folklore, and that **national monuments** now **gate** a false pre-Columbian past while keeping independent investigators off sensitive strata.

## 1. Author thesis — not ritual mounds, MFEE catastrophe sites

> **Author (verbatim sense):** The giant mounds found all over America may not be burial mounds as commonly thought, but instead may be the result of the MudFlood Energetic Event. During this event, the giants were wiped out by catastrophic electric discharges from the ground and atmosphere. The giants were too large and there was nowhere to hide. As they were struck down, their bones became instantaneously fossilized, and then buried forming the 'burial' mounds as well as 'dinosaur' fossil sites.

**What this means in repo terms:**

1. **Morphology is misread.** Conical hills, effigy shapes (serpent, turtle, bird), and shell middens are interpreted by archaeology as **intentional earthworks** — ceremonial platforms, staged burials, feasting deposits, astronomical alignments. The paradigm read inverts the causal arrow: the **shape is post-catastrophe**, not pre-planned ritual geometry.
2. **"Burial" is a retrospective label.** Once bodies (giant or otherwise) were **electrically petrified** and **mud-buried** in one pulse, later cultures and 19th-century institutions **named the heaps "mounds"** and **inferred mortuary intent** from bone clusters — the same inference error as reading **dinosaur bone yards** as millions-of-years-old mass deaths rather than **recent electrical mortality**.
3. **Scale matches MFEE geography.** The [MudFlood article](../page.md) notes hundreds of thousands of mound features across western America and eastern Eurasia after the Pugachev-era strike — correlating with the **giant elimination** thesis, not a continent-wide independent invention of identical mound-building religion.
4. **Smithsonian work follows catastrophe.** Institutional mound exploration (~1880s) is read here as **post-event forensic cleanup** — classify, remove, and **re-narrate** remains into **small-body indigenous prehistory** and **deep-time paleontology**, not neutral science.

**Evidence tier:** **Author / speculative** for mechanism; **documented** that mounds exist in vast numbers and were heavily excavated/destroyed in the 19th–20th centuries.

## 2. Bilbo Mound (Savannah, Georgia) — case study

### 2.1 Site facts (documented)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Designation** | Bilbo Site (**9CH4**), Chatham County, GA — mouth of Savannah River |
| **Type** | Shell midden / mound complex in low swampy terrain |
| **Footprint** | ~1,200 m² mound mass; up to ~80 cm above modern grade; base ~2 m below modern surface ([GA DOT Occasional Paper 17](https://www.dot.ga.gov/InvestSmart/Environment/CulturalResources/Pubs/Occasional_Paper_17.pdf)) |
| **Key excavators** | Antonio J. Waring Jr.; William G. Haag (1957, NPS/ONR-funded); UGA field schools (2000s) |
| **Radiocarbon** | Lower sterile clay with charcoal: Haag-era dates **~3540 BC** cited in popular summaries; later compilations also cite **~1870–1780 BC** ranges for midden phases — **dating disputes are documented** in the literature ([Americas Revealed summary](https://apalacheresearch.com/2021/11/06/the-bilbo-mound-and-village-site-in-savannah-georgia-3540-bc/)) |
| **Material culture** | Fiber-tempered pottery (among earliest in region), shell, post molds, canal/pond construction |

**Mainstream read:** Bilbo is a **permanent Late Archaic–Early Woodland village** with **intentional mound accretion** (shell + earth layers), possibly among the **earliest mound-building** in eastern North America — **not** a single-event burial.

**Paradigm read (qualified):** Bilbo's **pond → island → mound** stratigraphy (clay cap, sterile layers, sudden shell heaps) is **compatible with** a **post-MFEE re-occupation story** *or* with mainstream gradualism — the dossier does **not** close that fork on radiocarbon alone. What Bilbo **does** prove for this investigation:

- **Mounds sat in living cities** (Savannah golf course used mounds as obstacles by the 1790s — [Arclein/Thornton blog](https://globalwarming-arclein.blogspot.com/2015/12/bilbo-mound-in-savannah-with-richard.html)).
- **Federal and university archaeology** reframed the site through **centuries of institutional language** ("shell midden," "St. Simons phase") while **commercial and military river engineering** altered the landscape (Savannah River dredging lowered relative elevations).
- **Date controversies** ("Naw, that couldn't be right") show **institutional reluctance** to accept **very early American complexity** — rhyming with the broader **memory-war** pattern even when the paradigm mechanism is not invoked.

### 2.2 Bilbo vs author thesis

| Question | Mainstream | Paradigm (open) |
|----------|------------|-----------------|
| Why is it mound-shaped? | Incremental shell dumping + earth | Instant burial + later shell re-use |
| Why so old in RC years? | Genuine Archaic chronology | RC skew / pre-MFEE vs post-MFEE calibration ([radiocarbon investigation](/history/chronology/investigations/radiocarbon-dating-investigation.md)) |
| Why near Savannah urban core? | Long-term indigenous metropolis | Same — but **giants** may have built the **first** elevated refuges |

## 5. Smithsonian and Bureau of Ethnology — documented mound program

This section is **primary-source grounded** — independent of giant-hoax satire.

### 5.1 Division of Mound Exploration (1881–1890)

| Item | Source |
|------|--------|
| **Mandate** | Smithsonian Bureau of American Ethnology (BAE) **Division of Mound Exploration** |
| **Lead** | **Cyrus Thomas** (1825–1910), archaeologist to BAE from 1882 |
| **Scope** | Systematic mound excavation east of the Mississippi; field agents across ~20 states |
| **Archive** | [NAA MS 2400 — Mound Exploration Records](https://sova.si.edu/record/naa.ms2400) |
| **Published synthesis** | *Report on the Mound Explorations of the Bureau of Ethnology* (1894) — [Gutenberg partial](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/41557/41557-h/41557-h.htm) |

**Documented behaviors:**

- **Destructive excavation** was standard — "thoroughly explored" mounds often meant **complete dismantling** to locate central crypts.
- **Railroad and development** already destroyed many groups before BAE arrival (Thomas notes railroad lines carrying away mound earth).
- **Intellectual outcome:** Thomas argued **mounds were built by ancestors of historic tribes** (including Cherokee), **not** a vanished "Mound Builder race" separate from Native Americans — debunking **one** racist theory while **cementing** another baseline: **small-body indigenous builders**, pre-MFEE in calendar terms.

### 5.2 Hrdlička and the giant-skeleton lane (1934)

**Aleš Hrdlička**, Smithsonian curator of anthropology, publicly rejected **7–8 ft routine giant skeletons** (1934), blaming **amateur mismeasurement** and **"will to believe"** ([Wikipedia — Giant human skeletons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giant_human_skeletons) summarizes the institutional record).

**Documented:** Smithsonian **opposed** the "ancient white Mound Builder" myth.  
**Conspiracy layer (2014 satire):** *World News Daily Report* fake story about Smithsonian admitting destruction of **thousands** of giant skeletons — [Reuters fact-check](https://www.reuters.com/article/fact-check/claims-that-the-smithsonian-destroyed-thousands-of-giant-skeletons-are-many-ye-idUSL1N2ZG1I0/), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/fact-checking-029442241800), [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/smithsonian-giant-skeletons/).

**Paradigm method note:** Satire debunks **one headline**; per [METHODS_DEBUNK_OVERREACH](/docs/METHODS_DEBUNK_OVERREACH_ADJACENT_DISCARD.md), that does **not** automatically debunk:

- documented **mound dismantling**,
- **newspaper giant reports** (tiered separately),
- or the **author thesis** that **real large remains** were **reclassified** (giant → dinosaur, or giant → myth).

## 7. Mound destruction — housing myth vs deliberate erasure

### 7.1 Mainstream narrative

- Thousands of mounds **lost** to **farming, urbanization, railroads, gravel mining** — often **before** legal protection.
- **19th c. settlers** treated mounds as **convenient earth** — not uniquely anti-indigenous malice, but **collateral** to land rush.
- **Smithsonian/BAE** excavations **also destroyed** sites in the name of science.

### 7.2 Author thesis

> **Bottom line:** They were destroyed to **cover up evidence**, not to build cheap housing. The destruction **created** the myth of **"building on Indian burial grounds"** — yes deliberate, but not mere rudeness to a defeated culture; it was **erasing the original version** and replacing it with **cowboys and Indians** and nothing else.

**Unpack:**

1. **Volume timing:** Peak mound destruction overlaps **post-MFEE repopulation** and **federal Indian policy** (reservations from 1758+, Trail of Tears final stage 1830s — [common-questions](/history/common-questions.md)).
2. **Dual use:** Leveling removed **anomalous strata** while supplying fill for **roads and basements** — economic cover, **strategic effect**.
3. **Narrative product:** Once mounds were gone, **only folklore** remained — cursed developments, haunted subdivisions — **discouraging independent digging** without endorsing **giant archaeology**.
4. **Not mutually exclusive:** Farmers **did** want fill; the author claim is about **primary motive ordering** — **memory war first**, convenience second.

## 9. National parks, monuments, and federal gatekeeping

### 9.1 Protected sites as narrative locks

When the federal government **misidentifies** a structure's origin, **protection becomes imprisonment of the error**:

| Mechanism | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| [**Antiquities Act**](https://www.nps.gov/history/archaeology/tools/antiquities-act.shtm) (1906) | Presidential monument designation — **federalizes** interpretation |
| **NPS interpretation** | Official plaques, ranger scripts, **single authorized story** |
| **Archaeological Resources Protection Act (ARPA)** | Criminalizes **unauthorized excavation** — public cannot test plaques |
| [**NAGPRA**](https://www.nps.gov/nagpra/) (1990) | Repatriation frame assumes **indigenous tribal affiliation** — **hard-codes** the identity bucket |
| **World Heritage / UNESCO buffers** | International prestige **raises cost** of paradigm-challenging digs |

**Examples tied to mounds:**

- **Serpent Mound** — Ohio state park / NPS-affiliated interpretation as **Adena/Hopewell ceremonial** earthwork.
- **Cahokia Mounds** — UNESCO site; **Mississippian** narrative locked.
- **Effigy Mounds National Monument** (IA) — federal protection after **massive earlier destruction** — **preserves what survived erasure**, selects **which story** visitors receive.

**Author read:** The **gate opened** means the **official message on plaques and billboards** is the **permitted past**. Federal regulations **keep normal people away** from strata where **independent discovery** might contradict **cowboys-and-Indians prehistory** — not because every ranger knows the cover-up, but because **institutional identifiers** (tribal, Mississippian, Archaic) **channel** all anomalies back into **approved buckets**.

### 9.2 Misidentification cascade

```
MFEE catastrophe site → labeled "burial mound" → protected as "sacred indigenous earthwork"
        → excavation restricted → anomaly burial in museum basements
                → public sees plaque → "ancient Native American ceremony"
```
