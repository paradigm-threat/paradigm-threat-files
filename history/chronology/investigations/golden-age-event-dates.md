## Summary

- **syn.php:** "Between 10,900 BC and 3147 BC, Earth was part of a strange configuration of stacked planets"; "Initially, during a 1500-year period after 10,900 BC..."

A plasma stream from Saturn reached to Earth"; "The last 1000 years of this period is what we call the Era of the Gods"; **3147 BC** end of Era of the Gods, world flood (Cook prefers 3147 over 3114, based on 6 Baktuns before 747 BC Long Count inception).
- **time.php:** "Over the course of the thousand years of Saturn's rule, **4077 BC to 3147 BC**"; "When Saturn went nova in **4077 BC**, the surrounding glow mode coma disappeared and the skies of Earth cleared to also show the Sun"; "a bright crescent started to rotate around the central globe of Saturn on a daily basis"; "As the Earth rotated (below Saturn), the crescent appeared to move counterclockwise"; "The first experience of clock-time had started for humanity"; "**The eight lowerings of Mars** during the 'Era of the Gods' kept the God Horus alive in religious symbolism"; "In **3147 BC**, the home of the Gods was attacked by Jupiter."

**Clue — Earth rotation:** Cook states Earth *was* rotating below Saturn; the crescent's apparent motion gave "clock-time." This started at **4077 BC** when Saturn went nova and the coma dropped. So "Earth started rotating visibly" in the sense of day/night marker = **4077 BC** (same as creation). Whether Earth *began* rotating then vs. merely became visible is ambiguous.

**Clue — Mars lowerings:** "Eight lowerings of Mars" during Era of the Gods (4077–3147) — **no individual dates** for each descent. Pattern continued into Dark Ages (686 BCE final).

### Talbott (wget/talbott, The Saturn Myth)

- **No BCE dates** for Golden Age start/end. Talbott credits Velikovsky with directing him toward Saturn but does not adopt Velikovsky's chronology.
- **Velikovsky citation:** "first Venus and then Mars, in the period **1500–686 B.C.**, so disturbed the Earth's axis" — Talbott is reporting Velikovsky; his own focus is Saturn as polar sun, not Exodus/Venus chronology.
- "Mercury, Venus, Mars, and Jupiter are spaced at the four corners around Saturn" — configuration, no dates.
- "The invisible axis of the earth's rotation"; "daily rotation of the crescent" — describes mechanics, not when rotation began.

### Thornhill (wget/saturniancosmology.org/files/thornhill)

## Talbott Documentary: Canonical Order of Events

**Source:** *Remembering the End of the World* (David Talbott, full documentary), [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oophJNlP-fk). This sequence is the canonical order used across paradigm-threat docs. **It does not appear in *The Saturn Myth* (1980)** — the book focuses on symbols (crescent, enclosure, cosmic mountain) and Saturn as polar sun; it describes "Mercury, Venus, Mars, and Jupiter at the four corners around Saturn," not the nested Venus-center/Mars-inside layout or the step-by-step dynamical sequence below. ### Golden Age (timeless → crescent → destabilization)

1. **Timeless epoch** — No seasons, no day/night. Saturn at pole; Venus in center of Saturn; Mars inside Venus; Jupiter hidden. Diffuse cloud.
2. **Cloud dissipates** — More clearly defined day/night cycle.
3. **Crescent appears** — Bright crescent on Saturn from Sun's light.
4. **Earth rotates** — Crescent visually turns around polar center with Earth's rotation; first clock.
5. **Mars destabilizes** — Mars progressively destabilized; descends from central position toward Earth.
6. **Mars oscillates** — Returns to center, descends further, grows larger each descent.
7. **Mars drops below Venus** — Stream of material between Mars and Venus; gas/dust extends toward Earth.
8. **Lateral displacements** — Both Venus and Mars shift laterally.
9. **Band around Venus** — Spiralling material gathers into band encircling Venus.
10. **Mars descents continue** — Material stretches toward Earth with each descent.
11. **Material reaches Earth** — Disastrous effects.
12. **Recovery** — Skies clear; Mars moves away; band opens; four streams radiate again.

### Collapse and Dark Ages

13. **Saturn tumbles** — Jupiter appears; Saturn falls from pole.
14. **Venus displaced** — Band and radiating material collapse; spiralling between Venus and Mars.
15. **Temporary Jupiter renewal** — Configuration briefly dominated by Jupiter.
16. **Final breakup** — Configuration falls apart; planets to new equilibrium.

## The Saturn Myth: What It Does and Does Not Contain

*The Saturn Myth* (1980) describes:
- Saturn as polar sun, motionless at celestial pole
- Band/enclosure around Saturn; crescent as illuminated portion
- Crescent revolving with Earth's rotation (day/night marker)
- "Mercury, Venus, Mars, and Jupiter spaced at the four corners around Saturn" — a quartered layout, not Venus-center/Mars-inside
- Celestial order "collapsed with the death of the great god"
- No phrase "planets stood in line"
- No Mars "progressively destabilized," "descends," "oscillates"
- No "lateral displacements," band "encircling Venus," Jupiter "appeared for the first time"

**Conclusion:** The documentary's dynamical narrative (Grubaugh/Thornhill collaboration?) is a later synthesis not present in the 1980 book. That the documentary discloses more than the book may reflect managed disclosure—releasing information in stages.
