## 2b. Writing style — what was actually analyzed (and what was not)

### 2b.1 Author working theory (session — 2026-10-28)

> Shakespeare’s unique writing style came only from the uniqueness of **Eastern languages that were redacted and destroyed**. It is simply **how those languages spoke** — very poetically and metaphorically. Westerners are not used to that, and therefore they were impressed by Shakespeare’s writing style, when really they should just be impressed by **Cyrillic literature in general** — although they do not know how to read it, and consider much of it to be ancient and dead, preferring **Latin-based** languages even though Latin had no visible use.

**Unpack:** The “genius English” shock is a **misfiled Eastern poetic register**. After Latin-West preference + Cyrillic/eastern literature filed as dead antiquity, the Folio becomes the only place Anglophones meet that density of metaphor — and they credit a Stratford brand instead of the destroyed source tongues. Couples [Hidden History of Words](/history/chronology/the-hidden-history-of-words.md) (Latin west vs Cyrillic east after Horde fracture; Shakespeare as eastern-origin pin).

### 2b.3 Mainstream / computational analysis of “unique style”

Attempts exist; they answer a **different question** (which English playwright matches which English corpus) — **not** “does this English encode destroyed eastern languages?”

| Program | Method | Typical claim | Limit for AU-083 |
|---------|--------|---------------|------------------|

| **Neural nets / Marlowe–Shakespeare** (e.g. Matthews & Merriam 1994) | Discriminate trained canons | Can separate Marlowe vs Shakespeare blocks | Same closed English pool |
| **Caveats literature** | Experimental-design critique | Valid non-traditional attribution under present knowledge is **hard / unattainable** (control texts, chronology, opaque models) — e.g. [JEMS caveats](https://oajournals.fupress.net/index.php/bsfm-jems/article/download/7065/7063/6942); critiques of NOS quantitative claims | Undercuts “science proved unique genius” |

Clinic “single individual” is **R6-compatible**: one brand voice after translation committee.
