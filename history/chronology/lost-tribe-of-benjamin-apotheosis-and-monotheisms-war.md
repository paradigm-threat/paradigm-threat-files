## Franklin, complexion, and the Moors book fork

Franklin’s [*Observations Concerning the Increase of Mankind*](https://founders.archives.gov/documents/Franklin/01-04-02-0080) (1751) is ugly by modern standards and **documented** in every standard edition. On immigration he wrote of **Palatine Boors** — peasants from the Rhineland, not medieval Iberian Moors. In §21 he mentions the **expulsion of the Moors** when discussing **Spain’s** thin population; conflating §21 with §23 is one plausible origin of later **“Palatine Moors”** garbles.

§24 is the complexion passage that matters for the east-west map. Franklin ranks much of the world as **black or tawny** or **swarthy**; **Russians**, Italians, French, Germans (Saxons excepted), Swedes — **swarthy** in his English. Saxons with the English form the “principal Body of White People on the Face of the Earth.” He never calls George II “black” in the authentic essay.
