# Indigenous creation dates, legends, and geology — chronology hub

![Indigenous creation dates, legends, and geology — hub figure](indigenous-creation-legends-and-geology.png)

> **TL;DR:** **Entry page** for the timeline lane that connects **indigenous creation calendars** (cosmogonic years and cycles), **place-origin and volcano legends** (islands, mountains, lakes), and **institutional geochronology** (radiocarbon stacks, hotspot K-Ar / Ar-Ar, “deep time” public narrative). This file **does not duplicate** the investigations—it **routes** readers to them with a **single map** of how the pieces fit the [Earth history timeline](/history/chronology/page.md).

## What this hub is for

Three layers often get **talked past** each other:

1. **Creation dates** — explicit or computable **year-zero / cycle** claims in **indigenous and comparative** sources (Mesoamerican, global survey in progress).
2. **Place legends** — **moʻolelo**, **kuji**-class compilations, Pacific **island-fishing** myths, etc., that read **landforms** as **events in memory**, not only as **metaphor**.
3. **Geology-as-clock** — **radiometric ages**, **calibration curves**, and **field practice** that assign **10⁶–10⁹ yr** timescales to the **same rocks** legends name.

This hub treats those as **one research front** for the repo: **where calendars, story, and lab time disagree**, document **who said what**, **which method**, and **which evidence tier**—then link outward.

## Evidence tiers (how to read the linked files)

- **Primary / ethnographic** — Kamakau, Malo, Collocott, Gifford, community-authored scholarship (see legends dossier **§1.2**, **§2.1**).
- **Peer / institutional** — USGS, *JVGR*, IntCal20, *Radiocarbon* journal—**strong on method**, not automatic **disproof** of oral claims without **same object** (which rock, which legend layer).
- **Advocacy / hypothesis** — ICR excess-argon compilations, Peratt IEEE plasma–petroglyph work—**labeled** in the dossiers; do not **merge tiers** in argument.
