## How to read these entries

Substack Notes often mixes **three different things**:

| Layer | Example | Reader mistake |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Legitimate grievance** | Court capture, Epstein docket, Iran **two scoreboards**, war powers | Treating **all** critics as bigots — or **all** bigots as wrong on every sub-point |
| **Ethnic shortcut** | “**All Jews** evil,” “**Zionist rat hole**” as **whole** explanation | Confusing **vivid** with **complete** |
| **CO / engagement geometry** | Repetitive slur loops, **Hitler=Rothschild** swaps, **Nazi heroics** vs **Jewish evil** | Assuming **human debate** when the **shape** is **call-and-response** |

Our answers aim for **objective balance**: many threads contain **contradictory truths at once** — e.g. **named Jewish-identified actors** in consequential history **and** **Jewish dissent** in the same archive; **Israel alliance pressure** on U.S. policy **and** **Ajax → Shah → latency** boards built **before** modern Likud headlines; **real abuse cases** **and** **forbidden** leap to **collective guilt**.

**Not antisemitism under our frame:** evidence-based **who did what**; **Zionism as ideology/policy** critique; **structural** analysis **without** “**all Jews** on one side.”  
**Is antisemitism under our frame:** **monolithing**, **inherited evil**, **blood-libel** memes, **Nazi hagiography** paired with **Jewish essential evil**.

**Workflow:** [Common Questions](/history/common-questions.md) — anonymize, deduplicate, cite in-repo sources, note gaps.
