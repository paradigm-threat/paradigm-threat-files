# Two Camps of Medicine: Jaffa, Mercury, and the Radiation Cover Story

French war medicine, perceived invincibility, and what got redacted between Napoleon and Marie Curie

![Napoleon corpses — dry Egypt, Siberia, captured medicine (article lead)](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/napoleon-corpses-dry-egypt-siberian-disease.png)

The image everyone remembers is Antoine-Jean Gros’s Salon canvas from **1804**: Bonaparte in hero light, fingers on a soldier’s bubo. On **11 March 1799** at Jaffa, physician René Desgenettes helped **carry one corpse** with a burst bubo while Bonaparte kept calm. This essay is not about whether those men were literally immune. It is about **two camps of medicine** that fight over every such scene—and whether French soldiers **believed** they could not get sick because they had seen **advanced therapy** demonstrated in their faces.

> **TL;DR:** **Camp A** = terrain, morale, anticontagion dispute, **demonstrated** cures; **Camp B** = invisible agents, germ saints, diagnostic-only radiation, post-COVID vaccine dogma. **Jaffa 1799** + **Gros 1804** = real **felt invincibility** (not “fake news”); [plague dossier](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/health/investigations/napoleon-plague-murad-bay-screenshot-source-hunt-investigation.md) still tiers Gros as state art for forensics. **Egypt** = medical arrogance in terrain they misread; **Russia 1812** = reckoning. **Mercury** = documented elite pharmacopoeia trace; **radiation cure since Napoleon** = author thesis. **Curie 1914–1918** = same boast, **bullet-finder cover story** author finds preposterous. Ends **Semmelweis** + ***Twelve Monkeys***. [Investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/investigations/napoleon-egypt-burial-siberia-health-investigation.md).

## Two camps over time

**Camp A** treats disease as **terrain, depletion, and collective tone**—plus therapies that work in the open ward: morale, anticontagion stunts, compounds the army actually carries, and (in this repo’s bias) **radiation and voltage modalities** older than the textbook admits. It assumes [**prisca sapientia**](https://paradigmthreat.net/docs/PRISCA_SAPIENTIA.md): deep medical knowledge existed and was later degraded, not invented from zero in the nineteenth century.

**Camp B** treats disease as **invisible executors** you must believe in without seeing: miasma’s successor **germs**, then **viruses**, then **genomes**—always with a licensed product line. Handwashing becomes universal religion; **X-rays** become “only for finding bullets”; **COVID** becomes proof that no healthy person can simply **not catch** what is going around.

The camps do not map cleanly to “left vs right” or “science vs superstition.” They map to **who controls the story** after a demonstration in front of soldiers. ## Jaffa and the painting: demonstration, not debunking

Gros, advised by Egypt veteran Dominique Vivant Denon, fixed Bonaparte **touching the living bubo**—royal-touch Christ healing for the Salon. Desgenettes’s ward record is smaller and uglier: **one** corpse carried bare-handed in stench, **11 March 1799**. Gros’s earlier sketch had Bonaparte **carrying** a plague soldier; the final canvas chose what **photographs best for miracle**.

This investigation treats that cluster as **real morale medicine**, not a hoax the men saw through. Medical officers **Assalini**, **Desgenettes**, and allied British voices held plague **non-contagious** in the period’s dispute sense. Desgenettes **inoculated himself** with bubo material; Napoleon’s long hospital visit and corpse lift were staged so ranks would **calm their imagination**—French military phrase—because they already trusted the **kit** behind the stunt. The leadership caste may have known the limits of that kit. The ranks were not supposed to.

The expedition still buried roughly **seventeen hundred** plague dead. Mass illness is **documented**; the thesis is **belief and demonstration**, not literal invincibility.

Daniel Roytas’s **2024** *Can You Catch a Cold?* (Chapter 11, pp. 204–206) compresses Pyramids, **Murad Bay**, and zero French infections into one paragraph—the circulated screenshot. The [plague investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/health/investigations/napoleon-plague-murad-bay-screenshot-source-hunt-investigation.md) grades each clause; **endnote 8** is **Winterbottom 1828, *Edinburgh Medical and Surgical Journal* 30(97):321–44**. For **Gros as propaganda artifact**, read that dossier; for **why soldiers felt untouchable**, read the ward and pharmacology lane here.

## What the French thought they had

Napoleonic armies marched with a **military pharmacopoeia** that already looked “modern” to the men: **mercury** in compounds such as **calomel** and related purges, alongside opiates, cinchona, and the surgical chest. Mercury is not proof of **radiation**—it is proof that Paris sent **heavy, aggressive chemistry** into the field and trained physicians to deploy it. In Camp A, that is evidence of **access to advanced drugs** the men could confuse with **total protection** when a general touches plague and walks out.

**Radiation as battlefield cure in 1799** is **not** documented in this repo yet; it is **author thesis**: the same healing line Curie deployed in **1914–1918** may be **far older**, later redacted to a harmless diagnostic tale. Mercury and radiation sit in different tiers—**documented pharmacy** vs **continuity speculation**—until archives say otherwise.

## Egypt confidence, Russia reckoning

After **conquering Egypt**, Bonaparte struck into regions his staff **did not understand**—climate, water, local disease ecology—while carrying supreme **medical confidence**. That arrogance is the French elite pattern this file tracks: major wars against ground you are **not prepared for**, backed by faith that **your therapy** beats their terrain.

In Egypt the army still had reserves—morale, supply, favorable season, collective tone. In **Russia 1812** the mirror broke: retreat, winter, exile, **typhus**, **dysentery**, starvation. Confidence cannot refill **depletion** ([Tennant voltage frame](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/health/tennant/index-tennant-healing-voltage.md)). Russia does not prove Camp B was right about “germs”; it proves **spent humans** fall when the ledger runs out.

**Camp B today** insists even healthy people must **catch** what is circulating—especially since **COVID**—and that only schedules and products save them. That is the late victory of invisible-agent medicine over Camp A’s **herd that heals itself**; full argument in the [investigation §2](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/investigations/napoleon-egypt-burial-siberia-health-investigation.md).

## Curie: the preposterous bullet story

Mainstream history says Marie Curie’s **petites Curies** (**1914–1918**) brought **mobile X-ray** so surgeons could **localize shrapnel**—diagnosis, not mass cure. This repo **biases the other way**: she **treated** with ionizing radiation in ways later flattened; the public got **martyr-to-radium** memory instead of a **field cure** ([Curie investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/nukes/investigations/marie-curie-radiation-cures-suppression-speculation.md)).

The author finds the bullet-only story **preposterous** as the whole truth: if imaging alone won the war ward, show the outcome tables; instead we get the same **miracle-medicine boast** as Jaffa—French science **masters death** for the soldier—until the next Russia collects the bill. **1914–1918** **rhymes** **1799–1804**, not because Curie caused Napoleon, but because **both** sold **invincibility through elite kit** to men who could not audit the physics.

**Eben Byers** (1932, Radithor) and **Flexner (1910)** sit in the same corridor: radiation commerce and guild capture before **1945** fixed “radiation” as uniform poison in mass memory.

## Semmelweis: when Camp B wins

**Ignaz Semmelweis** (1818–1865) proved **cadaverous particles** on hands killed mothers when students went from autopsy to maternity; **chlorinated lime** collapsed mortality in **1848**. Vienna then **blocked his corpse work** (**Oct 1850** docent terms: **no cadavers**, mannequins only) and drove him out. He was right on the ward; Camp B later made him patron saint of **germs and universal handwash**, burying terrain medicine and sorting everyone into **supporter vs denier** around a name. Facts: [investigation §11](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/investigations/napoleon-egypt-burial-siberia-health-investigation.md).
