# Deep State / Pyramidal Empire / Fallen Atlantean Empire — terminology

This document tracks **which label** the chronology uses for the same continuity of priest-class control at different epochs. It is the editorial source of truth for `history/chronology/page.md`, cross-links from investigations, and timeline appendices.

**Related:** Timeline `docs/CONTENT_STRATEGY.md` (recurring concepts — link canonical definitions from first use in `content/`).

---

## One continuity, three names

| Label | Use when |
|--------|-----------|
| **Fallen Atlantean Empire** | Immediately **after the Golden Age ends** (post-Atlantis fall): survivors from Poseida, priest narrative of punishment, coalescence of obedience structures *before* the pyramid-grid phase is the main instrument. Emphasizes **continuity from Atlantis** rather than a modern political metaphor. |
| **Pyramidal Empire** | **Peak Dark Ages** control through the **world pyramid network**, entanglement gateways, simulated deities, Egyptian-phase “New Atlantis under God’s Authority,” through the **loss of that network** and collapse of unified pyramid command. **Reemergence:** after the **1666 Deep State climax** (see below), the same continuity grows bold enough to **surface** overt pyramidal architecture and emblems worldwide—use when stressing **visible hierarchical form**, not the clandestine moment itself. |
| **Deep State** | **Clandestine** or **fragmented** phases: hidden continuity, exile, calendar/religion capture without intact pyramid infrastructure, **modern** informal usage (secret societies, parallel governance, “deep” placement in society). **1666 (London / Cestui Que Vie):** use **Deep State** only—treated as a **climax** of the deep phase (apocalyptic expectation of *God* or *Jesus*; outcome reads as **the Beast** in sacred dress). Safe **umbrella** when the epoch spans both forms, or for **reader-facing** shorthand after the first precise term appears. |

**Guidance:** In one paragraph, prefer **one primary term** for the era under discussion; use a second only at a **transition** (“the Fallen Atlantean priesthood consolidated the **Pyramidal Empire** once pyramids spanned…”) or in a **gloss** (“what later analysis calls the Deep State”).

| Phase | Preferred primary term | Notes |
|-------|-------------------------|--------|

| **1666 CE** (Great Fire, Cestui Que Vie, London financial reset) | **Deep State** (climax) | Not **Pyramidal Empire** in running prose: the continuity is still *deep*, revealing itself as **the Beast** while masses expect divine restoration; confidence from this pivot enables **later** overt pyramidal surfacing. **Inland Empire:** still does decisive work through infiltration and suppressed knowledge. |
| Post-1666 → present (visible pyramids/seals/monuments + technocratic layering) | **Pyramidal Empire** (visible form) **and** **Deep State** (what remains hidden) | Pyramidal when the **emblematic / structural hierarchy** is the point; Deep State when stressing **Inland Empire** depth, institutional infiltration, and knowledge control. |
