## How to read these entries

Many objections to the 2026 Iran campaign are **presentation problems**, not intelligence problems:

| Mainstream habit | What it hides |
|------------------|---------------|
| One boolean: “war over” / “we lost” | **Combat tempo**, **blockade politics**, **HEU custody**, and **talks** run on different clocks |
| Cost and poll meter only | **Strike objectives**, **conventional dismantling**, and **latency** as the stated casus belli |
| “Trump / Bibi / oil” as the whole motive | **1953 → Shah nukes → revolution → clerical latency factory** — a board built over decades |
| Moral outrage without archive | **Who built the nuclear choke** and **who profits from the next emergency** |

When a comment is **strictly anti-Trump** (personality, “troll president,” mockery) with **no engagement on the file**, we label that **TDS-shaped framing**: easy to debunk because it refuses the complicated layer — not because every policy choice was wise.
