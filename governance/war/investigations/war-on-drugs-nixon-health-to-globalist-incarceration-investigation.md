## 2. Phase A — Nixon War on Drugs as health/nationalist frame

### Documented (Tier A)

- **17 Jun 1971:** Nixon special message / press conference frame declaring drug abuse **“public enemy number one”** and requesting expanded **treatment, education, and enforcement** funding ([Nixon Library / public papers](https://www.nixonlibrary.gov/); contemporary AP/UPI coverage).
- **SAODAP (Special Action Office for Drug Abuse Prevention)** and early-1970s federal treatment expansion — demand-side architecture, not only cops.
- **Controlled Substances Act (1970)** schedule architecture pre-dates / couples with the 1971 branding — federal classification power that later administrations could harden without inventing a new statute.

### Author read (Tier Author)

Nixon as **Nationalist** — grounded American, constituent-facing — matches the gauge already locked in [NSA-aligned presidencies §7](/governance/war/investigations/nsa-aligned-presidencies-nixon-reagan-trump-investigation.md#7-nixon--grounded--media-crook-inversion) and [AU-046](/governance/war/investigations/presidential-alignment-globalist-vs-nationalist-investigation.md). Phase A is scored as **pro-American public-health nationalism**, not as the later globalist incarceration export.

**Not claimed here:** That Phase A had zero enforcement or zero racial disparate impact. Claimed: the **center of gravity** in 1971 rhetoric and early offices was **health + national demand**, later **inverted**.

## 4. Phase B — post-Watergate conversion

### 4.1 Watergate as exit (author + corpus)

**Documented:** Nixon resignation **9 Aug 1974** after Watergate cascade.

**Author / site read:** Watergate as **managed scandal / containment** of a Nationalist off CIA war-state script — not proof he was “the crook.” See [NSA-aligned §7](/governance/war/investigations/nsa-aligned-presidencies-nixon-reagan-trump-investigation.md#7-nixon--grounded--media-crook-inversion); [JFK §1.2 inverse exit](/events/jfk/investigations/jfk-rfk-staged-assassination-hoax-investigation.md#12-post-kennedy-inverse-exit--nixon--watergate).

**Conversion claim (Author):** Once that tip was removed, the **War on Drugs brand** was free to become something else — incarceration industry + covert-war logistics — without a nationalist health principal holding the center.

### 4.2 Mass incarceration (Documented)

- **1980s–90s:** Anti-Drug Abuse Acts, mandatory minimums, **100:1** crack/powder disparity (later reduced), prison population surge, local militarization via federal grants.
- Outcome metrics (use rates, street prices, cartel consolidation) show **prohibition failed as a supply crusade** while **carceral capacity** succeeded as institutional growth — matches the earlier conversation’s retro verdict (failed health/supply goals; successful political/carceral machine).

### 4.3 Trafficking + proxy war (Documented — Contra specimen)

- **Kerry Committee (1989):** Contra associates, suppliers, pilots involved in narcotics; U.S. agencies often had information; State Department even paid some traffickers with Contra “humanitarian” funds while under investigation/indictment ([report summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerry_Committee_report)).
- **CIA IG Hitz (1998):** No CIA employee conspiracy to import drugs; **multiple** cases of failed cutoff / soft handling of alleged traffickers supporting the Contra program; **blind eye** language used in secondary press summarizing Hitz ([Independent](https://www.the-independent.com/news/cia-turned-a-deliberate-blind-eye-to-contras-drug-smuggling-1183305.html); [FAS Hitz testimony](https://irp.fas.org/congress/1998_hr/980316-ps.htm)).

**Pattern (Author):** This is not “Nixon’s health war.” It is **covert-war priority over narcotics enforcement** — the foreign face of Phase B — while domestic Phase B fills prisons.
