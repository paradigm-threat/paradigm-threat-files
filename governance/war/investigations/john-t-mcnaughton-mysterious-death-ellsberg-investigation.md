| Ellsberg hire | **1964** — Ellsberg becomes McNaughton’s special assistant / “alter ego” ([*Secrets*](https://archive.org/details/secretsmemoirofv00ells); timeline [Ellsberg profile](/timeline/evt-historical-antibodies-profiles)) |
| Death | **19 Jul 1967**, Piedmont Flight 22 / Cessna midair, Hendersonville, NC — McNaughton, **Sally**, **Ted** among **82** dead |

### 2.2 Managed-news DNA (Documented rhyme)

April 1963 *Time*: McNaughton advises Pentagon Press Secretary Arthur Sylvester facing a House subcommittee on “managed news”:

> “Never, under any circumstances whatsoever, use the word lie… If you have to tell the committee you want to lie down, say recline.”  
> ([Time, 5 Apr 1963](http://content.time.com/time/magazine/article/0,9171,830081,00.html) via [Paullin McNaughton blog](http://jtmcnaughtonfindawayout.blogspot.com/))

**Author read:** The man who understood **managed news** from the inside is the last person whose death should be closed by **managed news**.

### 2.3 Ellsberg pipeline (Documented)

Ellsberg’s *Secrets* establishes:

- McNaughton recruited him into the highest cable traffic (Tonkin night **4 Aug 1964** — Ellsberg on duty with McNaughton).
- McNaughton shared **private** assessments Ellsberg was not to broadcast inside the building.
- After Vietnam field time, Ellsberg returned **summer 1967** into the **McNamara study** McNaughton had just been ordered to seed — the study that became the **Pentagon Papers**.

**Author causal chain:** McNaughton did not merely “manage” Ellsberg as staff. He **armed** him with **eyes-only truth**. That transfer is the motive surface for a hit — whether or not McNaughton would later have approved Ellsberg’s **1971** leak (Halperin/McNamara *post hoc* claims that John “would have hated” the leak are **containment speech**, not exoneration of the 1967 death).

## 5. 19 Jul 1967 — official surface vs working theory

### 5.1 Official surface (treat as contested packaging)

| Claim (period / NTSB) | Content |
|-----------------------|---------|
| Aircraft | Piedmont **Boeing 727** Flight 22 (Asheville → Roanoke) vs **Cessna 310** N3121S (Lanseair / Rapidair pilot **John Addison**) |
| Time / place | **12:01 EDT**, ~Hendersonville, NC; **82** dead; no survivors |
| NTSB probable cause (1968) | Cessna **deviated** from IFR clearance into Piedmont’s airspace; FAA minimum procedures **contributing**; reason for deviation “cannot be specifically or positively identified” ([NTSB AAR](https://www.ntsb.gov/investigations/Pages/AAR68AJ.aspx)) |
| 2007 revisit | NTSB upholds original finding **3–1** against Paul Houle’s challenges ([Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedmont_Airlines_Flight_22)) |

### 5.2 Already-broken trust in the official crash story (Documented disputes)

Even **without** accepting the assassination thesis, the NTSB package is **not clean**:

- First major airline investigation by the **new** NTSB — institutional infancy.
- Historian **Paul Houle** (*The Crash of Piedmont Airlines Flight 22*): alleges **conflict of interest** (NTSB investigator **Thomas Saunders** brother of Piedmont VP **H.K. Saunders**); omitted **cockpit ashtray fire** preoccupying the 727 crew in final seconds; disputed radio-transcript handling (“via Two-Three-Eight” heading) ([BlueRidgeNow](https://www.blueridgenow.com/story/news/2018/07/19/51-years-later-look-back-at-flight-22-disaster-over-hendersonville/11448941007/); [GoUpstate](https://www.goupstate.com/story/news/2005/07/16/collision-in-the-sky/29764324007/)).
- Cessna pilot Addison was a **substitute** — Lanseair’s chief pilot “incapacitated”; Addison planning to quit flying for real estate.

**Author:** These disputes do **not** by themselves prove a hit. They prove the **official accident story was already politicized / compromised** — which is exactly why this investigation **refuses** to let NTSB+wire copy close the case.
