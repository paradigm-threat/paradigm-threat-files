## 4. What RAND is (baseline, not the name fight)

- **Form:** Nonprofit think tank; first regularly labeled “think tank” in American usage ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND)).
- **Core early mission:** Long-range weapons / Air Force planning; operations research; later education, health, international affairs.
- **Method brand:** Systems analysis, game theory, quantitative policy — “objective” civilian brainpower for military clients.
- **Russia / East war (Documented function):** From day one, Project RAND’s charter lane was **intercontinental warfare** planning for the Army Air Forces / USAF — the institutional brain for fighting a peer adversary across oceans. Cold War product stack (nuclear strategy, Soviet studies, Vietnam systems analysis) is the **mechanical** half of Author’s “prepare for war in the East” claim. Author: Vietnam is a **nested** containment theater under the Russia plot, not the master plot.
- **Corpus read:** Institutional cousin to the **secrecy culture** Ellsberg describes — clearances, presidential-eyes-only product, Vietnam study ecosystem. Not identical to CIA HUMINT or NSA SIGINT; a **third node**: **analytic legitimacy** for war-state choices.
