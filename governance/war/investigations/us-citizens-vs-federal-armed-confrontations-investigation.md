## Scope and inclusion criteria

This file tracks **armed** or **militarized** episodes where **non–federal-government civilians** (or mixed groups including them) faced **U.S. federal forces**: regular military, federalized militia under federal command, or federal law enforcement (including rangers and tactical teams). **“Armed confrontation”** includes exchanges of fire, fixed sieges with rifles deployed, or mass deployments with violence short of sustained battle.

**Excluded or marginal:** purely local/county-only fights without federal agents or troops (e.g. many labor strikes handled only by Pinkertons or state militia unless federal troops were ordered in); interstate war (U.S. Civil War as a whole — see [US Civil War investigation](./us-civil-war-investigation.md)); riots where only municipal police engaged unless federalized units appear.

**Two comparison axes used below:**


| Tag                | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Bundy-like**     | Public land / grazing / mining / refuge; BLM, Forest Service, USFWS, or courts enforcing fees and orders; armed supporters; standoff and negotiation or federal pullback.     |
| **Jade-Helm-like** | Large-scale **domestic** military or SOF **presence**, maps/exercise framing, “federal overreach” or “martial law” **narrative** — *not necessarily a shooting war* (see §1). |

## 1. Jade Helm 15 (2015) — narrative scale vs. armed clash

**Jade Helm 15** was a scheduled **U.S. Army Special Operations Command** multi-state training exercise (approximately July–September 2015), including Texas and other states. Officially: unconventional-warfare training. It did **not** produce a documented **shooting engagement** between citizens and troops. Its place in this catalog is as possibly the **largest modern footprint** of federal **military** activity **inside the United States** in a “peacetime” year—fueling conspiracy narratives (martial law, Walmart tunnels, confiscation, etc.) summarized in mainstream sources such as [Wikipedia: Jade Helm 15 conspiracy theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_Helm_15_conspiracy_theories).

**Why it belongs in the same research folder as Bundy:** both episodes fed a **distrust-of-federal-power** discourse in the mid-2010s—Jade Helm through **troop movement and map labels** (e.g. “hostile” training notional areas), Bundy through **BLM enforcement** and **armed resistance**. Gov. Greg Abbott’s order for the **Texas State Guard** to **monitor** the exercise (April 2015) was an unusual state-level response; Sen. Ted Cruz and others commented publicly. **Hypothesis-focused** treatment (Cruz, Texas, **Angry Birds** / media frame, 2016 primary) lives in **[Jade Helm — “real op” hypothesis stack](#jade-helm-hypothesis-stack)** below.

### Documented facts (mainstream narrative — baseline)

These points are **widely sourced** and are **not** the hypothesis in the stack below; they anchor what that stack must explain or extend.

1. **Jade Helm 15** was a **real**, scheduled **U.S. Army Special Operations Command** training exercise (Jul–Sep 2015), multi-state, including **Texas**. Official description: unconventional warfare training; some training maps labelled Texas **“hostile”** for **role-play** purposes—fuel for conspiracy amplification.
2. **Fringe theories** (martial law, Walmart tunnels, gun confiscation, etc.) are summarised in [Wikipedia: Jade Helm 15 conspiracy theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jade_Helm_15_conspiracy_theories) with citations to **Alex Jones**, **All News Pipeline**, **Chuck Norris** commentary, etc.
3. **Gov. Greg Abbott (R-Texas)** on **28 Apr 2015** ordered the **Texas State Guard** to **monitor** the exercise—an extraordinary political step that **validated** grassroots fear in the eyes of critics. Sources: same Wikipedia article; [Houston Chronicle](https://www.chron.com/news/houston-texas/article/Ted-Cruz-understand-Jade-Helm-concerns-6246697.php) coverage of the wider political reaction.
4. **Sen. Ted Cruz**, **2 May 2015** (reported from South Carolina GOP convention): said his **office contacted the Pentagon**, was **told it was a military training exercise**, had **“no reason to doubt”** that, but **“understand[s] the reason for concern and uncertainty”** given distrust of the **Obama-era federal government**. Sources: [Bloomberg / David Weigel](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2015-05-02/ted-cruz-says-he-has-asked-the-pentagon-for-answers-on-jade-helm-15) (cited via [MSNBC summary](https://www.msnbc.com/rachel-maddow-show/cruz-sympathizes-jade-helm-15-conspiracy-theorists-msna587991)); [Houston Chronicle](https://www.chron.com/news/houston-texas/article/Ted-Cruz-understand-Jade-Helm-concerns-6246697.php); [New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/article/121726/cruz-abbott-indulge-jade-helm-conspiracy-because-texans-demand-it).
5. **President Obama** later called Jade Helm **“his favourite conspiracy theory”** (reported interview, e.g. Wikipedia article above).

**Important correction to a common misremembering:** Cruz was **not** the **highest authority in Texas**. The **governor** (Abbott) and **state** apparatus outrank a **U.S. senator** on **state** mobilisation; **federal** executive authority lay with **Obama** and **DoD**. The thesis that Cruz was the **most influential national figure** *from Texas* **reassuring** a **presidential-primary audience** is **arguable**; the claim that no one **higher** could confirm or deny is **false** in strict hierarchy terms. The investigation **preserves** the sentiment that **Cruz’s voice mattered uniquely for movement credibility** while **recording** that **Abbott**, **Perry**, **Paul**, **Gohmert**, and **national media** were also in the mix.

**Tags:** **Jade-Helm-like** (primary). Not Bundy-like (no grazing fee dispute).

### Investigator sentiment and core thesis (preserved)

The following is the **working narrative** this stack exists to test—not a finding of fact.

- **Jade Helm 15 may have been “real” in a sense the official story does not admit:** not merely a training exercise on paper, but cover for a **penetration or takedown posture** aimed at **Texas state government** or at **neutralising a political threat** embedded in Texas’s role in national politics.
- **Strategic target hypothesis:** **Sen. Ted Cruz (R-Texas)** was, at the **Jade Helm / 2016-primary** juncture, the **largest credible Republican runner-up** in a field that otherwise looked **manufactured or disposable**—a **churn** of short-lived notional front-runners. The investigator’s **“Angry Birds”** image is **anchored to the 2012 election cycle** in press satire (see **2012 — Angry Birds** later in this stack), **not** as a documented 2016 headline; it is used here as **cross-cycle** colour for **fragile, crashing candidacies**. In that reading, **Cruz stood out** with a **coherent, serious platform**—an **America-first** posture **relatively rare** in the pre-Trump GOP mainstream—and therefore had to be **contained** if the eventual nominee (and the deep-state alignment behind the race) were to be controlled.
- **Coup / control hypothesis:** The **missions** U.S. Army SOF conducted under Jade Helm’s cover could have served **intelligence preparation of the battlespace** in political terms: habituation of the public to troop movement, mapping of “hostile” / “permissive” terrain in official briefings, and leverage over **Texas institutions**—with **Cruz** as the figure whose **rise** had to be checked without an obvious assassination narrative.
- **Aftermath hypothesis:** Once the exercise ended and the **panic was managed**, the **same networks** that benefited from **debunking** the “martial law” story needed **Cruz**—as **Texas’s most visible national Republican** alongside the governor—to **validate the official line** at the **highest credible level available to “concerned patriots.”** That **does not require** Cruz to have been the only official who spoke; it claims his **Senate stature + presidential campaign** made his **voice disproportionately effective** at **cooling** the story among **primary voters and movement conservatives**.
- **Meta-hypothesis:** What looked like **Cruz “helping” dispel** a fringe theory may, in this reading, have been **compliance** after **compromise**—so that **everything** emitted from his office afterward (statements, campaign messaging, even **text / surrogate comms**) could be **shaped** or **held hostage** by actors the investigator bundles as **deep state**.

This stack **holds all of that open** while demanding **independent evidence**.

## 2. Bundy standoff — Bunkerville, Nevada (2014) and family-linked events

### 2.1 Summary

A long-running **civil** dispute over **trespass grazing** on **BLM** and **National Park Service** lands near **Bunkerville, Clark County, Nevada**, escalated in **April 2014** into an **armed standoff** between **federal** contractors and law enforcement (under court authority) and **hundreds** of **Cliven Bundy** supporters, including **armed militia** travelers from multiple states. The BLM **suspended** the cattle **impoundment** and **released** held livestock to **de-escalate**; **no one** was **killed** in the immediate confrontation. The episode became a **movement** **touchstone** and fed later **Malheur** (2016) and **criminal** **conspiracy** trials.

**Tags:** **Bundy-like** (definitional). **Jade-Helm-like** only weakly (militarized federal presence in the West, overlapping **movement** networks).

### 2.2 Background — ranch, permits, and “vested rights”

- **Land tenure narrative (Bundy family):** Cliven Bundy’s family traces settlement in the **Virgin River** area to the **late 1800s** (e.g. grandparents in the **Parashant / Mount Trumbull** area per press timelines). **BLM** **grazing permits** on the **Bunkerville Allotment** date to **1954**; Bundy **paid** federal **grazing fees** for roughly **two decades** from **1973** onward ([Las Vegas Review-Journal — archived timeline](https://web.archive.org/web/20160216141915/http://www.reviewjournal.com/news/nevada/range-war-timeline-events); [Wikipedia: Bundy standoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundy_standoff)).
- **1993 break:** The **BLM** **revised** the permit—**reducing** herd size and **changing** use rules—citing among other factors **desert tortoise** **habitat** concerns under evolving federal environmental policy. Bundy **rejected** the new terms, **stopped paying** fees to the BLM, and began asserting **state** or **county** authority and **“vested”** **grazing** **rights**—arguments **federal courts** would **reject** repeatedly ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundy_standoff)).
- **1994–1995:** The agency issued **orders** to **remove** cattle; Bundy **did not** comply. In **1994** he reportedly attempted to pay **Clark County** instead of the BLM; the **county** **returned** the payment as **outside** its **jurisdiction** (see [Center for Biological Diversity — Gold Butte grazing history PDF](https://www.biologicaldiversity.org/programs/public_lands/pdfs/GoldButteGrazingHistory.pdf) cited in secondary reporting).

### 2.3 Civil litigation — injunctions and money damages

- **1998:** The **United States** sued; **U.S. District Judge Johnnie B. Rawlinson** issued a **permanent injunction** (**3 Nov 1998**) barring Bundy from grazing the **Bunkerville Allotment** and ordering **removal** by **30 Nov 1998**. The **Ninth Circuit** **affirmed** in **1999** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundy_standoff); docket citations in court records).
- **2008–2012:** The BLM **cancelled** permits and escalated **administrative** enforcement; a **planned 2012** roundup was **suspended** over **safety** concerns ([Review-Journal timeline](https://web.archive.org/web/20160216141915/http://www.reviewjournal.com/news/nevada/range-war-timeline-events)).
- **2012–2013:** The United States filed a **new** action; **Senior Judge Lloyd D. George** granted **summary judgment** **9 July 2013**, **permanently enjoining** trespass on **Gold Butte** “New Trespass Lands,” ordering **removal** within **45 days**, and authorizing **seizure** and **impoundment** of remaining cattle. **Judge Larry R. Hicks** issued a **related order** **8 October 2013** reinforcing **non-interference** with any **federal** **gather** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundy_standoff)).
- **Arrears:** By **2014**, agencies and courts summarized roughly **1 million** or more in **unpaid fees, fines, and penalties**—orders of magnitude above other **nationwide** BLM **fee** **delinquencies** reported in contemporaneous press ([AP, 2024](https://apnews.com/article/ffff74b4e3224fb596e6bb735cedef98)).

### 2.4 April 2014 — impoundment and standoff

- **March 2014:** **Notice of intent** to **impound** **unauthorized** livestock on **BLM** and **NPS** lands; **27 March 2014** temporary **closure** of roughly **145,000 acres** for the operation ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundy_standoff)).
- **Roundup:** Contract cowboys and **federal** **rangers** began **gathering** cattle **5 April 2014**. **Clashes** included **6 April** arrest of son **Dave Bundy** on a **public road** (misdemeanor **charges** such as **resisting** / **refusing to disperse** per news summaries).
- **12 April — “Battle of Bunkerville” imagery:** Supporters—some **armed**, some filming—**massed** along the **I-15** corridor and near **holding pens**. Widely circulated **images** showed **rifles** aimed toward **federal** positions and **overpass** crowds. The BLM and interagency command **ended** the **gather**, **released** roughly **three to four hundred** impounded cattle (reports vary), and cited **serious concern** for **employee** and **public** **safety** ([CNN, Apr 2014](https://www.cnn.com/2014/04/10/us/nevada-rancher-rangers-cattle-showdown); [ABC News, Apr 12 2014](https://abcnews.go.com/US/nevada-cattle-rancher-wins-range-war-federal-government/story?id=23302610)).
- **Cliven Bundy’s demand to the sheriff:** Press accounts describe Bundy **ordering** the **Clark County Sheriff** to **disarm** federal agents—part of a **county** **supremacy** / **constitutional sheriff** **framing** common in **patriot** discourse ([Wikipedia — context](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundy_standoff)).

### 2.5 Aftermath — 2015–2018: prosecutions, Malheur, outcomes

- **2015:** “**Liberty**” **anniversary** events; **June 2015** reports of **shots** fired near **survey** crews in **Gold Butte**, prompting **agency** **restrictions** and **FBI** interest per timelines ([Biological Diversity PDF summary](https://www.biologicaldiversity.org/programs/public_lands/pdfs/GoldButteGrazingHistory.pdf); [Review-Journal timeline](https://web.archive.org/web/20160216141915/http://www.reviewjournal.com/news/nevada/range-war-timeline-events)).
- **2016 — Malheur:** Sons **Ammon** and **Ryan Bundy** helped lead the **armed occupation** of the **Malheur National Wildlife Refuge** (Oregon), nominally over **re-sentencing** of the **Hammond** ranchers; **LaVoy Finicum** was **killed** at a **roadblock** **26 January 2016** by **Oregon State Police** (federal **FBI** involved in the overall operation). **Cliven Bundy** was **arrested** **11 February 2016** upon **flying** to **Portland**—linking the **Nevada** and **Oregon** arcs in one **federal** **prosecution** strategy ([Wikipedia: Occupation of Malheur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occupation_of_the_Malheur_National_Wildlife_Refuge)).
- **Nevada conspiracy trial cluster:** In **2017–2018**, prosecutions over the **2014** standoff **collapsed** in part due to **Brady** / **discovery** failures and **mistrials**; **mainstream** reporting emphasizes that **no Bundy family member** was **finally convicted** on the **central** **conspiracy** counts prosecutors had pursued, while **some** **defendants** **pleaded** to **lesser** charges ([AP retrospective](https://apnews.com/article/ffff74b4e3224fb596e6bb735cedef98)). **Civil** **trespass** **orders** and **unpaid** **balances** remained **politically** and **legally** **unsettled** for years after.

## 6. Reconstruction-era supplement (federal military vs. armed citizens / paramilitaries)

This subsection answers the former open question on whether **Reconstruction** belongs in scope. **Yes:** several episodes match the **federal-force vs. armed non–federal** pattern, though **race**, **election**, and **paramilitary** **white-supremacist** violence dominate the historical literature—different moral geometry than **public-land** disputes, but the **coercion** **geometry** is comparable for this catalog.

### Colfax massacre (1873) — federal role largely **after** the fact

**Easter Sunday, 13 April 1873**, **Grant Parish, Louisiana:** In a disputed **1872** election aftermath, **white** **militia** (including **former** **Confederates** and **KKK**-linked forces) **overwhelmed** **Black** **freedmen** and **militia** **defending** the **courthouse** at **Colfax**. **Dozens to 150+** **Black** men were **killed** in the **assault** and **executions** after surrender ([Wikipedia: Colfax massacre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colfax_massacre); [National Archives — U.S. v. Nash](https://www.archives.gov/fort-worth/highlights/columbus-nash)). **U.S. Army** **companies** arrived **days later**; they **documented** **dead**, **pursued** **fugitives**, and **fed** **federal** **Enforcement** **Act** **prosecutions** (**United States v. Cruikshank**, 1876, later **limited** **federal** **authority** over **private** **conspiracies**). **Colfax** fits this file as **armed** **mass** **violence** **touching** **federal** **Reconstruction** **law** and **troop** **presence**, **not** as a **standoff** where **regulars** **fired** **first**.

### Battle of Liberty Place (1874) — **White League** vs. state forces, then **U.S. Army**

**14–17 September 1874**, **New Orleans:** Roughly **5,000** **White League** **insurgents** (ex-**Confederate** **veterans**) **fought** **integrated** **Metropolitan** **Police** and **state** **militia** for **control** of **government** **buildings** in a **disputed** **governor** **race**. **President Grant** **ordered** **federal** **troops**; **insurgents** **withdrew** as **Army** **forces** **arrived**, and **Republican** **Kellogg** was **restored** ([Wikipedia: Battle of Liberty Place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Liberty_Place)). This is a **clear** **example** of **U.S.** **military** **power** **used** **against** **armed** **citizen** **paramilitaries** **on** **U.S.** **soil** **in** **peacetime** **(civil** **unrest)**.

### Ku Klux Klan Act deployments (1870–71) — federal troops **against** insurgent networks

Congress’s **Enforcement** **Acts** and **1871** **Ku** **Klux** **Klan** **Act** authorized **suspension** of **habeas** and **Army** **deployment** in **states** (e.g. **South** **Carolina**) where **Klan** **terror** **prevented** **federal** **rights** **enforcement**. **Grant** **used** **those** **powers** **intermittently** ([Wikipedia: Enforcement Acts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enforcement_Acts)). These are **large-scale** **federal** **military** **interventions** **against** **armed** **locals** **(and** **night-riding** **cells)**, **not** **single** **sieges**—listed here as **pattern** **precedent** for **domestic** **force** **multipliers**.

## 8. MOVE bombing, Philadelphia (13 May 1985)

### 8.1 Contemporary summary (Rogin / PBS)

In **May 1985**, the **Philadelphia Police Department** dropped an **improvised** explosive—a police “**entry device**”—on a **residential** **row house** at **6221 Osage Avenue** that served as the headquarters of **MOVE**, a **Black** **revolutionary** / **back-to-nature** organization founded by **John Africa**. The **bomb** and the **ensuing** **uncontrolled** **fire** killed **11** people (**six** **adults**, **five** **children** aged **7–13** per standard victim lists) and destroyed **more than 60** nearby **homes** (often cited as **61** or **65** structures). **PBS NewsHour** correspondent **Ali Rogin** summarized that chain of events in a **May 2025** segment marking the **40th** **anniversary** ([show page with transcript](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/show/the-largely-forgotten-history-of-philadelphias-police-bombing-of-black-organization-move); [video clip](https://www.pbs.org/video/forgotten-tragedy-1747508774/)).

### 8.2 What officials said they were doing (documented baseline)

- **Context:** A **1978** standoff in Powelton Village had already killed a **police officer** and yielded **MOVE** convictions; **MOVE** relocated to **6221 Osage Avenue** in **1981**. By **1985**, Mayor **Wilson Goode** and Police Commissioner **Gregore J. Sambor** publicly framed **MOVE** as a **terrorist** threat; **warrants** cited **weapons**, **threats**, and **contempt** ([Wikipedia: MOVE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOVE_%28Philadelphia_organization%29)).
- **13 May 1985:** ~~**500** officers **evacuated** adjacent row houses, cut **utilities**, and demanded surrender. After a prolonged exchange of **gunfire** (~~**90** minutes) and heavy **tear gas**, police sought to breach a **fortified rooftop bunker**.
- **Bombing:** From a **Pennsylvania State Police** helicopter, PPD bomb-squad commander **Lt. Frank Powell** dropped **two** **“entry devices”** (DuPont **Tovex** plus **FBI-supplied C-4**) onto the roof. Official **intent** was to **open** the bunker so gas could enter and occupants could be arrested—not a covert operation disguised as something else ([Wikipedia: 1985 MOVE bombing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_MOVE_bombing); *[Inquirer* — how the bomb decision was made](https://www.inquirer.com/archive/how-bomb-decision-was-made-20210506.html)).
- **Fire:** The blast **ignited** **fuel** on the roof (later reporting reconstructed **gasoline** **cans** and intense heat). **Firefighting** was **delayed** / **limited** while the **structure** burned; the fire **spread** along the row, destroying **61+** **evacuated** homes and killing **11** people inside the MOVE house—**Ramona Africa** and **Birdie Africa** survived (*[Inquirer* — how the fire spread, 1985](http://www.philly.com/archive/move-fire-philadelphia-1985-chronology-20250401.html)).

### 8.3 Answering speculation: a staged “MOVE had the bomb” story?

**Question:** Did police plan to **blame** MOVE for an explosion—e.g. claim MOVE **stockpiled** bombs that **detonated** by accident—rather than admit a **city** **airstrike**?

**Answer:** **No** in the sense of a **false-flag** “they blew themselves up” **hoax**. The bombing was **ordered openly** by **city** leadership, covered **live**, and later **investigated** as **city** **conduct**; the debate was whether the **tactic** was **lawful** and **wise**, not whether a **bomb** **fell** **at** **all**.

**Post-hoc blame-shifting (documented in press):** Some officials said the charge was only meant to **breach** the roof, not **torch** the block; Powell maintained MOVE **started** the fire; Sambor said he did not expect **Tovex** to **ignite** a **city-wide** conflagration. Investigative reporting reconstructed **gasoline** on the roof and extreme heat from the blast. The **MOVE Commission** (1986) still called dropping a bomb on an **occupied** row house **“unconscionable”**; Goode apologized (*[Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/archive/how-bomb-decision-was-made-20210506.html)*; [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1985_MOVE_bombing)).

**How did city leadership avoid criminal accountability?** Grand juries did not produce convictions that satisfied critics; a federal civil jury awarded **1.5 million** in **1996**; City Council apologized in **2020**. “Getting away with it” means **structural** impunity for violence backed by **city** power—not a lack of public record that police **bombed** the house.

**Outside the United States:** State militaries bombing or shelling **their own** or **occupied** cities in **civil** or **counterinsurgent** war is a different scale and legal frame—e.g. Russian forces in [Grozny (1994–95)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Grozny_(1994%E2%80%931995)) and [(1999–2000)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Grozny_(1999%E2%80%932000)), or urban warfare documented in UN reporting on Syria and elsewhere. Those cases involve **armies** and **mass** civilian exposure, not one **U.S.** **city** **administration** approving **demo** charges on a **single** **row** **house**.

**In this file’s scope:** MOVE remains unusual—a **U.S. city** using **military-grade** explosive **from the air** against a **domestic** neighborhood. The **federal** hook is **FBI-supplied C-4** and later **federal civil** litigation, not **FBI or Army command** of the **raid**.
