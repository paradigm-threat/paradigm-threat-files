# World War 3 Anyway

Built on every side, and Europe would not stand down

I heard jets resume their overhead training today. I felt like it's been a while, like everyone paused for the elections, and now the fight is back on.

The last long stretch over this city was the 4th Fighter Wing. F-15E Strike Eagles from the 333rd and 334th flew out of the Savannah Combat Readiness Training Center while the runway at Seymour Johnson, in Goldsboro, was being worked on. A [local radio log on 8 August](https://coastalgeorgiaradio.blogspot.com/2026/08/4th-fighter-wing-from-seymour-johnson.html) still had them flying morning through night, and a photograph on that page is dated 15 June. People here were still under them in mid-September. On [6 October](https://www.reddit.com/r/savannah/comments/1wyx21o/are_the_f15s_still_at_the_airport/) someone in town asked if the F-15s had gone back to North Carolina, and a reply that day said they were home. Then the election. Today I heard them again.

Apparently Iran thought a blue wave would be enough to stop Trump. They slowly woke up to the hangover. Trump is in charge of our military for two years. In his own words he is no longer looking for a deal with Iran.

On 22 September at the United Nations he said they were waiting to see how he did in the midterms, and that a deal would come right after. [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/trump-administration/trump-address-united-nations-general-assembly-iran-war-rcna599085) reported that waiting line. In the same stretch he said he is not on the ballot, he would be here for two and a half years, and the election does not enter his mind on Iran. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/22/unga-iran-us-trump-war) reported the two and a half years.

On 24 September, Iran's president, Masoud Pezeshkian, said he did not want it to reach the midterms. He wished the United States back to the June memorandum before the vote. [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/iran/irans-president-says-tehran-wants-deal-us-midterm-elections-rcna599638) reported both lines.

On 3 November the Democrats took both chambers. [Iranian officials celebrated](https://apnews.com/article/us-iran-midterm-election-f3c48a92df2bd8a658d3e704a7291d11). Security chief Mohsen Rezaei [posted that Trump had lost the war and the election](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202611091291). Supreme Leader Mojtaba Khamenei [said a change of party in Washington would not end American hostility](https://www.dw.com/en/iran-signals-satisfaction-skepticism-after-us-midterm-vote/a-79867149).

On 12 November Trump shut the deal track. "We don't need a deal with Iran. Those days have long since passed. We've got their money, Hundreds of Billions of Dollars, and we've got their Strait!" He pulled JD Vance off the talks and onto crime and corruption in Democratic-run cities. [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/donald-trump/us-doesnt-need-peace-deal-iran-vance-corruption-trump-rcna609027) and the [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/politics/2026/11/12/trump-shuts-down-vance-iran-negotiating-efforts-says-no-deal-needed/) both carried the post.

Nine days after that celebration, the man who still commands the military said the deal days have passed. Khamenei had already said the election would not change Washington.

A reader of American front pages for the last two months heard Iran, gas prices, and the midterms. Germany's military registry has been in force all year. Croatia put conscripts back in barracks in March. On 10 November, NATO diplomats were working a planning draft aimed at a major European war. The same week as the Abu Dhabi talks, Ireland's foreign minister lined up 113 new sanctions. Those items ran in European papers and on the wires. They did not lead the American election week.

CNN and Fox covered Germany's recruitment bill in November and December 2025. The New York Times covered the European recruiting debate on 24 November 2025. On 21 September 2026 CNN ran an essay on Europe preparing for a wider conflict. Fox carried a NATO commander in Warsaw who said that if the alliance is attacked, it will fight. Those pieces were filed as recruiting and deterrence. An American watching October and November got the election and Iran. The November collision, talks plus a war-planning draft plus sanctions, lived in the European copy.

World War 3 was remote-controlled. The machinery was built on every side before the public was asked. Russia built defensively. The United States built in advance, so a large share of the people and the equipment could go to Ukraine under Biden, and America could still fight Iran. Europe was ordered to attack anyway, so a peace cannot work. Deep state influence is the name for that build. It is the pattern of figurehead wars, and of an American hawk lane that copies Europe.

Lindsey Graham spent years on the maximal Ukraine line, then died on 11 July 2026 after a trip to Kyiv. The [BBC obituary](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cvgj25j6nmeo) filed him as a Trump ally. The jets I heard today, and the 12 November post, are the Iran war refusing to pause for the election. The part Europe can still kill is the Ukraine settlement. America stood out there. The stockpile could be spent twice. The public, and a president who ended up in the room trying to finish the Ukraine war, could not be aimed the way Europe's cabinets can.

## Built on defense in Russia

Russia armed and called men because the pressure was coming from the west.

In 1812 Napoleon marched east. In 1941 Germany marched east. Those were attacks on Russia. Russia's mobilization and its war industry in this war have that shape. They answer an army already on the western approaches. Once the disputed territories are the settlement, that machine has no further march in it.

In August 2026 the president of Finland, Alexander Stubb, told *Bild* what he could see, and [*Die Zeit*](https://www.zeit.de/politik/ausland/2026-08/alexander-stubb-finnland-russland-nato-angriff-2029) reported it. He saw neither evidence nor facts for a Russian attack on NATO by 2029. He called the crystal ball empty. His figures were an army losing 30,000 to 35,000 soldiers a month and recruiting about 27,000. On that math, an extra mobilization to invade another country was extremely unlikely. A new call-up in the autumn, to feed the war Russia was already in, was the more plausible sentence.

That grind stays inside the war Russia is already fighting.

## Built in Europe, and used anyway

The lists were in place before anyone flew to Abu Dhabi.

Germany's military-service law, the Wehrdienst-Modernisierungsgesetz, took effect on 1 January 2026. Men have to fill out the questionnaire. The medical exam is back, and a man who does not appear can be brought in. Service itself is still voluntary. Conscription of men is still in the Basic Law, and the 2011 peacetime suspension is still the rule, until the Bundestag passes another statute if volunteers run short. The government does not get to flip that switch alone. Since the start of 2026, men aged 17 to 45 have also needed approval for a stay abroad longer than three months, a rule that used to wait for a state of tension. The [German Foreign Office](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/2755596-2755596) describes the service law. [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2026/04/27/defence-which-european-countries-have-mandatory-and-voluntary-military-service) reported the travel rule. The list, the exam, and the permission to leave are how a later vote gets to the van.

France did not restore the draft. Emmanuel Macron, at an infantry base near Grenoble, said the only way to avoid danger is to prepare for it, and then: "We need to mobilise, mobilising the nation to defend itself, to be ready and remain respected." The [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c0edw7g7z79o) reported a paid ten-month service, starting around 3,000 volunteers and aimed at 50,000 by 2035. He said mobilise. He funded a few thousand volunteers.

Croatia did restore compulsory service. On 6 March 2026 [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/croatia-re-launch-mandatory-military-service-after-17-years-2026-03-06/) reported the first 800 recruits in two-month training, after a seventeen-year gap, with about 4,000 conscripts a year planned. Austria, the Baltic states, the Nordics, Greece, and others were already drafting. France, Britain, and Germany were still on voluntary service.

Then the week of 10 to 17 November, with the laws already on the books.

On 10 and 11 November, Russia, Ukraine, and the United States sat down in Abu Dhabi for the first direct talks in nine months. They agreed in principle to a prisoner exchange. Jared Kushner called it the biggest humanitarian release of the war. There was no ceasefire and no map. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/europe/us-russia-ukraine-start-second-day-abu-dhabi-peace-talks-2026-11-11/) reported the second day.

The same day the talks opened, [POLITICO](https://www.politico.eu/article/nato-shrink-forces-missions-abroad-focus-fight-russia/) reported a draft of NATO's political guidance that would stop requiring the alliance to prepare for a major European war and for crisis missions somewhere else at the same time. The word in the draft was or. A major war in Europe would come first. Camille Grand, a former NATO assistant secretary-general, described a clear understanding that a major contingency in Europe overtakes any other priority. The capability targets in that planning run toward 2029. General Carsten Breuer, the Bundeswehr's inspector general, had already put the date in public: ready by 2029, and "we must be able to fight tonight." The [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c62v63gl8rvo) carried that line.

On 17 November the talks were still live and still unsigned. Volodymyr Zelenskyy called the session with the American envoys and the Europeans "not easy," and said they would meet the Coalition of the Willing in Berlin. Kushner wrote that important challenges remain, and that a final agreement will require difficult decisions. Trump told reporters he thought Vladimir Putin was backing down a little, and that he would like to see it finish. The same day, Irish Foreign Minister Helen McEntee paired "renewed talks" with 113 new sanctions and more military support, and said Russia's position had not changed. Dmitry Peskov said Moscow wants a peace of "years, even centuries," not a pause that lets Ukraine recover. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/17/ukraine-holds-peace-talks-with-us-europe-as-conflict-escalates) reported the day.

Moscow still refused Europe a seat. The capitals that want the seat are the capitals that have offered troops after a ceasefire. A settlement that puts those soldiers on Ukrainian ground is a settlement Russia has already said it will not sign with their governments in the room. A settlement that leaves the disputed territories without those soldiers is the settlement those same governments have spent years calling a prelude to the next attack. Either rule lets a few cabinets hold a peace the US envoys are trying to close.

A [GLOBSEC-linked count of 71 Ukrainian security experts](https://www.dw.com/en/what-have-the-talks-with-russia-and-the-us-in-abu-dhabi-achieved-for-ukraine/a-79930613), reported by Deutsche Welle, put the chance of a peace or even a ceasefire this year or next at 19 percent. No ceasefire was signed. The machine was already built, and this week it did not switch off.

## The street

Ukraine already pulls men off the street.

The practice has a name there: busification. Men are stopped in public, checked, and put in vans for the recruitment center. Ukraine's ombudsman, Dmytro Lubinets, counted 18 complaints at the start of the full-scale war in 2022 and 6,127 by the end of 2025. His office told [France 24](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20261006-busification-inside-ukraine-s-controversial-recruitment-practices) it had processed 5,024 requests between 1 January and 3 September 2026. Monitoring visits recorded illegal detention, beatings, and coercion, including of men who had deferments. In April 2024 the draft age dropped from 27 to 25. On 8 July 2026 a crowd in Lviv overturned a recruitment vehicle. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/19/in-ukraine-forcible-military-recruitment-efforts-escalate-into-violence) reported the street phase getting more violent through 2026.

[YouGov](https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/54146-four-years-into-the-ukraine-war-where-does-western-european-opinion-stand), four years into the war, found support for sending soldiers as peacekeepers after a deal at 66 percent in Denmark, 62 percent in Britain, 48 percent in France, 44 percent in Germany, and 35 percent in Italy. An earlier wave in the same series found France at 68 percent and Germany at 58 percent for mandatory service for young people. The publics are split. In Germany and Italy, support for stationing troops is the weaker number.

Germany's registry is the list and the exam. Service is still voluntary, and the needs-based draft still needs another law. The registry is how that later vote finds the man. Ukraine shows what the vote is for, once a government decides the volunteers are not enough. I don't think there's any reason that can't happen in Europe now, even where a large share of people are against the war.

## The reasons they gave

The Napoleonic wars of 1812 were technically a world war. World War Zero. They made no sense, and they got blamed on one person, like World War II. That is the proof we repeat our mistakes after learning about them in school. School teaches Napoleon. Later it teaches Hitler. Then a new war arrives with a new face.

I recently found the Secret War as a major missing puzzle piece that seems to explain everything about modern warfare. It sits between 1812 and World War I. It stayed secret only because a war between planets is the only way a war stays secret.

We were not meant to have all this technology. It leaked out through successive conflicts. The one that got out in public was the War of the Worlds event, apparently true, and the leak was in Boston. London is the novel that sealed the shelf. [The Secret War](https://paradigmthreat2.substack.com/p/the-secret-war) is that reconstruction. Much of the war since looks like containment of that leak, and of the people who knew anything. McKinley was killed to cover the war. Edison was the public face put on the kit. Anyone else who knew a secret was in the same set, whatever the particular secret was. Electric flying vehicles, nuclear engines, and the rest surfaced briefly in public knowledge and were suppressed on the way into the twentieth century. The 1896–97 airships appeared and vanished. Garrett P. Serviss put electrical ships in print in 1898 and then the shelf stopped retelling him.

Inside that ignorance it was easy to prepare people for global war with crude weapons and diesel engines, and to scare them with radiation, while the harm that kept showing up was depleted uranium. The Hollywood bomb, the disintegrating flash for miles, is the hoax half. Depleted uranium is real and cancer-causing. The public was aimed at the mushroom cloud and was not taught the depleted-uranium threat. That split is the [nuclear half-hoax](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/nukes/) already on the record, and it is in the Iran accounting of [Mission Accomplished (Again)](https://paradigmthreat2.substack.com/p/mission-accomplished-again-what-the).

The sloppiness of all of it, and the desperate attempts to look away from life on Mars and from flying cars, could only be explained as an incomplete containment of a leak of knowledge we were never meant to have. The seal never held. Then the schoolbook wars started, with diesel and a face to blame.

World War I made no sense. Over terrorism? Really? On 28 June 1914 Gavrilo Princip shot Archduke Franz Ferdinand in Sarajevo. A Serbian secret society sat behind him. That killing is still the reason they teach. The alliances are what the schoolbook uses to turn one murder into a continent. [The schoolbook calls it an accident](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/war/investigations/reverse-crusades-comparison.md), so nobody is responsible. A world war over that act does not make sense.

America had no business entering World War II. Pearl Harbor was clearly not a good enough reason, even if real Japanese pilots were involved. It was little more than a deception some influence pulled off, and no reason to do what America did after. Not a day that lived in infamy as much as the rest of the carnage caused by America entering the war and firebombing so many Japanese islands and cities. Mark Selden's accounting of the incendiary campaign, excluding Hiroshima and Nagasaki, is 67 cities, 180 square miles destroyed, more than 300,000 dead, and another 400,000 injured. The [Asia-Pacific Journal](https://apjjf.org/-Mark-Selden/2414/article.html) is that count. Russia would not have done that had we let them take Japan. We looked insane. They did not. [The West was pulled into that war at the wrong time for the wrong reasons](https://paradigmthreat2.substack.com/p/mission-accomplished-again-what-the). The harbor attack is in the [Pearl Harbor investigation](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/war/investigations/pearl-harbor-hijacked-imperial-japan-investigation.md).

Now that the USSR is gone and the post-Cold War is hot, we still look insane. They still do not.

World War III makes the least sense of all. The reason on offer is that Russia will attack us if we do not commit to a full world war to stop them. There is no evidence for that attack. The poor reasoning is the Minsk agreements, and the history of that region in general.

Minsk was a ceasefire and a political deal for the Donbas. Special status, elections, and a fight over who would control the border, and when. The deal sat inside Ukraine. In December 2022 Angela Merkel told [*Die Zeit*](https://www.zeit.de/2022/53/angela-merkel-russland-krieg-wladimir-putin) that the 2014 Minsk agreement was an attempt to give Ukraine time, and that Ukraine used the time to become stronger. That boast stays inside the Donbas war. A settlement of the disputed territories does not become a Russian invasion of NATO, or of the United States, on the strength of that sentence.

The history that actually sits there is Crimea, the Donbas, 2014, and the territories still on the table. Zelensky's letter compresses Maidan, Minsk, 2014, and the language law into one guilt story. [Zelensky's letter is a memory weapon](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/war/zelensky-forward-history-memory-war.md) is that compression. Napoleon in 1812 and Germany in 1941 were attacks on Russia. The loss on the table now is the disputed Ukrainian territories. Stubb said he had no evidence of a NATO attack by 2029. Germany's operations chief said in November 2025 that Russia could not do the large assault while the army was tied down in Ukraine. By November 2026 Russia was still tied down in Ukraine. I can't imagine a threat story past those territories that holds.
