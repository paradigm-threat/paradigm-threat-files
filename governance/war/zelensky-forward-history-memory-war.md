NATO, geopolitics, language — named and sealed out. Year zero: February 2022. Former FM Kuleba (June 2026, Tier B) read the signal as confidence, not sincere negotiation — which fits an open letter: transparency for observers, not a back channel.

## The simplified Jew — before the Holocaust, after the memory

Historians and commemoration often write “the Jew” as one actor. Prewar reality was plural; postwar memory collapsed that plurality into a single survivor type — increasingly Israel-aligned in Atlantic public life, though many survivors never fit that mold (Bundists, Haredim, Soviet Jews, anti-Zionist Orthodox, Mizrahi worlds elsewhere). The full grading ladder — rhetoric, law, east–west gradient, compliance categories — lives in [dossier §0.1 and §4](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/genocide/investigations/holodomor-holocaust-memory-redaction-investigation.md); here the pattern only.

Nazi talk graded in layers. Rhetoric (Ostjude, Judeo-Bolshevism, “foreign” Jew) rode Weimar immigrant panic (Tier A–B). Law fixed Jew by ancestry from November 1935 supplements (Tier A). Lethal pressure arrived later and unevenly — east harder, Reich west softer until 1941, when deportation lists and survival rates diverge (Tier A–B). Documented Mischling and hidden-identity categories exist; how many defined Jews “passed” by breaking community ties is unquantified (Tier B/S). That rhyme with immigrant frenzy in the 1930s and racial removal after 1941 does not mean compliance reliably saved defined Jews, or that westernized Reich Jews were never targeted.

Nazi talk conflated Jew with Eastern and Russian-revolutionary threat — a pogrom ideology upgraded to state power. Regional Jewish cultures (Yiddish east, assimilated west, Sephardi elsewhere) carried different legal and social positions. Only from the east does the density of Yiddish / Pale / revolutionary association make sense as geography — without inventing a secret cabal.

After the Holocaust, that eastern source-map was largely lost in public. Money, food, and administration tropes moved to banned conspiracy grammar; crypto-Jew and interfaith survival histories became untouchable or caricature; “Jew” simplified to one identity slot — producing modern frictions (Beta Israel recognition, “who counts as Jewish” fights) when state and synagogue pretend one definition always existed. Commemoration politics and IHRA memory flatten diversity (Tier B). A stronger read (Tier S): the flattening severed Horde/Russian lineage from Jewish public memory and relocated sacred geography under Scaligerian chronology compression — see [prisca sapientia](https://paradigmthreat.net/docs/PRISCA_SAPIENTIA.md); not established here as fact.

Pre-modern ruling networks spanned Eurasia; 1917–45 fractured them into nation-states where Jews and others could hold local power — producing pogrom chemistry when old imperial memory met new borders. Holodomor and Holocaust sit on that same Bloodlands strip ([Snyder bridge — dossier §19](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/genocide/investigations/holodomor-holocaust-memory-redaction-investigation.md#19-bloodlands-frame-snyder--investigative-bridge)); Reverse Crusade reads WWII as contain Russia, not only defeat Hitler ([Paperclip file](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/war/investigations/operation-paperclip-nazi-continuity-and-wwii-narrative-investigation.md)). ## Holodomor — the genocide class forgot

A decade before peak Holocaust killing on overlapping territory, the Ukrainian SSR suffered state-engineered famine. Best demographic consensus: ~3.9M excess deaths 1932–34 in Ukraine ([Wolowyna et al., 2015](https://journals.library.ualberta.ca/csp/index.php/csp/article/download/21772/18080/0); [dossier §17](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/genocide/investigations/holodomor-holocaust-memory-redaction-investigation.md#17-holodomor-demographic-consensus-sheet)). Soviet memory erased it; Western Holocaust machinery later displaced it in classrooms. Western publics still map genocide onto Belsen/Dachau (camp-photo west) while most Jewish killing happened east of Germany ([dossier §3, §5](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/genocide/investigations/holodomor-holocaust-memory-redaction-investigation.md)).

![Holodomor — famine mortality on Ukrainian soil, classroom displacement](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/genocide/holocaust/holodomor.png)

December 1932 Moscow resolutions tied grain quotas to anti-Ukrainization and trade starvation — primary register in [dossier §2.5](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/genocide/investigations/holodomor-holocaust-memory-redaction-investigation.md#25-moscow-stated-rationales-193233--primary-register). Soft history converts that paper trail into “Stalin authored a stoppable genocide.” Author lock: Holodomor was baked into the communist revolution; Stalin was not an absolute tip who could cancel Holodomor-scale rural death inside that OS ([§2.7](https://paradigmthreat.net/governance/genocide/investigations/holodomor-holocaust-memory-redaction-investigation.md#27-author-lock--stalin-agency--holodomor-inevitability-under-communism-2026-08-09)).
