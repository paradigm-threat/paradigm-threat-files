## 3. Arizona — routers, logs denied; expensive recount as validation

### Documented

Maricopa County’s 2021 Arizona Senate review (**Cyber Ninjas** and contractors) received **ballots**, **tabulators**, and large volumes of materials, but the county **refused** to hand **network routers** and **Splunk logs** to the Senate contractors — citing security, unrelated confidential data, and operational risk ([AP](https://apnews.com/article/technology-joe-biden-arizona-senate-elections-voter-registration-ac12771352412717aa3799435241d8c7); [AZ Mirror](https://azmirror.com/2021/09/17/county-senate-reach-agreement-to-end-subpoena-fight-over-routers/)).

Settlement: a **special master** (John Shadegg) reviewed routers/logs under limited questions — **not** open Cyber Ninjas network forensics ([AZ Central](https://www.azcentral.com/story/news/politics/elections/2021/09/17/arizona-audit-maricopa-county-reaches-deal-arizona-senate/8374051002/); [Shadegg report on DocumentCloud](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/27912041-shadegg-special-master-final-report/)).

County officials and allied messaging treated the ballot-heavy review as answering “did fraud flip Arizona?” while the **network-log** fight was framed as a side issue.

### Author read

That split **is** the method: spend millions on a **spectacle recount** that returns a **no-outcome-changing-fraud** headline, while the **audit surface that could show remote access / log deletion / network events** stays behind special-master walls or county refusal. The public remembers “Arizona audited; Trump still lost.”

**Tier:** Router/log refusal + special-master path = **Documented**. “Staged to validate no-fraud” = **Author**. Cyber Ninjas competence / conflict-of-interest critiques = **Documented controversy** (separate from whether network forensics were blocked).
