''The satellite tracks the two missiles as they break off parts and launch each of their six warheads, which will detonate all along the U.S. East Coast in 15 minutes and thirty seconds. The satellite then tracks the [[Special Air Service/Modern Warfare|SAS]]/[[United States Marine Corps/Modern Warfare|Marine]] teams that have [[All In|breached the facility]], which is being analyzed. The team will have to go from the vents down to the barracks, go through the storage area, go across the launch silos, pass a heavy blast door, and finally into the launch control room.''

'''[[Gaz]]''': Team One moving in.

'''Marine''': Team Two heading for base security.

'''SAS''': Team Three has entered the base.

'''[[Overlord|Command]]''': Bravo Team, we got good news and bad news. Launch control is located southwest of your position, less than half a klick away. That's where you'll need to upload the abort codes to destroy the missiles in flight.

'''[[John Price|Captain Price]]''': Gaz, go with the Yanks and hit the security station. [[John "Soap" MacTavish|Soap]], [[Griggs]], and I will head for launch control.

'''Captain Price''': The hell with it. We'll give it our best shot. Out.

''The satellite tracks Sgt. "Soap" MacTavish in the facility's air vents.''

'''Captain Price''': Yeah... well... get in line mate... if he doesn't find us first...

'''Captain Price''': All right, get in the trucks! Let's go!

'''SSgt. Griggs''': You heard the man! Move!
