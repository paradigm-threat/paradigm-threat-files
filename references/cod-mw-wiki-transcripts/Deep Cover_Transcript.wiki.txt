== Cutscene ==
'''THREE HOURS EARLIER'''

''[[Nikolai (Reboot)|Nikolai]] is in a hangar working on maintenance for his helicopter while the song "'''Bonebreaker'''" by Slaughter to Prevail plays in the background. [[Kate Laswell]] calls out to him from behind.''

'''Laswell''': Nik... Nikolai!

''Nikolai stops working.''

'''Nikolai''': Laswell...

'''Laswell''': Really...?

''Laswell walks over to Nikolai's laptop and switches off the music player. Nikolai turns to face Laswell.''

'''Nikolai''': [[Vladimir Makarov (Reboot)|Makarov]]?

'''Laswell''': Word travels fast.

'''Nikolai''': Only if he wants it too...

''Nikolai climbs down from the helicopter fuselage.''

'''Laswell''': You think he's working for the [[Kremlin]]?

'''Nikolai''': I wouldn't be surprised.

'''Laswell''': I have [[Yuri Volkov|a contact]] who might know the answer. I need transport.

'''Nikolai''': Where?

'''Laswell''': Arklov Military Base.

'''Nikolai''': It's heavily guarded.

'''Laswell''': Can you do it?

'''Nikolai''': To stop Makarov... Of course.

'''Laswell''': I need to get in and back out. Could go upside down quickly.

'''Nikolai''': I specialize in upside down, Laswell.

''A spark bursts from Nikolai's helicopter, prompting Nikolai to bang his hand against the fuselage.''

'''Laswell''': That's what I'm afraid of...

'''Nikolai''': Who's your contact?

'''Laswell''': Yuri Volkov.

''Nikolai chuckles.''

'''Nikolai''': Yuri's on the payroll?

''Laswell smiles.''

'''Laswell''': Yuri never took a dime.

'''Nikolai''': That's Yuri... When do we leave?

'''Laswell''': How's now...?

''The scene shifts to [[Verdansk]] as Nikolai's helicopter flies above the mountains below. Suddenly, Nikolai pilots his helicopter into a sharp dive and flies close to the ground. Inside the cockpit, Laswell wears [[Soviet Armed Forces|Russian army]] fatigues as she steadies herself.''

'''Laswell''': Nice...

'''Nikolai''': We need to fly low...

'''Laswell''': Under the radar...

'''Nikolai:''' Exactly.

''Laswell gives a thumbs up.''

'''Nikolai''': Exactly. We are a mile out...

'''Laswell''': Don't get compromised. Dust off and go... Call you when I need you.

'''Nikolai''': Roger.

''Laswell looks out the window and sees the Arklov military base in the distance. Nikolai lands his helicopter on the ground.''

'''Nikolai''': Go-go-go!

''Laswell jumps out of the chopper.''

'''Laswell''': I'm clear!

'''Nikolai''': Copy!

''Nikolai takes off and flies away.''

''Cut to black.''

'''Nikolai''': Copy, Watcher. Shit's about to get real... Keep a low profile...

'''Laswell''': Major's breaking away. Heading upstairs towards an office.

''Laswell follows the Major inside the building as the Major speaks to a female Russian soldier.''

'''Kastovian Major''': Status on the communications array?

''Laswell guns down the Major and the female soldier quietly.''

'''Laswell''': He's down.

''(If the Major spots Laswell...)''

'''Kastovian Major''': Show me your ID, soldier.

''Laswell takes the keycard from the dead Major's body.''

'''Laswell''': Target eliminated. Keycard secured.

'''Nikolai''': He took one for the team...

'''Laswell''': Had to be done.

'''Nikolai''': That one's on Makarov, not you...

'''Laswell''': Yuri better make this worth our while...

''A Russian guard notices Laswell nearby and gets suspicious.''

'''Kastovian Soldier''': Who's that?

'''Laswell''': Got some unwanted attention, here...

''Laswell uses the keycard to enter the administrative building and takes off her military cover.''

'''Laswell''': I'm in.

'''Nikolai''': Laswell, Yuri is in the storage room. Take the hallway to the right of the entrance. It should be there.

''Laswell goes down the hallway and opens the door to the storage room.''

'''Laswell''': Makin' entry./I'm there, Nik. Out.

'''Laswell''': Yuri...

'''Yuri''': Watcher... I'm impressed, but... Not surprised. 

''Laswell and Yuri walk around the shelves to greet each other.''

'''Yuri''': Any issues?

'''Laswell''': Several.

'''Yuri''': Collateral damage?

'''Laswell''': I hope it's worth their sacrifice.

''Yuri offers his cigarette to Laswell, who accepts it. He chuckles.''

'''Laswell''': What do you have?

''Yuri walks over to a desk and drops a USB device on it.''

'''Yuri''': FSB files on [[Roman Barkov|Barkov's]] research.

'''Laswell''': Chemical weapons.

'''Yuri''': As a soldier, Makarov was... enamored with their potential. 

'''Laswell''': Can they be neutralized?

'''Yuri''': I'm giving you everything we know.

''Laswell takes a drag from the cigarette.''

'''Laswell''': Why? 

'''Yuri''': [[Russia/Modern Warfare|Russia]] and the West face similar threats from within. It is imperative we collaborate to control them. ''Противник моего противника — мой союзник.'' (The opponent of my opponent is my ally.)

''Yuri gives Laswell the USB device.''

'''Laswell''': ''Николай бы согласился'' (Nikolai would agree.)

''Laswell hands back Yuri his cigarette. He takes a drag from it.''

'''Yuri''': ''[chuckles]'' ''Настоящий патриот''. (A true patriot.) The Kremlin is not behind these chemicals.

'''Yuri''': There are extremists in [[United States of America/Modern Warfare|both]] our governments...

'''Laswell''': Come again, Bravo?

'''Price''': Kate- ''[static]'' Laswell, are you there!?

'''Laswell''': Bravo, your signal's weak. Come again.

'''Price''': This is 6, in the blind! We have two missiles incoming to the Arklov Base! They have chemical warheads! If you are there, get out!

'''Yuri''': This could be a drill.

'''Laswell''': It's not...

''An explosion shakes the base, causing Laswell and Yuri to stumble.''

'''Laswell''': There's another one coming!
