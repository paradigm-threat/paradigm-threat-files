## Paradigm Threat Project

The goal of Paradigm Threat is to pose an **intellectual challenge** to the reigning paradigms of every discipline — history, cosmology, medicine, physics, religion, finance, governance — using sourced research, cross-referenced chronologies, and a willingness to follow the evidence. Investigations assume [**prisca sapientia**](/docs/PRISCA_SAPIENTIA.md) ([timeline overview](https://timeline.paradigmthreat.net/timeline/evt-prisca-sapientia)): ancient understanding was profound and later degraded, not that every modern textbook is an upgrade.

Much of western "conspiracy media" is [controlled opposition](https://caitlinjohnstone.com/2019/02/11/how-to-tell-if-someone-is-controlled-opposition/) — conflating verifiable falsehood with real threads to mock the whole inquiry. This project refuses that sloppiness.

Method: assume the solution already exists, then ask how evidence is kept from the public. The most effective hide is **in plain sight** — [Revelation of the Method](https://www.counterpunch.org/2022/04/29/revelation-of-the-method/) and media [predictive programming](https://joyoffaith.com/predictive-programming.html). [Transactional analysis](https://www.simplypsychology.org/transactional-analysis-eric-berne.html) helps name what the viewer is compelled to believe or *not* believe.
