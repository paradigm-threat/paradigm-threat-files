### Transactional Analysis of Predictive Programming

My method for solving conspiracy theories is to start with the basic assumption that the solution already exists,
and then determine by what means the evidence of a conspiracy is being kept from the public.
The most effective method for hiding a conspiracy seems to be **in plain sight** resulting in the phenomenon -
[Revelation of the Method](https://www.counterpunch.org/2022/04/29/revelation-of-the-method/) where a piece of the
influence/conspiracy is *revealed* to the public in media [programming](https://joyoffaith.com/predictive-programming.html).
In this strategy, whenever a conspiracy theory is explained to a person who has already received
programming on the theory, they may give the programmed response, which may include mockery, incredulity, our even
outright hostility. They will also likely take as a personal attack the supposition that they could have been fooled by
any predictive programming in media to begin with.
I think the [MI6](https://michaelhoffman.substack.com/p/secrets-of-the-cia) would agree with me that no other method
could be as effective or as devastating to our sense of reality.
This project focuses on predictive programming that exists in historic literature as well as modern media.
To understand the motivations behind *predictive programming*,
[transactional analysis](https://www.simplypsychology.org/transactional-analysis-eric-berne.html)
allows us to determine precisely what the viewer is being compelled into believing,
or *not* believing.
