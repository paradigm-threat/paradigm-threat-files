## What the new lanes argue (in plain terms)

**The Internet That Should Have Been** is the computing flagship for 2026. Tim Berners-Lee discussed a machine-processable web in **1994**; Windows had OLE Automation; CORBA had IDL; later WSDL, Semantic Web, OpenAPI, Anthropic MCP, and Google's **WebMCP** (Feb 2026). The **idea** of opening the hood was never secret. What never arrived was a **neutral layer** crawlable and teachable by ordinary people. Instead: **hypertext for humans**, **hidden APIs for insiders**, **feeds**, **walled gardens**, and **algorithmic show/hide**. The essay's **car metaphor** is deliberate: you may drive and wash the paint; only the **dealership technician** may open the sealed panel — and in **2026** that technician is increasingly the **browser vendor's AI agent**.

The hardware arc in **§II** compresses the [Quantum Leap (of faith)](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/computing/quantum-leap-of-faith) lane: IBM's **DOS cliff** (Microsoft kept and resold the standard), Wintel lock-in, Nintendo **CD containment** ([Nintendo–Sony breakup](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/stolen_credit/nintendo-sony-breakup) prequel), GPU→crypto→AI furnace economics. **§III** names enclosure: Berners-Lee **"devastated"** by what the Mesh became; Semantic Web hijacked into the Facebook **Like**; *Starship Troopers* (1997) as **TV-channel internet** preview — Federal Network plus **"Would you like to know more?"** navigation buttons, civic software utility **out of frame**. **React / Hermes / Vercel**: industry blocked a **universal VM**; **WASM** is the honest substrate; Hermes is **RN-only** in practice (author lived attempt). **Opaque reach**: this site's articles are **barely seen** without **podcast bypass** around algorithmic shadow-ban; **layered-reality hijack** — most visitors may get **substituted** experiences in the pipe — requires **global monopolization** to work at scale.

**§IV–VI** cover the **AI gate** (Chrome **~4 GB Gemini Nano**, WebMCP as **SEO for agents**), courts wobbling on AI citations, **Terminator 2029** as Moore-schedule predictive programming (*T3* virus ↔ desktop Nano distribution), and **§VII bridge under protest** — use the tools, distrust the principal, migrate off platform throats toward **Relay mirrors**. Full tiering: [687-line investigation dossier](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/computing/investigations/internet-that-should-have-been-investigation) (DOS §9.6, Starship Troopers §10.2.1, opaque reach §10.5, Terminator §11.4).

Installment I named schooling as conformity engine; Installment II names **forward duty** in the AI era: ***Fantastic Planet*** headset dystopia vs **knowledge at your side**; **ethics and QA** as the reduced educator scope; **lab, gym, tutor** irreplaceable; **human-validated** literacy; parent checklist.
