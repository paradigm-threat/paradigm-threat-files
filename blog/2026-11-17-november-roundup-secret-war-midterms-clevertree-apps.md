## Three new apps

These are Clevertree products, separate from the essay archive. Each one is in public use while it is still being built. Each one has a place to start, and each one can take help from a person who has never opened the research files.

### [MyProblem](https://myproblem.app)

[MyProblem](https://myproblem.app) is a free front door for local problems and for local businesses. A shop can send a person into a ticket instead of a phone loop. The person writes, talks, and shows photos. One question stays on the screen until the request is specific. A language model investigates the ticket in a batch and leaves a dossier before anyone has to meet. It is not a live chat with the shop. Businesses pay nothing and owe nothing: no application fee, no subscription, and no duty to answer a lead. If the help was good, a tip is welcome. It is never required. Pilots are in Georgia and Israel. Status and how to help: [myproblem.app/dev](https://myproblem.app/dev). A ticket can be started at [myproblem.app/submit](https://myproblem.app/submit).

### [Clevertree Chat](https://clevertree.chat)

[Clevertree Chat](https://clevertree.chat) — Cleverchat for short — is a debate that stays on a page. You write one post. It lands on the point you are making. It does not sink under a reply chain. A topic and a one-line argument make the address. Sentences that make the same argument share a key. A different reason stays beside it, so someone watching can see the debate. The language model files the claims and writes short positions. It does not vote, and it does not pick a winner. People do. The public lines on the page are “A new way to debate,” “Don’t get lost in the weeds,” and “Make your opinion finally heard.” A browser extension is at [clevertree.chat/extension](https://clevertree.chat/extension). Build notes are at [clevertree.chat/dev](https://clevertree.chat/dev). Boards and the API are at [api.clevertree.chat](https://api.clevertree.chat).

On 15 November, park chat at [paradigmthreat.net/chat](https://paradigmthreat.net/chat) was wired so the mesh IRC channels (`#pt` and `#pt-<topic>`) sync to Clevertree boards through the IRC bridge. The same rooms are the place guest questions on the site get an answer.

### [QuizGate](https://quizgate.app)

[QuizGate](https://quizgate.app) is a free Android app for family tablets and phones. Soft quizzes unlock screen time. A correct answer earns the next stretch. The usual rhythm is about every half hour, with a morning gate and quiet hours overnight. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscription. A language model curates curriculum packs in a closed loop. Nothing on the child’s device becomes an open generative feed. Parents keep every final decision, and sync can be turned off.
