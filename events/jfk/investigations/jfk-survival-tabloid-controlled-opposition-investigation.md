## 1. Author’s originating thesis

> **Author (2026-08-20) — pattern stakes:**
>
> Investigate this and other **tabloid controlled opposition leaks**. The idea was to get the public **used to** the sensational claims made by leakers and conspiracy theorists that followed the JFK assassination. In fact, **mocking conspiracy theorists** and **hardening the society against them** was the **main goal** of nearly all of these leaks **and the hoax itself**. The evidence presented is usually a **blurry photograph without any citations**. The tabloids are designed to look like **complete dead ends**, the kind of thing that would be **absolutely useless** in a serious research project, even with misleading statements and disprovable facts. But the point is that there **were printed articles** that came out for **many years** that suggested that **JFK was not killed**, that he **survived** the assassination, and that he was **hiding in secret** — which is **precisely what we’re investigating**: the possibility that he actually **did** go along with a **death hoax**.

**What this means (unpack):** The survival claim is **not** owned by Weekly World News. WWN and peers **colonized** the claim with **garbage exhibits** so that “JFK didn’t die in Dallas” sounds like **Bat Boy** and **Elvis at Burger King**. Serious readers learn: *only cranks say that*. The site thesis — **coerced / agreed staged death**, not a blurry wheelchair photo — then inherits the **ridicule field** those covers fertilized. Same grammar as Kickin/Bran Flakes on *Friday* and Edison/Dominion on 2020: **poisoned hinge**, not adjudication.

### Surface (as printed)

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Masthead / date | **WEEKLY WORLD NEWS** — **November 6, 1990** |
| Main headline | **JFK PHOTOGRAPHED AT SECRET HIDEOUT!** |
| Photo | Grainy outdoor still; woman leaning over seated elderly figure in wheelchair; **white circle** on face |
| Caption | “Exclusive pictures show Jackie with crippled Kennedy **PROVING HE DIDN’T DIE IN DALLAS!**” |
| Side box | Aged JFK headshot — “Will he run for President in **1992**?” |
| Bottom | Circular crop of the face + “See the **$million** check that can be yours — for leading us to JFK!” |

### Documented context

WWN (1979–2007 print) was the black-and-white supermarket tabloid spun from the **National Enquirer** press line when the Enquirer went color — openly in the **fiction / absurdist** lane for most of its life ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weekly_World_News); [TIME](https://time.com/archive/6908434/the-late-great-weekly-world-news/)). TIME notes JFK as an **evergreen**: “would show up every few years glimpsed behind estate gates.”

### Author read of this cover

Every design choice trains the wrong response:

| Design | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Blurry photo + hand-drawn circle | “Proof” that cannot be audited |
| No citation / no chain of custody | Useless in serious research |
| Jackie + wheelchair melodrama | Soap-opera, not forensics |
| 1992 presidential tease | Absurd escalation → laugh track |
| $million tip check | Marks the story as carnival contest |
| Checkout-line placement | Millions of eyes; zero scholarly respect |

**Tier:** Cover contents as described from the archived scan = **Documented (this file’s asset)**. CO *intent* of the cover = **Author**. That “JFK survived Dallas” is the **same proposition** the site investigates under **staged death** = **Author** (parallel claim; different evidence stack).
