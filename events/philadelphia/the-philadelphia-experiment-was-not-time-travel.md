Men aboard the SS Andrew Furuseth in Norfolk watched a destroyer **appear in the water** — reportedly from Philadelphia — in **minutes**, not centuries. On **28 October 1943**, per the richest secondary account chain, the Furuseth crew saw the event from Norfolk while the trial ran at the Navy Yard. **Disappearance** and **reappearance** were **separate functions** of the same device — not two names for one trick. When the coil shut off, survivors were **fused into hull steel**, **phased invisible** and back, **frozen** in comatose nets, or caught in fires that would not extinguish. Some men were **lost during the transit itself**, not only after shutdown. No one turned permanently invisible. **No one time traveled.**

## What the Navy denies

The Philadelphia Naval Shipyard ran serious electromagnetic research in WWII — **degaussing**, radar ECM, rotating fields against mines. The Navy denies any operational teleport of **USS Eldridge** (DE-173). Deck logs place Eldridge **away from Philadelphia** in October 1943. Debunkers cite Allende hoax confessions and misread degaussing as the whole story.

I record the debunk layer and do not treat it as closure. Degaussing is **real adjacent tech** — the kind of cover that lets a stranger hear "rotating magnetic field" and stop asking. It does not explain the **ONR-paid Varo reprint** of Jessup's annotated UFO book, the **specificity of hand-laying fire**, or the **paired-harbor grammar** (Philadelphia dock ↔ Norfolk) in the richest narrative corpus. ## Allende's letters — the detail-rich layer

The best single source is **Carl Meredith Allen** ("Carlos Miguel Allende") writing to Morris K. Jessup in **1955–1956** — preserved and analyzed by [David Halperin](https://www.davidhalperin.net/the-philadelphia-experiment-the-allende-mystery/) and others. Allende claimed service aboard the Furuseth and described the trial from the **Norfolk witness** side: Philadelphia → Norfolk in **very few minutes**, then return.

Inside an **oblate spheroid field** roughly **a hundred yards** across, crew looked **vague in form** — heat-mirage limbs, men **walking on nothing** inside the bubble. **"Going Blank"** could feel pleasant; **"Getting Stuck"** was **"Hell Incorporated"** — semi-comatose freeze in a subjective nether world, unaware of ordinary time passing. **"The Flame"** froze men and set them alight; one victim burned **eighteen days** while shipmates tried **laying on of hands** — carrying **compasses** when the fire would not stop — until **"The faith in Hand Laying Died."** Two or more crew had to **touch a frozen man quickly**; bystanders at the Navy Yard were told to join if they saw **wavering digits**. Men **fused into the hull**, **walked through a quarters wall**, and flickered **in and out of visibility** during the run. Allende's verdict: **"The experiment Was a Complete Success. The Men were Complete Failures."**

That is **space routing with catastrophic bio-EM storage** — not a chronology machine. Time distortion in the letters is **subjective freeze** for stuck crew, not travel to another year.

## Jessup, Varo, and the artifact chain

The Philadelphia story does not begin in 1943 pop culture. It surfaces a decade later through **Morris K. Jessup**, an Indiana University–trained scholar who wrote *The Case for the UFO* (1955) — a serious, if fringe, argument that unidentified craft might be extraterrestrial. **Allende's letters** arrived as marginal commentary on Jessup's world: Philadelphia, Einstein's unified field, an **electromagnetic bottle**, a destroyer that **vanished and reappeared** between harbors.

Then the chain goes institutional. In **1957**, the **Office of Naval Research** reportedly **paid** for a limited **Varo edition** reprint of Jessup's book — not because the Navy endorsed UFOs, but because **margin annotations** from **three** anonymous debaters looked **operationally literate**. The notes debate propulsion, field coupling, and trial outcomes in the language of engineers, not carnival barkers. They argue about **electromagnetic bottles**, **unified field** references attributed to **Einstein**, and whether ship-scale field stress could explain UFO maneuvering without invoking aliens at all. The Varo edition is a **documented artifact** whether or not the teleport happened.

Moore and Berlitz would later (**1979**) name the operation **Project Rainbow** and package the letters for mass readership — but the **artifact chain** already existed: Allende's **1955–1956** correspondence, Jessup's published UFO case, ONR's **paid reprint** with stranger marginalia than the main text. That sequence is hard to square with "random hoaxer mails a professor." Hoaxers do not typically trigger **limited government-funded reprints** of annotated fringe books.

Jessup was found dead in **1959** — his car run off the road, exhaust routed into the cabin. Officially suicide; the timing and the Varo episode keep the file open. [*Skeptical Inquirer*](https://skepticalinquirer.org/2021/08/solving-a-ufological-murder-the-case-of-morris-k-jessup/) treats much of the Allende correspondence as hoax-adjacent — fair on personality, weak on **why ONR would fund annotated UFO marginalia** if the underlying trial grammar were pure fiction. Debunk explains Navy denial. It does not close the **artifact chain**: letters → annotated reprint → dead researcher → **1984 film** that swaps harbors for **Nazi time escape**.

The **Quester Files** and [Halperin](https://www.davidhalperin.net/the-philadelphia-experiment-the-allende-mystery/) preserve eyewitness-adjacent summaries; the investigation dossier holds the row-by-row audit. I cite them once here and do not reproduce the table — the point is structural. **Primary corpus** (letters, Varo) describes **space**; **secondary deposit** (1984, Montauk title) describes **time**. That split is the control pattern, not an accident of storytelling.

Philadelphia, in the project read, **could not** have been organic civilian science. It required **extreme classification** and **ordered reproduction**: controlled humans on Earth attempting to **rebuild** portal-class hardware **under command**, without Golden Age **operator literacy**.

The **negligence** is the tell. Bizarre injuries, mad crew, eighteen-day fire during hand-laying — that is not a lab accident; it is **incomplete mastery** of a medium that **stores polarization in living tissue** when the device shuts off. Golden Age transit assumed **pyramid-class shells** and geometry that matched the sky; [Stonehenge](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/stonehenge-quantum-portal-golden-age-and-dark-ages.md) names the bio-EM safety culture **1943 operators lacked**. They had a **mobile toroid** on a destroyer escort and no **off-switch** for human substrate. Success at routing; catastrophic failure at **decoherence in bodies**.

I do not read Philadelphia as folklore that grew in a vacuum. I read it as **prototype-class engineering** — prove the harbor pair, burn crew, learn that **touch** can temporarily rebalance stored medium stress until cascade failure ends the faith in hands.

The mechanism model in the dossier is explicit: **rotating shipboard B-field** beats against Earth's telluric field → **æther shear** → energy routes along a **pre-scarred least-resistance path** to the Norfolk node → hull and crew **couple** to the medium inside an **oblate spheroid** roughly a hundred yards across → device **OFF** → stored polarization **decays through bodies** as Flame, Freeze, and madness. **High amplitude, low frequency** through living tissue is what Golden Age transit assumed would be **safe** when geometry matched the sky. **1943** had amplitude without the shell. Success at **routing**; catastrophic failure at **decoherence in bio-EM storage**. That is the same class problem CERN would industrialize at larger scale — Part A's ring is the successor hardware, not a unrelated physics fantasy.

## The Navy Yard nexus

The legend is set at the **Philadelphia Naval Shipyard** — and that yard was a **documented military-scientific nexus** in the same war.

From **1942–1945**, **Isaac Asimov** worked as a civilian chemist at the **Naval Air Experimental Station**, West Philadelphia. **Robert A. Heinlein** and **L. Sprague de Camp** worked there too. Asimov drafted early **Foundation** material during this period — empire collapse and secret science written **beside** naval R&D. Heinlein would later encode military futures; de Camp would edit and shape the field.

**Proximity is not proof** that any of them witnessed **Project Rainbow** or **USS Eldridge**. It **is** proof that the yard where the legend places the trial was simultaneously home to **rotating-field degaussing**, radar countermeasures, and the writers who would teach the postwar public how **ships, fields, and impossible transits** feel in prose. Debunkers cite **degaussing** as the whole story; debunkers rarely mention that **the same institution** employed the men who built modern portal fiction's vocabulary.

When pop culture later retimes Philadelphia as **time travel**, remember: the **cover layer** (mine avoidance, signature reduction) and the **SF layer** (harbor jumps, field bottles) were **already neighbors** on the Delaware River.

Heinlein had served in the Navy before the war and returned to uniform-adjacent work at the yard; de Camp brought editorial discipline and pulp craft. Asimov's **Foundation** — drafted in stolen moments between chemistry tasks — imagines **psychohistory** and **collapsing empire** while literally standing inside the institution that debunkers cite as the **only** explanation for rotating fields. None of that proves Rainbow. It **does** name where **postwar portal fiction** would be written: beside **real ECM**, beside **real degaussing**, beside men who knew how **ships and fields** feel in technical prose. [Foundation index](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/asimov/foundation-index.md) records Navy Yard drafting dates; tier as **documented proximity**, never as eyewitness claim.

## When Hollywood retimed the portal

The deposit pattern is consistent: **space teleport plus fixed endpoint** is the operational layer; **time travel, Upside Down, or hell dimension** is the later shell.

**Lovecraft's *From Beyond* (1934)** predates the legend in print: a **resonance machine** reveals overlapping planes — literary rhyme for **field stress** opening what ordinary sight cannot hold. The same leak adds **timeloops** and a **"time-travelling missile"** atop the place jump — accurate routing vocabulary and explicit chronology deposit in one plot. I read that as **sequel PP** to the destroyer trial: prove the grammar on a **ship**, scale it under **Lake Huron** fiction, industrialize under **Geneva** in Part A.
