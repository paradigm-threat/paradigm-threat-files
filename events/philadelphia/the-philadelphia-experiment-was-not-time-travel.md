Men aboard the SS Andrew Furuseth in Norfolk watched a destroyer appear in the water — reportedly from Philadelphia — in minutes, not centuries. On 28 October 1943, per the richest secondary account chain, the Furuseth crew saw the event from Norfolk while the trial ran at the Navy Yard. Disappearance and reappearance were separate functions of the same device. When the coil shut off, survivors were fused into hull steel, phased invisible and back, frozen in comatose nets, or caught in fires that would not extinguish. Some men were lost during the transit itself, not only after shutdown. No one turned permanently invisible. No one time traveled.

## What the Navy denies

The Philadelphia Naval Shipyard ran serious electromagnetic research in WWII — degaussing, radar ECM, rotating fields against mines. The Navy denies any operational teleport of USS Eldridge (DE-173). Deck logs place Eldridge away from Philadelphia in October 1943. Debunkers cite Allende hoax confessions and misread degaussing as the whole story.

Degaussing is real adjacent tech — the cover that lets a stranger hear "rotating magnetic field" and stop asking. That layer explains mine avoidance; it does not close the file. ## Allende's letters — the detail-rich layer

The best single source is Carl Meredith Allen ("Carlos Miguel Allende") writing to Morris K. Jessup in 1955–1956 — preserved and analyzed by [David Halperin](https://www.davidhalperin.net/the-philadelphia-experiment-the-allende-mystery/) and others. Allende claimed service aboard the Furuseth and described the trial from the Norfolk witness side: Philadelphia to Norfolk in very few minutes, then return.

Inside an oblate spheroid field roughly a hundred yards across, crew looked vague in form — heat-mirage limbs, men walking on nothing inside the bubble. "Going Blank" could feel pleasant; "Getting Stuck" was "Hell Incorporated" — semi-comatose freeze in a subjective nether world, unaware of ordinary time passing. "The Flame" froze men and set them alight; one victim burned eighteen days while shipmates tried laying on of hands — carrying compasses when the fire would not stop — until "The faith in Hand Laying Died." Two or more crew had to touch a frozen man quickly; bystanders at the Navy Yard were told to join if they saw wavering digits. Men fused into the hull, walked through a quarters wall, and flickered in and out of visibility during the run. Allende's verdict: "The experiment Was a Complete Success. The Men were Complete Failures."

Space routing with catastrophic bio-EM storage — subjective freeze for stuck crew, not travel to another year.

## Jessup, Varo, and the artifact chain

The Philadelphia story surfaces a decade later through Morris K. Jessup, who wrote *The Case for the UFO* (1955). Allende's letters arrived as marginal commentary: Philadelphia, Einstein's unified field, an electromagnetic bottle, a destroyer that vanished and reappeared between harbors.

In 1957, the Office of Naval Research reportedly paid for a limited Varo edition reprint of Jessup's book because margin annotations from three anonymous debaters looked operationally literate — propulsion, field coupling, trial outcomes in engineer language, not carnival barkers. The Varo edition is a documented artifact whether or not the teleport happened.

Moore and Berlitz (1979) later named the operation Project Rainbow and packaged the letters for mass readership. The chain already existed: 1955–1956 correspondence, published UFO case, ONR-paid reprint with marginalia stranger than the main text. Jessup was found dead in 1959 — car run off the road, exhaust routed into the cabin. Officially suicide; the timing and the Varo episode keep the file open. [*Skeptical Inquirer*](https://skepticalinquirer.org/2021/08/solving-a-ufological-murder-the-case-of-morris-k-jessup/) treats much of the Allende correspondence as hoax-adjacent — fair on personality, weak on why ONR would fund annotated UFO marginalia if the underlying trial grammar were pure fiction.

The letters and Varo edition describe space between harbors. The 1984 film and Montauk title describe time. That split is the control pattern — see [investigation §5](https://paradigmthreat.net/events/philadelphia/investigations/philadelphia-experiment-portal-precursor-investigation.md).

## Ordered reproduction, not folklore

Philadelphia could not have been organic civilian science. It required extreme classification and ordered reproduction: controlled humans on Earth attempting to rebuild portal-class hardware under command, without Golden Age operator literacy.

Bizarre injuries, mad crew, eighteen-day fire during hand-laying — incomplete mastery of a medium that stores polarization in living tissue when the device shuts off. Golden Age transit assumed pyramid-class shells and geometry that matched the sky; [Stonehenge](https://paradigmthreat.net/history/chronology/stonehenge-quantum-portal-golden-age-and-dark-ages.md) names the bio-EM safety culture 1943 operators lacked. They had a mobile toroid on a destroyer escort and no off-switch for human substrate. High amplitude, low frequency through living tissue was safe when geometry matched; 1943 had amplitude without the shell. Success at routing; catastrophic failure at decoherence in bodies.

The mechanism: shipboard rotating B-field beats against Earth's telluric field, local æther shear, energy routes along a pre-scarred least-resistance path to the Norfolk node, hull and crew couple to the medium inside the oblate spheroid, device OFF, stored polarization decays through bodies as Flame, Freeze, and madness, crew touch attempts manual rebalancing until cascade failure. Same substrate grammar as [CERN investigation §1–§2](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/cern_portal/investigations/cern-mars-quantum-gateway-investigation.md) — smaller aperture, worse safety culture. Philadelphia proved the harbor pair and burned crew to learn hand-stabilization limits; [Part A's ring](https://paradigmthreat.net/science/cern_portal/out-of-this-door-might-come-something.md) industrializes the same class problem at larger scale.

## The Navy Yard nexus

From 1942–1945, Isaac Asimov worked as a civilian chemist at the Naval Air Experimental Station, West Philadelphia. Robert A. Heinlein and L. Sprague de Camp worked there too. Asimov drafted early Foundation material during this period — empire collapse and secret science written beside naval R&D. Heinlein had served in the Navy before the war and returned to uniform-adjacent work at the yard; de Camp brought editorial discipline and pulp craft.

None of them need to have witnessed Project Rainbow or USS Eldridge. The yard where the legend places the trial was simultaneously home to rotating-field degaussing, radar countermeasures, and the writers who would teach the postwar public how ships, fields, and impossible transits feel in prose. Asimov's Foundation — drafted in stolen moments between chemistry tasks — imagines psychohistory and collapsing empire while standing inside the institution debunkers cite as the only explanation for rotating fields. Cover layer and SF layer were already neighbors on the Delaware River. [Foundation index](https://paradigmthreat.net/influence/predictive_programming/fiction_encoding/asimov/foundation-index.md) records Navy Yard drafting dates — documented proximity, never eyewitness claim.

## Hollywood and the time-travel deposit

Pop culture retimed the same hardware as chronology fiction. The 1984 *Philadelphia Experiment* film added Nazi time escape while keeping only a weak ship-jump backdrop. *The Montauk Project* (1992) shouts "Experiments in Time" on the cover and builds a 1943↔1983 loop in the title while Nichols material still describes teleport and Mars tunnels. *Avengers: Endgame* (2019) routes a quantum-realm time heist through vocabulary borrowed from place-teleport sci-fi. *The Flash* (TV) turns a particle accelerator into timeline edits.

Lovecraft's *From Beyond* (1934) predates the legend: a resonance machine reveals overlapping planes — pre-1943 vocabulary for device-driven plane overlap that rhymes with Allende's electromagnetic bottle and ring-lab accident fiction.

Mobile coil, sudden harbor, crew trauma implied. The same leak adds timeloops and a "time-travelling missile" atop the place jump — accurate routing vocabulary and explicit chronology deposit in one plot. ## Why the harbor pair fits

Built on [**prisca sapientia**](https://paradigmthreat.net/docs/PRISCA_SAPIENTIA.md): Navy press releases are not final truth. Treating Philadelphia as time travel means ignoring the richest sources in favor of the 1984 deposit.

Allende names two harbors and minutes. He separates invisibility from teleport. He describes Flame, Freeze, and hand-laying with a specificity debunk does not dissolve into "sailors got dizzy." The Varo edition exists whether or not the destroyer jumped. Furuseth crew at Norfolk saw a destroyer appear — after a separate disappearance event in Philadelphia. Pop culture collapses invisibility and teleport into one vanish button; Allende does not. [Events hub](https://paradigmthreat.net/events/philadelphia/page.md) and secondary summaries preserve the October 28, 1943 date and fused-into-hull aftermath — bio-EM storage failure, not random sailor dizziness.

Time-travel branding explains mockery and film titles. Norfolk ↔ Philadelphia in minutes is the oldest coherent layer in the letter chain. Classification explains why Project Rainbow does not arrive in a tidy FOIA bundle. Navy denial plus degaussing cover the admitted electromagnetic work; they leave the Varo reprint, Jessup's 1959 end, and hand-laying fire specificity on the table.
