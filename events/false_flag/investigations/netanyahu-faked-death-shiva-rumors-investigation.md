## Feasibility: how realistic is the staging theory?

This section does not require “deepfake sci-fi” to be plausible. It relies on mundane state capacity: message discipline, access control, and a small number of aligned decision-makers — plus, at the hard rung, continuity tech in the same feasibility class as other elite media deceptions (9/11 plane-fakery thesis lives in linked 9/11 dossiers; not re-litigated here).

**What is easiest to stage**

- Short videos, stills, audio statements, or tightly framed “proof-of-life” snippets.
- Meetings with classified details and small aligned witness sets (Blair House roster).
- A health narrative released on a “stabilization day.”

**What is hardest to stage**

- Uncontrolled crowd contact, long continuous footage, independent hostile journalists in large numbers.
- A **joint session of Congress** / Capitol speech with opposition parties, cameras, and midterm political leverage.
- Leaks from medical staff, security layers, and foreign delegations over time.

**Where Aoun / Lebanon fit**

- Meeting **did not occur**. Prediction that any such meeting would be thin on third-party detail remains untested; refusal itself removes an independent foreign witness.

**Zelensky / Graham funeral epistemology**

- Real meeting compatible; public belief ≠ private sit-down audit.
- Funeral venue + possible Graham staged-death lane = **control signal**, not disproof.

**Dual-track photo-op / “will a meeting into existence” (Jul 2026)**

- Documented: Israeli press treated a WH visit as imminent; WH sources denied a scheduled meeting and framed Netanyahu as forcing optics ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/16/netanyahu-mystery-trump-meeting)); trip canceled mid-month; late-month salvage with **closed** format and **no** joint presser.
- **Author read:** a quarrel over guaranteed **photo-op** / standard summit imagery is a quarrel over the **proof-of-life product**. Curated closed-door imagery still feeds continuity without uncontrolled audit.

**Why the April 24 cancer disclosure is significant**

- Leader health managed and timed as narrative; soft non-death disappearance mechanism (reduced scrutiny, later “all clear” re-entry).
